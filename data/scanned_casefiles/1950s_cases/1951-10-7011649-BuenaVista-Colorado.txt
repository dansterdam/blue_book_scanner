On/or about 2000 - 2030 hours, 28 October 1951, I was at the base of a mountain approximately seven (7) miles South from Buena Vista, Colorado, on a hunting trip and was sitting in my car facing northeast. I was startled by the appearance of a very brilliant light, a greenish-blue. This object approached from the northwest going north to Southwest and in a descending curve. I would estimate the angle it was descending at approximately 20°.

The object stayed within my sight approximately 20 seconds - the brilliant light that is. I wouldn't attempt to estimate the speed. After the passage of the object, there appeared to be a tail or incandescent glow in the form of a tail. It was just a glow after the light was gone practically. It lit up the country quite a bit. The glow was just an incandescence in the sky. This glow lasted about 15-20 seconds and it changed color and seemed to change toward a white - the green blended in into sort of a milky color.

The only deduction I could draw from it, assuming an object had passed by, is that the speed at which it was going caused it to glow and particles from this object changed color as the temperature of the particles changed.

With a visit to the spot I could, I believe, very accurately determine the direction the light was traveling. The direction gave previously were an estimate as the roads were only tracks along the mountains and we ways in and around, and I am not positive of the exact direction I was facing when I saw the object.
