SUBJECT: Unidentified Flying Objects

1. Reference is made to AFR 200-2, dated 12 August 1954, subject as above. On 26 May 1955 this office was advised by [redacted] both of the 3457th STURDY, F E Warren AFB, Wyoming, that they had observed unidentified flying objects at approximately 2100 hours, 22 May 1955, approximately five miles south of Cheyenne, Wyoming, while their car was parked on US Highway #85.

2. At approximately 1600 hours, 25 May 1955, both airmen were interviewed and the following information was obtained:

a. Description of objects.

(1) Shape. Objects Nos. 1 and 2 - slender, rectangular, in vertical position. Objects Nos. 3 and 4 - oval, with protruding top.

(2) Size. Objects appeared slightly larger than baseball.

(3) Color. Objects Nos. 1 and 2 - white; Objects Nos. 3 and 4 - dark, with intermittent bluish-purple illumination.

(4) Number. Four (4).

(5) Formation. Side by side.

(6) Features. Nos. 1 and 2 - slender, vertical, rectangular. Nos. 3 and 4 - oval, with protruding top.

(7) Tail, trail of exhaust, including size. Negative.

(8) Sound. Negative.

341st Student Squadron
Francis E. Warren Air Force Base
Cheyenne, Wyoming

23 May 1955

STATEMENT

On the 22nd May 1955, three other airmen and myself were on the Denver

highway coming back to the base for bed check when our car went out of gas.

We pulled to a stop on a hill. About 10 miles from Cheyenne. We stopped a

car on the highway and we told two of the other airmen to go get gas while

Airman [redacted] and myself stayed with the car, between 2400 hours and 0100

hours. I looked out of the right front window and I saw a funny looking

object it was shaped something like a pencil with a white light all around it.

The object remained for about 10 seconds then disappeared. About one minute

later it was there again, then there was a flash of light and there were two

of them they started moving skyward and then they were gone. As soon as they

were out of sight suddenly in the spot they disappeared from there were two

cone shaped objects they had a sort of white color at first then changed to

a bluish purple color at first. Then as they moved skyward they kept getting

brighter and I could see them glittering then they were gone from sight.

Airmen Shapiro and myself just looked at each other, and I said I guess we

should report it, so at 0730 Monday morning 23 May 1955 we reported what

happened to M/Sgt Francis J. Martin. We were coming back from Gre ly on route

85 when this all happened.
