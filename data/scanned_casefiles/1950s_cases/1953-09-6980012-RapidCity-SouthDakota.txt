AIR INTELLIGENCE INFORMATION REPORT

FROM (Agency)                                                    REPORT NO.
740th AC&W Sqdn, EAFB, Rapid City,                             PAGE 2 OF 2 PAGES

On 15 September 1953, at approximately 1945 hours MST, Miss [redacted]
of Black Hawk, South Dakota was attending a 4-H meeting when she glanced into the
upper and west glow which seemed to be traveling approximately 150 to 200 mph.
The children at the meeting also saw the object but Miss [redacted] stated that their
ages range from nine to fifteen years and it would be almost impossible to get a
complete and accurate report of the object from them. The object was seen for
approximately thirty minutes and then disappeared behind a hill.

At the same time, Mr. & Mrs. [redacted] also of Black Hawk, noticed the
object and reported the same as Miss [redacted] to this organization.

At 2330 hours MST, Mr. & Mrs. [redacted] again saw the object which appeared
over the movie screen at a Drive In Theater. They stated that the object seemed
to be traveling closer to the screen and then suddenly turned to a brilliant white
such as an automobile headlight and then disappeared behind a hill to the north.

The sky at these times was clear of clouds and there were many stars. The
moon was in the west at the time and was giving off a very dull glow.

Mr. & Mrs. [redacted] stated that they watched the object for approximately
twenty minutes before it disappeared.

This object seemed to appear to stand still at times and also to flicker
such as a star would do. Mr. & Mrs. [redacted] also stated that the object turned
to a yellow glow but for a few seconds and then changed to the brilliant white.

Comments of Preparing Officer:

All three sightings reporting this object have been involved before in
flying object reports. It is the personal opinion of the undersigned that if
the objects matched the description's as reported, they would have been seen
and reported by many more people.
