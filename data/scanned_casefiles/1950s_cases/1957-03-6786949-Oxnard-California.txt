II. RELIABILITY: SOURCE, wife of a UNITED STATES AIR FORCE captain who was a Staff Judge Advocate, was very cooperative although concerned about being ridiculed because of reporting the sighting. SOURCE named gives average in intelligence. SOURCE answered all questions quickly and without hesitation. Some answers given were very general and not at all specific. SOURCE possessed considerable sense of humor regarding a judgment. SOURCE added to expressions of a personal or professional nature many contradictions, essentially tying in which certain events had taken place and the timing thereof. SOURCE had made a sketch of the object during the sighting trip had misplaced it so that it was not available during the interview. SOURCE seemed excitable and possibly a little "high". On nine of significant her young daughter had been slightly ill, and SOURCE was possibly a little upset as her husband was away on TDY. SOURCE talked at using certain colors when describing the object and her interview. SOURCE was alert and had good powers of concentration. In view of the foregoing the information given below may be considered possibly reliable.

III. SOURCE'S DESCRIPTION OF SIGHTING AND EVENTS:

1. At approximately 23/0182 MAR 57 SOURCE arose from bed to answer the telephone. While on the phone SOURCE observed an unusual green object in the northeast sky about ten (10) degrees above the horizon. The green object glowed fluorescently and was brighter than the surrounding stars.

2. SOURCE described the object as being round, but changing shape occasionally (see incl #2) to that resembling a helicopter. Object was estimated to be about 20 and one-half (20½) feet in diameter. SOURCE stated that at one time it appeared to be about the size of a quarter held at arm's length and at another time stated it appeared about the size of a dime held at arm's length. Object seemed to throb and pulsate. There was no sound. Object was sharply defined. Object alternately had and did not have a tail. A shiny aluminum-like pole appeared at times on top of the object. Object was seen to move in a semi-smooth and also to remain static. Sometime it jumped around. The object gave off no smoke and had a green or orange luminescence.

3. SOURCE described the glow from the object as projecting downward only. SOURCE stated that while initially the glow definitely did not shine upward and compared the impression of the glow to that created by blackout paint.

4. At approximately 23/0732 MAR 57 SOURCE telephoned the Officer of the Day, a personal friend, at OXNARD AFB and reported the sighting.
