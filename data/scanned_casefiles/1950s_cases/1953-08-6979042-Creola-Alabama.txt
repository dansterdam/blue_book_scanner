UNCLASSIFIED

STATEMENT OF
CRESOLA, ALABAMA

On 1 August 1953 at 20:20 PM local time, I was just gettin into my vehicle when I saw the object in the sky. It was on the West side of Hiway 43 heading in a SW direction. I could not make out any shape to it and the only thing I noticed about it was the variation in the color of the lights. It appeared to be glowing a reddish color in the leading part of the object and a dull amber glow in the rear part. It was traveling at a very high rate of speed and the duration of the sighting could not have lasted two seconds before it disappeared to the SW. I could not approximate the altitude although what I did see of it seemed fairly large in size. There was no sound associated with the light and I all not see or hear any aircraft in the area on the time of the sighting. It appeared to be going in a straight and level flight.

CRESOLA, ALABAMA
