13. REMARKS
Green ball-shaped object traveled at a high rate of speed in a slightly downward course, when took a further downward dip and disappeared. Elevation of object when first sighted was 15° above the horizon. Estimated speed of object was faster than a jet a/c and slower than a meteor. Reliability of source is unknown

STATEMENT

TIME: 1815 hours, 21 Dec 1952
LOCATION: Fraser-Tilden Highway approximately one mile South of the Seven Sisters Road intersection San Antonio, Texas
DIRECTION: Object was traveling in a South-Eastern direction

I was driving my car on a heading of approximately 0 degrees. Suddenly, a pale green light appeared in the sky at about one o'clock. It had much the appearance of a "ball from somewhere in the sky at an elevation of about 15 degrees above the horizon and traveled very rapidly to a two o'clock position on a slightly downward course. The brightness of the object seemed noticeably slower than a meteor but faster than any jet aircraft which I have observed.

The intensity of the object's light seemed to vary. For after an interval which I would estimate to be one to one and a half seconds, as the object was roving in the downward course, its took a further downward trend, smoothly, losing velocity and light, and disappeared from view. The object appeared to be about 10 degrees above the horizon when it disappeared.

At the time the above took place I was alone, however, one car was stopped on a South heading about 250 yards South of the Seven Sisters intersection, and another car's headlights going East on the Seven Sisters Highway was observed. It is possible that occupants of these cars observed this phenomena.

I certify that the above statement is true and correct to the best of my knowledge:

30. Please add here any further comments which you believe are important.

My first impression was that it was a taillight of an airplane. Since
it was in the plane of an airplane taking off toward me I discarded the
taillight idea. Stopped my car while still keeping the objects in sight.
Got out of the car and heard no sound of any kind. Suddenly the object
whipped straight upward then downward with its switch showing its bottom side which was a
deep orange red. It shot upward in pulses from right to left at about
45 degrees, then vertical. Then it accelerated past due south. Suddenly
it streaked across the sky toward the south at about an angle of 50 degrees
with its white side toward me the major portion of the time. Three times
it rolled through a plane which showed its reddish under side. A point
about midway through this roll showed no light. It finally took up a
position to the south of the planet Jupiter and stopped at an altitude which
I estimate at 100 miles. All this happened in 5 minutes. At this position
it would dart forward each time in an alternate direction, Left or Right.
I watched until 7:45 P.M. or a total of 15 minutes later.

Note: I walked across the highway to a gas station and called the
attendant's attention to the object. This was 5 minutes after the object
reached its zenith and stopped. He confirmed its movement. Do not know
attendant's name or type of gas sold in his station.

TIME: 1:15 hours, 21 Dec 1952
LOCATION: Three-Ridden Highway approximately one mile south of the Seven Sisters Road intersection near Arlington, Texas
DIRECTION: Object was traveling in a southeasterly direction

I was driving my car on a heading of approximately 0 degrees, suddenly a very bright light appeared in the sky at about 2 o'clock. It had much the same appearance as a "ball from a roman candle". It was at an elevation of about 15 degrees above the horizon and traveled very rapidly to a two o'clock position on a slightly downward curve. The velocity of the object seemed considerable slowing when faster than any jet airier it which I have observed.

The intensity of the object's light seemed to vary, for after an interval which I would estimate to be one to one and a half seconds, as the object was moving in the command source, it took a sharper command trend, seeming locating velocity and lights, and then curved again. The object appeared to be about 15 degrees above the horizon when it disappeared.

At the time the above took place I was alone, however, one car was stopped on a South heading about 250 yards South of the Seven Sisters intersection, and another cars headlights using East on the Seven Sisters Highway was observed. It is possible that occupants of these cars observed this phenomenon.
