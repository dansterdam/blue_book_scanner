Richmond, Virginia                                23 February 1953

I. Description of Incident

At 1200 EST an Air Force colonel while traveling at 60 mph in his auto near Richmond observed a flash of metal with a long narrow rectangular contrail trailing from south to north. The object crossed the path of the auto from left to right traveling at less than jet speed. The object was observed approximately 15 minutes.

II. Discussion of Incident

Observer visited a friend of his about 1 hour later. The friend initiated the conversation by stating that he had seen a bright contrail in the sky about 1200 at considerable distance. At 1200 EST on this date the sun was setting. The sun, reflecting from contrail, made it easily visible.

III. Conclusion

Probably contrail of jet aircraft.

1. This report pertains to the observation of unusual aerial objects by four (4) persons at the Richmond Quartermaster Depot, Richmond, Va. The time of sighting was approximately 0615 hours, 10 Feb 53.

2. On 10 Feb 53, S/A Walter P. Jacobs, Richmond Regional Intelligence Office, 1009th CIC Detachment, Richmond, Va., telephonically advised the Richmond Detachment, 21st District CSI, that four (4) civilian guards at the Richmond Quartermaster Depot at approximately 0800 hours, 10 February 1953.

3. On 11 February 1953, Charles L. These objects passed directly over Camp's head, proceeding southwest at a "slow speed". They hovered over a 75 feet water tower located at the southeast end of the Depot. Camp described then as "moving like puppets in a string". Shortly thereafter the objects disappeared over the horizon in the vicinity of Warehouse #, which is located approximately 150 feet south of the water tower. 5. On 11 Feb 53 Harry L. Derr, Civilian Guard, Richmond

five hundred(500) feet south of the main gate. At this point, the

spent considerable time in the vicinity of airports and that he is certain
that these lights were not navigation lights, landing lights or any other
type of light that he has observed on conventional aircraft. Derr stated
that the objects or lights were circular in shape, yellowish in color and
that the objects did not emit a beam of light. Derr stated that from
his vantage point, the lights appeared approximately 18 inches in diameter.

witnesses. All of the witnesses appeared to be reliable, intelligent,
sober individuals who were well acquainted with conventional aircraft.
All of the witnesses stated that they have never observed any
similar objects and could offer no logical explanation for the
sighting.
