CONTACTS IN BRAZIL (continued from Bulletin No. 6)

So far four people have claimed to have contacted entities from other worlds and all the four are state of Sao Paulo residents. Three of them live right here and one other lives in near-by Santos. We have subdivided the stories of P.A. or Fidelis, or Dino Kraspedon, the case of Marcilio Godoi was covered by V.I.P.A.O. He covered and the flying Saucer; now we have two cases left. We will call them the "Rossi case" and "the Guimaraes case". Like the two former cases mentioned, there is no reason whatever to even slightly suppose that the contacts are authentic. They are only hypothetical in the reasons for telling lies. We believe the liars themselves would find it extremely difficult to tell themselves why they have lied. Perhaps one or two of them "know" that they lied, but at least we believe that they have not lied: Mr. Guimaraes and Mrs. Rossi. If we were allowed to suggest we reasons why these contact stories were concocted we put it this way:

Fidelis (P.A.) - partly out of vanity, partly to make money. He want people to know how wise he is, how sharp is his sense to ask unanswerable questions and also to offer a theory for the saucer's propulsion. He is terribly in need of money and think he can live as a writer.

Godoi: does not want to make money; just likes to have a crowd around him to listen to his wild yarns.

Rossi: Wants to make money, but believes he went to Venua. Hallucination.

Guimaraes: Hallucination. Does not want to write a book. Preaches love among all human beings. Wants atomic explosions to stop. Mystic.

Rossi and Fidelis have written books. Rossi has pityily wait that he wants to make money. Nice honest fellow. Fidelis, a shark. Is going to write some more books about saucers. At the bottom he is amusing himself hugely.

Since Rossi has written a book, we will deal with. Let us take the recent case of Professor Joao Freitas Guimaraes, who got the headlines not only in Brazil but also in Europe. We are herein conveying first hand information. We here to expose Professor Guimaraes as another hoaxer, because he has always been a very respectable citizen. He teaches Roman Code at the law School of Santos; he is president of the Lawyers Association; he is a Chairman for several high institutions and belongs to a traditional family in Sao Paulo. Nonetheless his story is not true. The maximum we allow him is that he has had an hallucination and maybe take he has been concocted as "a good cause".

About the Guimaraes case we have written extensively to Lex D. Mebane, Vice-president of Civilian Saucer Investigation of New York, 245 West 104th Street, New York 25, N.Y. Also on August 23 we mailed a letter to Maj. Donald E. Keyhoe, Director of NICAP about the same subject. Since this last letter contains the story bridged, we reprint it partly.

"On August 13 1957 practically all newspapers in Sao Paulo published the story of a new contact. The thing seemed to be big news because a protagonist was Dr. Joao Freitas Guimaraes a respectable professor at the Santos law School. I know that five days before the news got newspapers attention the Escobar Pa-ria had been in Santos trying to interview Mr. Guimaraes, but this latter turned his back on Escobar, saying, over the telephone, that the case was being investi gated by Aeronautical authorities, to whom Escobar should as his questions. Allegedly on June 15 or 16 (about 14 months ago) Mr. Guimaraes was calmly watching the waves from a sitting position from a Sao Sebastiao beach in the state of Sao Paulo coast. It was about 19:00 o'clock local time (22:00 GMT) dark, cold clear night; Mr. Guimaraes attention was suddenly called to something bulky poised right were the waves touched the sandy beach. Although the thing was sur-rounded by a phosphor glow, Guimaraes's first thought was that a whale had gotten itself stranded. A whale is not a common sight around Sao Sebastiao but it is not too rare either. Besides the bulky thing was spouting some more into the air. Soon however, Guimara as realized that he had before him a 20 meters-wide con-trapition whose general shape was like the saucers usually seen in magazines. The thing was about 6 or 7 meters high. Besides the red glow around the periphery, there was another glow on the top of a dome. Guimaraes said he saw distinctly two - repeat - two balls under the belly of the disc, a though they were landing gear balls. Amazed he got up exactly when a hatch from the saucer lower part opened and a flight of steps was lowered toward the ground. Two men came down the steps and walked straight to the place where Guimaraes was. Sort of startled Guimaraes spoke to them in rortuguese, in English, in French and Ita-lian, asking to know whether they were in trouble and needed help. The men from the saucer never opened their mouths to answer him. When Guimaraes became aware that he was mentally telepathically being invited to go inside the disco and even if he wished to take a little trip aboard it. Guimaraes doubted his senses, but again and insistently the same forceful impression came to his mind. He then decided to enter the craft. He walked between the two men. When they were well inside the ship, the hatch was closed and Guimaraes sat on a curved sofa, facing the portside. Soon he heard a humming sound which increased in pitch until the frequency was too high for him to notice it. He was taking off.

The trip lasted for 40 minutes and the craft landed about 60 meters from the starting point on the Sao Sebastiao beach. During the trip, a few minutes after take-off, the saucer started vibrating and Guimaraes rather afraid asked one of the crew-members whether they were having some trouble. The mental answer he received was: "We are leaving your planet's atmosphere; we are then shifting to a magnetic means of propulsion."

Through the port-holes Guimaraes could see that they had passed a very dark zone, "louay" with stars and flaming clouds and were now approaching a zone of very intense violet light. He could see the earth as a blue sphere about 1/4 of the size of the full moon. According to the newspapers Guimaraes reckoned he had been just cut of the earth atmosphere. 500 to 600 kilometers high. Then the saucer again passed the dark zone and soon landed. Joao Freitas Guimaraes rushed out of the saucer without looking back. Upon arriving at the hotel where he was staying for the night, he noticed that it was 20:25 o'clock (23.25 GMT) and he then corrected his anti-magnetic wrist watch that had stopped working soon after he entered the saucer.

Answering reporters questions Guimaraes said that there were five people manning the saucer, but he had only seen three of them. During the whole trip he was accompanied by a standing crew-member. All but were tall, hairless, blonde and their hair reached to their shoulders. They seemed to be of a nordic type. They never spoke among themselves. Their eyes were blue and full of kindness. They dressed a sort of ski-suits, green in color; their shoes seemed to be made of green rubber. It would be difficult to say whether they were men or women. They refused to tell their origin. Case 166, Yonkers, N.Y. June 29, 30, July 3, 1956—The Herald-Statesman of Yonkers, July 3 said: "Skywatchers report thrilling post-midnight sight. . . . It is of orange color, more nearly red. To one with a 30-power telescope, it appears round, then oval. It was moving at what seemed to be considerable speed. One report is that the phenomenon was noted after 1 a.m. on Saturday. Another report tells of the orange-in-the-sky around the same hour or a little later just ahead of the rainstorm. . . ." While the remainder of the newspaper's item gibbered in ridicule, a letter received from [redacted] of Yonkers, describes his observations in more detail. Said Wiegers:

". . . I phoned the Herald-Statesman and spoke to Oxie Reichler, editor, regarding my sighting on June 29-30. He asked, 'Are you talking about the orange colored thing seen in the sky over Yonkers?' I told that I didn't know anyone else saw anything and not to use my name if he were to write a story. Replied Reichler, 'We got a report that a red ball was seen before 2 a.m. only this morning (July 3). There were others, too. . . .' Then described his sighting of June 29 as follows: '2:45 p.m., clear evening, no wind. My wife pointed to a round, reddish-orange light to the left of the moon and slightly above it. Through a 30-power telescope it appeared well defined in outline to the naked eye and emitted no sound. A moment before I had been observing Saturn with my 60×90 mm. Bausch & Lomb Halscope and turned when my wife said, 'Look at that peculiar light.' Quickly swinging the scope around, I first looked with naked eye and saw it standing still and silent. Then sighting it in the scope, it appeared orange of a flashing brilliance. Suddenly, it began to move north and I soon found it extremely hard to keep in vision due to its accelerating speed. I tracked it for about 11 seconds, then lost it from view due to a building in the way.'

"On June 30, I was alone on the back porch about 10:30 p.m. and saw another object coming from the south. It was going directly overhead. My scope was not properly focused for this object but I got it in view and observed it for some time. It went by very rapidly and silently. I confess I was unprepared, and cannot say what it was—but I am sure the previous night's UFO was the same type reported by [redacted]"
