DATE 3JULY 54

Came on duty in Control Tower at 1615 hrs. Pacific; Was advised of object at 1840 hrs.
Began to take pictures at 1900 hrs of shortly after. Object appeared almost over the stat
at first and moved from NE to SW while surface winds were from the SE at about 5 miles per
hour. Gieser Tower was asked concerning object and advised they had seen it all day
afternoon. Spokane Radio advised thier first report was by a civilian pilot about 1405hrs
and object was over Chelan, Wash. at that time.
Gieser Tower had two jet fighters report on the object. First jet at 43,000 ft.
and second jet a F-86D at nigser altitude and both aircraft advised the object to be
at least 60,000 feet and appeared like an enormous balloon.
Gieser CAA weather station reported the first favorable wind moving in the direction
of the object to be at 60,000 ft.
Object was observed through 7 x 50 binnoculars and appeared in shape of a teardrop,
and lccked of a translucent color as if we could see through it, and appeared to have
an object attached to it similar to a weather balloon.
The sky conditions at the time was very clear with no clouds at all and the visibiliy
was unlimited.
Also several of the photographs were taken with a pair of 7 x 50 binoculars affixed
to the front of the camera.
