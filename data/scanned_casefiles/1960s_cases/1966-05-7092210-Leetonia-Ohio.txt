One object seen for the last three weeks between 2130 and 2300 hours (local) always heading west. High altitude but not as high as the stars. Sometimes appears to be going slightly northeast and sometimes slightly southwest. Only observed on clear nights. The reddish amber light does not blink on and off so witness ruled out airplane. Info in letter was quite sketchy, therefore, 164 was sent.
