I understand any sighting of unidentified flying objects are to be reported to the U.S. Air Force. If this is not handled by your office, will you please forward the following to the proper department.

On Tuesday evening, April 5, 1966, between 9:10 and 9:15 P.M. E.S.T., I observed an object of unusual conditions. While traveling north on the Schuylkill Expressway, at a point about one quarter mile north of the Girard Avenue Bridge and slightly west of the expressway, there was an object in the sky. Throughout the observed period, approximately 1 minute, I observed no movement to any degree of the object. It appeared absolutely stationary. Its height was about 500 feet above the ground. I noted three distinct red lights on its apparent oval or football shaped outer section. Extending from the nucleus were two wing like extensions. At the end of each wing was a very intense light that periodically went on. About every four seconds they flashed on for approximately a half second duration, then went out for about 4 seconds again. The wing span was about that of a Piper Cub but only about 5 feet wide. The hub or center section appeared to be about 15 feet by 10 feet in an oval shape. Outside of the 5 lights there was no glow of the object from the bottom view.
