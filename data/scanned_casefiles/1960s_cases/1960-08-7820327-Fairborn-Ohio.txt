10. BRIEF SUMMARY OF SIGHTING
Two starlike dots with larger object which seemed to trail the dots, color bluish-white to faded yellow. Object appeared as a light with rays. It appeared to stand still, explode, give off smoke, change brightness. The elevation increased 5-10° as it proceeded NW. Object still visible after 40 min after initial sighting.

11. COMMENTS
It is possible that the objects were the star Alpha Aquila (Altair) mag 0.9 and two smaller stars.
