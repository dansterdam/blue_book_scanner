15 Jul 65
Canberra, Australia

UAO Over Canberra Airport

On the 15th of July press wires carried the details of the sighting of a mysterious glowing object which hovered near Canberra airport while the U. S. Mariner space probe was taking pictures of Mars. The initial story said that the object "has baffled experts in Canberra". The object was spotted by air traffic control officers and other expert aircraft observers on the 15th, at 10:50 a. m.

Six members of the traffic control tower told the press that the object "hung suspended" at about 5,000 feet for 40 minutes and that when the sun glinted on it, it became perfectly visible. It disappeared when an Air Force plane was sent out to identify it.

In the initial press story, experts were quoted as "wondering if it was coincidence" that the object was sighted shortly before nearby Tidinbilla tracking station was scheduled to pick up Mariner signals. The station, the report said, had unusual difficulty in locking on to Mariner at the time.
