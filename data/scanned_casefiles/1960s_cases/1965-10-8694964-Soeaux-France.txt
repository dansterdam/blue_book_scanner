It was the 13th of October 1955. I was at Fontenay aux Roses, South of Paris. I casually looked up to the sky and my attention was drawn to an extremely bright star almost directly above Paris, somewhat west of it. (It is, of course, difficult to evaluate the perpendicular of a thing that is at the high altitude.) This seemed abnormal to me. It was still earths, even though the sun was setting. The thought of Venus crossed my mind. I gave the matter my attention and my observation was distracted perhaps for two or three seconds. I looked up again to study this mystery. The star had disappeared. It was 18:21 o'clock. At the same spot, I was able to observe a long trail of white vapor thousands of meters in depth and almost perpendicular northwards. Within this congagation I observed numerous things, one of which appeared to be round [sketch attached] it had of a reddish color. The observation could have lasted no more than three minutes. Once the cloud of vapor had dispersed, nothing more was visible. I only regret very much that I missed the moment of the disintegration. I believe it was a stage of the USAF [a USAF missile]. This seems to be confirmed by the fact that approximately 1 hour 3 minutes later, that is, approximately 19:23 hrs, when I looked up again a satellite passed from west to east, as it is often seen, and gradually perpendicular to the preceding thing.

Unless I was under an illusion, could you tell me at what altitude this disintegration took place, that is, the encounter of the object with the upper atmosphere?
