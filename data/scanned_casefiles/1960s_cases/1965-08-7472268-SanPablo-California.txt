1. PURPOSE: This report answers W. O. 65-105, from Maj. Quintanilla, Jr. (TDEW/UFO), requesting Photo Analysis of one roll of 8 MM movie film taken on 7 August 1965. Analysis was conducted by the Photo Analysis Division, TDDPA, and the Photo Processing Division, TDDPR.

2. ANALYSIS: Approximately one-third of the 8 MM film that dealt with the possible UFO was taken from a moving vehicle. In this section of film the only image which might be that the image was part of a larger image showing the top half of a man in a shirt coat, with the smaller light toned image being part of the shirt and collar. At one point, the faint image of a second person can also be seen. The image of a man appears more clearly in the last section of film showing the sky.

3. CONCLUSION: Although a double exposure can only be seen on the section of film showing the sky, it is felt that a major portion of the 8 MM film has been double exposed. This phenomenon of the second set of images is felt to be a double exposure rather than a reflection because of the lack of distortion to the image and the appearance of the image not being reversed. A careful examination of the 8 MM film failed to reveal any discernible image that could be classed as a UFO.
