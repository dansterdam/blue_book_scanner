11. BRIEF SUMMARY AND ANALYSIS
Observer reported viewing cigar shaped object that had three diamond shaped windows. A heard a high humming and hissing sound during the observation. The object was hovering when it was first observed, then zig-zagged, then shot away in a straight path to the east.

Contact was made with the witness while Project Blue Book investigator was in Suffolk County AFB. Investigation of the witness revealed him to be unreliable. He appeared to be out to get his name in the various news media around the area. The local news media that Suffolk County AFB had told him that jets had been scrambled to intercept UFOs. When asked of this, he finally said that he was told by a friend. The witness has seen several UFOs and reported such to the AirForce.
