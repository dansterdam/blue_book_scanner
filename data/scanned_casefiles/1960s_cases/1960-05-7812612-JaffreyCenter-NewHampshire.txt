10. BRIEF SUMMARY OF SIGHTING:
Very bright and deep gold, about 3 times as large as a planet, size similar to the sun seen high through thick clouds, clear-cut circle or disc. Motion was plainly visible as it crossed a window aprox 5 ft x 3 ft high diagonally from top to bottom. Appeared E of S, high in sky. Disappeared W of S, low in sky, seemed to be descending.

11. COMMENTS:
It is believed that the object of this sighting was probably a reflection, however there is not sufficient data to support this claim. If the object had been a balloon it probably would not have crossed the area covered by the window so quickly and an a/c would probably have crossed much faster. This case contains insufficient data to reach a conclusion.
