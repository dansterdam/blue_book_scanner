11. BRIEF SUMMARY AND ANALYSIS
Object appeared as a bright disc, larger than an aircraft. Made approach to runway, hovered for approximately 1 1/2 minutes, then it disappeared into the southeast. Looked like a welding light. Like a star only much brighter. Apparent motion due to motion of auto. After form 164 was sent back evaluated as planet Venus.
