10. BRIEF SUMMARY OF SIGHTING
Pink wheels observed by one witness moving very fast from E to W. At least four evenly spaced about 1/2 minute apart. All alike in all respects. Each object in view only about one second. Circular and huge. No trail, tail, sound. Objects appeared to be spinning or rolling. Spacing was even. Observed thru window. Watched about 25 minutes after the last one appeared and no more appeared.
