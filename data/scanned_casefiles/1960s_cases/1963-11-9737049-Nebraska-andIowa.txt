MEMO: 5 Nov 63

At 0353, local time, 5 Nov 63, Sgt Brown from AF Command Post at Pentagon called this office - TDEW.

Five reported sightings of 5 Nov 63, duration 10-15 seconds, direction of travel North to South

1. United Airlines, flight 742, traveling Northwest of Omaha sighted object at 1104Z, light dim to bright alternately, 60 deg above horizon tracked to 30 deg, about 100 deg arc, plane was at 37,000th altitude.

2. American Airlines, flight 900, Northeast near Sioux City, Iowa, light dim to bright.

3. TWA, flight 58

4 & 5 military flights

M TSgt David Moody called SPADATS, there was no decay for this date.
