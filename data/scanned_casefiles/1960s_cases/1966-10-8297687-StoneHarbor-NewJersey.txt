The Details of our investigation

Date - October 1, 1966

Time - 7:15 PM to about 7:30 PM, about dusk.

Direction travelling - Southwest then Northwest then South toward us.

Angle to Horizon - about a 45° angle.

Approximate Height - about 200 ft.

Color - It was a pale pink in the distance and as it flew over us it had 3 pale amber lights. The lights did not blink.

Sound - There was no sound.

Size - It was about 200 ft in diameter and about the size of a jet high in the sky.

Brightness - In the distance it was the size of Sirius and amber lights were about the size of medium sized star.

Weather Conditions - it was a cool clear night with a few large clouds.

Location - 93rd St. on the beach and 96th St on the beach in Stone Harbor, New Jersey.

Duration - it all lasted about 15 minutes then it disappeared.
