UFO INCIDENT
6 March 1963

1756Z and 1846Z

Summary: (1) Silver object observed from F-101's at 50,000 feet.
Object above at 80 degree elevation 10 degrees off port. Sighting between Ventura and Santa Barbara. In sight 5-10 minutes.
F-101's made turn and lost sight of object. Estimated object to
be at 100,000 feet going 500 knots. (2) Bright light resembling
star, white with out feature, no contrail or exhaust. High against
clear blue sky. Object 30 degrees above horizon. Disappeared
instantaneously to East like lightbulb going out. Reported from
Santa Barbara, Vandenburg, Oxnard area. All sightings air visual.

traveling at 500 knots at 100,000 feet. F-101's at 45,000 feet.
