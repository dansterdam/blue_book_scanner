10. CONCLUSION: Satellite(Pageos A)

4. NUMBER OF OBJECTS: One

11. BRIEF SUMMARY AND ANALYSIS

6. TYPE OF OBSERVATION: Ground Visual Ex

Observer watched a star like object travel slowly toward the south. The object was just like a star or similar to a satellite. The color was duller than the average star, being brown or orange in color.

7. COURSE: Southward

Pageos A was over the eastern US states at 0357Z traveling toward the south. At the height that the satellite travels it could be visible for up to approximately 5000 miles. The description is consistent with that of a satellite observation.
