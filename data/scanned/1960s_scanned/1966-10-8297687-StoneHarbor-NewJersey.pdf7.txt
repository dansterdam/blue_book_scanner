AUG 18 1967

Dear [redacted]:

This will reply to your recent correspondence regarding your October 1, 1966 observation of an unidentified flying object (UFO).

We regret that you were unable to return our form and investigation report. Unfortunately we are unable to undertake a complete investigation due to the lapse of time. Air activity records are normally destroyed after thirty days; therefore, it would be impossible to check the records on the date of your observation.

Although we are unable to identify positively the stimulus, your information will be added to the present data base.

Thank you for reporting your observation to the Air Force.

Sincerely,
