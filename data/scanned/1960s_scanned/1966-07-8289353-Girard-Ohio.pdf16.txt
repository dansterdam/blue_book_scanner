No haze or clouds, none any where. There were countless stars in the
sky.

Looking directly to the east when I noticed light first appear. Just
at edge of visition when I first noticed it. Frequently saw aircraft. Think
the air strike was in effect. Airport 5 miles north of us. See jets pass, planes
going east to west, the Air Force planes going east and west, going all night
long. Can hear the jets even when they are only specks. I didn't observe any
protrusions, tail fins, stubby wings, etc. Perhaps this was due to the
whiteness. It wasn't a dazzling white but more like a white in a candle.
Didn't blind your eyes. Wasn't as white as a star. More like the white of
a candle. Similar to a flourescent light. Little brighter than a candle.
