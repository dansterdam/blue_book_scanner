Sheboygan, Wis.

May 4, 1966

appeared in amazement. On saw two bright, white lights, the space between which seemed to be like that of a large airplane. Alone with white light were glowing flames - one red in color, the other green which, by the silhouette, seemed to be contained within a small shape base. I was not driving, so I concentrated on it, adjusting my eyes to try to distinguish any features. The object seemed to be just large enough to clear the tree tops and houses; yet all I could make out was an outline by the glow or flare base - whatever it was that formed a shallow V shape.
