Source: Saucer News, Vol 13, #1, Mar 66

Buenos Aires
15 July 1965

The full account of these above-mentioned incidents occupies a full page in each of four consecutive editions of the Buenos Aires newspaper "Cronica," (9/24/65-9/27/65), and space permits us to give here only the first of the incredible series of events. On the night of July 15th, while driving home from the Hotel De Ensenada, the Busquets family made a sighting described as follows: "It was a very large object, very luminous, bluish in color; but the color varied at times to grayish-red. It was quite near us. It had the shape of a short cone, although we could not see clearly where its upper part ended, as its surface was engulfed in a haze which was being emitted from on top. The bottom part was circular and convex. Taking the nearby hills as a reference, we estimated that it was 10 to 15 meters (yards) in diameter. In a moment we two were engulfed in a luminous haze. The object remained stationary, and no noise was heard....After a long moment, it descended below a nearby hill, and the ground hid it, but we continued seeing the light it produced. Finally the light went out completely. We then continued our journey to the ranch, where we found that the laborers had seen it also."
