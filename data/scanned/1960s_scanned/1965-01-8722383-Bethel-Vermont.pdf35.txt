Source: Saucei News, Vol 1?, #1, March 1955

Burlington, Vermont
3 January 1955

On the night of January 3rd, Dr. Richard S. Woodruff, a highly respected physician, was driving with a Vermont State policeman, near Burlington, Vermont, when the two men saw three reddish UFOs pass across the highway at treetop level. The objects were moving at such high speed that the observers could not give a detailed description....
