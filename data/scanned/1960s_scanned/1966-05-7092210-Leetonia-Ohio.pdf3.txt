Dear Major Welton Quartermaster:

I have just received additional information in the paper, a letter signed "Flying Saucers --- God Bless You Rod."

Shell Demould, like to tell you about seeing a "UFO." He says upon viewing the object for the last three weeks now between 9-3 ---11 PM I smell hay and describe it as best as I can. It's bright reddish amber about mama from the last towards the west, moves faster at sometimes then other times, kind of flickers at times. It does not always follow the same, lately sometimes it is about 100 yards up to the north and sometimes about 100 yds to the south left always heading west.

I am not stating it is a flying saucer but would like to find what you see in' clear nights.        (Send 164)
