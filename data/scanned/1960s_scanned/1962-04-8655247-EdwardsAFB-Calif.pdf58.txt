SUBJECTS

SEATTLE (AP)--TEST PILOT JOE WALKER SAYS FILM TAKEN DURING HIS RECENT FLIGHT IN THE ROCKET-POWERED X-15 EXPERIMENTAL PLANE ADDS A RECORD HEIGHT
OF 215,000 FEET ABOVE THE EARTH TO HIS CREDIT. IT WAS THE THIRD REPORT OF UNEXPLAINED OBJECTS AT HIGH ALTITUDES.

WALKER SIGHTED BOTH A DOZEN OR MORE REDDISH OBJECTS AND A GREENISH GLOWING OBJECT DURING ORBITAL FLIGHTS AROUND THE EARTH. WALKER SAID FOR THE NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION, HE DISCLOSED AT THE NATIONAL CONFERENCE ON UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECTS TUESDAY.

WALKER, AIDED BY AN ENGINEER WHO ANALYZED MOTION PICTURES, DISCUSSED EXPERIMENTAL FLIGHTS. GLENN ALSO WAS ON THE PANEL.

"I'VE NEVER BEEN ABLE TO EXPLAIN THESE THINGS," WALKER COMMENTED ON THE FIRST SIGHTING OF THESE OBJECTS. HE SAID THE OBJECTS APPEARED AS THE PLANE MOVED OVER THE TOP OF ITS FLIGHT AND HEADED FOR EARTH.

"DON'T FEEL LIKE I CAN RULE OUT THE POSSIBILITY OF THESE OBJECTS," WALKER SAID. "ALL I KNOW IS WHAT APPEARED ON THE FILM IN LATER STUDY."

ASTRONAUT JOHN GLENN, IN SEPARATE SESSION, CONFIRMED HE HAD BEEN UNABLE TO ESTIMATE THE RANGE OF PERHAPS SEVEN TO ELEVEN MILES OF OBJECTS DURING HIS THREE ORBITS. HE WAS QUESTIONED ABOUT REPORTS THAT THEY MIGHT HAVE BEEN CONDENSED CRYSTALS OF SNOW KNOCKED OUT BY HIS CAPSULE -- SNOWFLAKES. "I DON'T FEEL LIKE TYPE SNOWFLAKES, OR PAINT PEELING OFF THE OUTSIDE," HE SAID. "THEY WERE NOTHING LIKE THAT. THEY SEEMED TO MOVE AROUND ME." HE SAID THE OBJECTS WERE STEADY AND ABOUT THE SAME COLOR AS "GREENISH-YELLOW." THEY SEEMED TO BE A GOLDEN COLOR OF THREE TO FIVE MILES AND ABOUT 3 FEET IN DIAMETER OR A HOUR EITHER FASTER OR SLOWER THAN THE CAPSULE.
