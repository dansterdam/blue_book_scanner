35. Information which you feel pertinent and which is not adequately covered in the specific points of the questionnaire or a narrative explanation of your sighting.

IT CAME AT ME APPROXIMATELY 5 FEET OFF THE HIGHWAY
WITH DULL COLORED, NO LIGHTS, NO SOUND. THERE WAS
A BUBBLE ON TOP BUT IT WAS THE SAME COLOR
AS THE REST AND WAS NOT TRANSPARENT FROM
OUTSIDE. WHEN THE OBJECT BANKED TO GO ABOVE ME
I COULD SEE A SMALL OPENING IN THE BOTTOM
THAT APPEARED TO BE OPEN AND TUCKED UNDER.
THERE WERE NO VISIBLE MOVING PARTS TO THE OBJECT
BUT GAVE THE APPEARANCE OF WORKING BY MAGNETIC
FORCE ONLY AN ASSUMPTION.
