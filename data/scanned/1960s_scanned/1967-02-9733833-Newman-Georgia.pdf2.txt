35. Information which you feel pertinent and which is not adequately covered in the specific points of the questionnaire - a narrative explanation of your sighting.

[handwritten: We first sighted the object I was standing looking through upstairs glass. Danced at clock with illuminated dial noting time. It be 10:30 P.M. To make sure that it was a reflected off the glass I went outside and observed object. There was an absence of sound from adjacent dwelling and green lights flashing on object above there constantly shifting pattern, a haze about the object flashed on and off irregularly. Upper flashing on the haze stayed on for about 2 to 3 seconds outlasting and having metal skin of object distinctly. Being an Amateur astronomer I have observed any exerted planetary movements, satellites, and meteors. I wonder all exertions and more have some only resemblance to this object.]
