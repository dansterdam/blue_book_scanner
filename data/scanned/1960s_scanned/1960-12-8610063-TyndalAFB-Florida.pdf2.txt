SUBJECT: Report on Unidentified Flying Object (UFO)

TO: Aerospace Technical Intelligence Center (AFCIN) 4E2X
Wright-Patterson AFB, Ohio

1. In accordance with AFR 200-2, paragraph 15, dated 14 September 1958, the following Flying Object Report is submitted:

a. Description of the object:

(1) Shape - slightly elliptical.

(2) Size of a grapefruit.

(3) Color - Similar to a star but very bright.

(4) Number - 1

(5) Formation - N/A

(6) Discernible features: Object was traveling on a steady course and appeared to weave slightly.

(7) Tail or Trail: Non observed.

(8) Unusual Features: Object seemed to stop and move in spurts.

b. Description of Course of Object:

(1) When I first noticed the UFO at 1845 hours, 19 December 1960 at Canshart Housing, Tyndall AFB, Florida, azimuth 315°, it seemed to be stopping and moving in spurts. I observed it for a while to make sure it was not an aircraft. I called Captain _____ [redacted] (Rated Pilot) and MSGT_____ [redacted] (Director of Operations) to look at it. I tried to call Captain_____ [redacted] but he was not at home. The Base Weather Station was notified.

(2) Angle or elevation and azimuth of object: I first noticed the object in the northwest on an azimuth of 315°.
