At 2045 hrs, 1 Aug 1962, I was watching the swelling sky for the expected appearance of a B-57, when vapor trails could be readily seen. Right beside the star Arcturus (I think) I saw what appeared to be another star, but moving toward me. As it moved thru the sky, almost directly overhead, I was satisfied that it was the Echo satellite, and watched it move toward the NNE. About 3° above the NNE horizon it began veering to the right or toward the east and at this time turned in an easterly direction until it faded from view.
