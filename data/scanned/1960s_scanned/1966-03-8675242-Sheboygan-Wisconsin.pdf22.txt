35. Information which you feel pertinent and which is not adequately covered in the specific points of the questionnaire or a narrative explanation of your sighting.

The first thing that caught our attention was a "pair" of lights, white lights.

When we got closer, the object we could see that the space between the lights was approximately 4the the span of the wings of a large airplane. At this time all the lights as depicted in drawing on page 3, no.19, were not flashing but were steady. We are not sure whether the shadow - shape we could make out was two bow or an outline of the object.

Were were the only people on the street at this time. The object did not disappear while we were watching it, but after we moved to a different location we could not see it where it had been. We thought it did disappear. We were amazed when suddenly at re-appeared from behind us (from opposite direction we were watching) there right over us, just above the tree tops, flew very low across the city and disappeared from view at the time of this second sighting. There were more lights, mostly white, some red and green. This time the lights were flashing in an irregular pattern.
