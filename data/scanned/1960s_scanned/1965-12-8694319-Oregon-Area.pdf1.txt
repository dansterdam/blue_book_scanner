5. LENGTH OF OBSERVATION
From 30 sec to 5 min

11. BRIEF SUMMARY AND ANALYSIS
Observers sighted a round beam shaft of light about the size of a star. It was white blue green. It was balloon-shaped with long tail which engarged in size. There was a sound and the object seemed to vaporize and leave a contrail. Object traveled in straight line to the rest. The time of the sighting coincides with the launch of a Minuteman missile from Vandenberg AFB. There appeared to be an unusual amount of visual phenomena associated with this particular launch.
