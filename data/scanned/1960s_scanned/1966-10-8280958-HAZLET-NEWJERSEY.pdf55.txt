[redacted] abed to the undersigned that while enroute to her
place of employment at Fairmont, West Virginia, from her residence
in Grafton, at approximately 5:55 a.m. DST, October 12, 1966, she
did observe the following: A light moving in the sky at a low
altitude which appeared to her at first to be a jet aircraft. She
stated it had a red flame approximately three feet to the rear of
the craft and was gliding toward the Earth. She was of the opinion
that it was going to crash. She further stated that she applied
her brakes not knowing whether she completely stopped the vehicle or
reduced to a slow rate of speed. She watched the object descend over
the crest of a large hill and into the valley to her right. She
stated that the object was close enough now that she could see it
had no running lights such as those found on aircraft and that the
object had no wings.

Her description of the object is as follows: Approximately 20 feet
in diameter, round in structure, and three to four feet in thickness at its thickest point. She stated it was giving off a soft
bluish glow. No other lights were visible with the exception of
the flame from the rear as previously described.

She stated that she became alarmed and frightened when she realized
that she was seeing an object that she had only read and heard about
previously. She watched the object as it glided into the valley.
It stabilized itself hovering or moving very slowly, parallel with
her vehicle. Her direction of travel at that time was West on U.S.
Route 50. She stated that she observed the object for at least 15
seconds or more and then it gained altitude and passed out of her
field of vision, over the top of her vehicle and over the crest of
the hill to her left. She stated that she believed the object was
