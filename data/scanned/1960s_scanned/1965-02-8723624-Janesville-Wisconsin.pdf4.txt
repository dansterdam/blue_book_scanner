35. Information which you feel pertinent and which is not adequately covered in the specific points of the questionnaire or a narrative explanation of your sighting.

[handwritten text transcription:]
I had just been to the bathroom and before I went back to bed I walked over to the closet. It was 3 a.m. I got in to bed and sat up close to the window to see if I could see any stars. I saw just one. – It had been very cloudy. When I went to bed at 10:30, but was clear later with a very few light clouds. I didn't see the moon. In the east the horizon was a dark gray strip. Then this thing appeared. It was such a surprise that all I could do was look.

The thing itself was light but it didn't shed any light. The vicinity of the thing was no lighter than the rest of the sky. The back part had no highlights. But the rays were like rays was showing by there. There was a dark rim of some kind on the front of the ball, but I didn't have time to look back for it. It was going from N.N.E. to S.S.W. I got up and looked at the clock after it had passed and it was 3:05. I never that of paying attention to all the things you asked about. I just was happy I had seen it. It looked so clear and so close I that it would drop near the airport or almost. I heard no sound.

I hope this will help you.
Sincerely
