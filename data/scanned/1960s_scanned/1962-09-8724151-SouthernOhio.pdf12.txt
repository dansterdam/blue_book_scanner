UFO REPORT

TO-X2                                                           18 Sep 62

TO: TD-F (Col Petend)

The following, extracted from Maj Edward's UD Report dated 17 Sep 62,
is quoted for your information:

Two UFO reports were received. The first was from the Dayton
Post of the Ohio State Patrol which was reporting third person
sightings made by Sheriffs Deputies, private citizens, and Ohio
State Patrolman in the St Clairsville sector around 0905
EDT on 9/15/62. The second was by the Lockbourne AFB
SAC Command Post which gave the reports of two sightings by
B-47 Aircraft Commanders who were "in trail" 63 NM apart flying
from Tennessee (353 N/4700N).

Names: Deputy Donald Corey, 433 Idora, Youngstown, Ohio
       Deputy John P. Rafina, 138 Monroe St., Campbell, Ohio
       Patrolman John W. Gowder, Ohio State Patrol, Box 377,
           St. Clairsville, Ohio
       Dispatcher E.L. Kuhn, Ohio State Patrol, Box 397,
           St. Clairsville, Ohio
       [illegible] Clairsville, Ohio
       Mrs. [illegible], St Clairsville, Ohio

NOTE: None of these people reported directly - report was third
      person by Dayton Post of Ohio State Patrol.

The second report was from Lockbourne AFB SAC Command Post:

Aircraft Commanders:
    Lead Aircraft - Captain J.S. Krill, 4745th, 513-h
                    Bomb Sqdn, Lockbourne AFB
    Aircraft 22  - Captain W.C. Wetzel, 4752nd, 513-h
                   Bomb Sqdn, Lockbourne AFB

Briefly: SAC pilots and ground observers saw "funnel shaped
         clouds" in a level position like a "super con tail"
         with an extremely bright light at the apex of the cone.
         Light lasted for about 30 seconds and cloud dissipated
         quickly after light went out. SAC pilots were over
         Tennessee headed north East and ground observers were
         in Ohio looking south. Time: 0405am (090352). Weather:
         Clear, moonite night, visibility unlimited.
