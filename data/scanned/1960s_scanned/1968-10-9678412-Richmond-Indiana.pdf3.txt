MEMO FOR THE RECORD                                    21 October 1968

Subj:  UFO Observation, 21 October 1968, Richmond, Indiana

Mrs [REDACTED], Richmond, Indiana  47374,
called on 21 October 1968 to inform the Aerial Phenomena Branch of an
unidentified flying object sighting.

DATE:       21 October 1968
TIME:       0500 hours
DURATION:   2- 3 mins
DIRECTION:  W to E
DESCRIPTION:  Sighted two constant white lights (that appeared
to be one foot apart) and a blinking orange light between.  There was
no sound.  The lights were low on the horizon and moved at airplane
speed.
