[black redaction]

Sheboygan, Wis.

May 4, 1966

A weird feeling came over us, especially since we were the only people on the street and in the only moving vehicle on the street at the time. We had the feeling it might descend and become a little afraid. However drove to the next corner, turned right, and continued for about a half block; then we parked across the street from a safe distance; but now it seemed to have disappeared. While we were wondering about the disappearance, we were completely surprised when the large object came from behind us and passed over us approximately 50 ft. above ground level. Now it seemed that it might have circled around to pass over us again. Now we could see numerous lights, mostly white hat
