KEESLER AF'B, MISSISSIPPI

4 APRIL 1965

The time of the sighting was approximately 4:40 am, C.M.T. The observer sighted a black oval object estimated to be 40 ft. long and appeared to be the shape of a football with four lights evenly spaced from one end to the other on the underside. The lights were all white and of high intensity and looked the same when they disappeared as when they appeared. The intensity was compared to that of a satellite but the object was not a satellite because it was too low and four lights rather than one. At first the observer only saw the four lights, but when the object got closer it looked like a dark oval, darker than the cloud background. There was a cloud layer at 1200 ft., and the object was slow these clouds. The observer first saw the lights at approximately 45 degrees elevation and 200 - 230 degrees azimuth, in the southwest. The object passed overhead and headed northeast. It faded into the clouds 15 - 20 seconds later at approximately 30-45 degrees elevation and 020 degrees azimuth. This would make a total run of 90 degrees. The observer did not venture a guess on speed but stated that it was fast. Also, no sound was heard and no trail or exhaust was observed.

COMMENTS:

The observer at first thought the object was an aircraft and he called FAA and they had no aircraft in the vicinity at the time. Keesler has no flying at night, unless it would be a Coast Guard aircraft, everything on the flight line is quiet between 10 pm and 6 am.

A balloon has to be ruled out since the wind was from 130 degrees and the object moving largely across way to wind.
