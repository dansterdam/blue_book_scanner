11. BRIEF SUMMARY AND ANALYSIS

6. TYPE OF OBSERVATION
Ground-Visual

Observer lives on top of hill and spotted object at 75 deg above the North, and after 2½ minutes noticed object change course to 30 deg, altitude of 50,000 ft with estimated very high speed. Object was white, bright as Venus. After change of 30 deg course, heading 20 to 5, he noted 2 glares objects orange lights traveling parallel to flight path and gaining altitude as on attempt to intercept objects. All three objects passed directly overhead and traveled SE. The large object appeared 2/3 the original size after 4 minutes of observation, the two smaller orange lights did not intercept the larger object. No sound.
