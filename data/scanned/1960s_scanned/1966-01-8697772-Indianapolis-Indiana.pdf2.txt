January 14, 1966 - Friday Evening - 6:30-6:50 P.M. (timed
by watch). We were 6200 North and 1 block west of
Collin Ave. (Indianapolis, Indiana.
Sighted at 6:30 P.M., had just walked out the door of
the above address (Doctor's Office). Spotted it hovering
about 300 ft. high; fingernail held at arms length, at
about 30 degree angle, whirling or in motion, colors of
red, yellow and white. Seemed dip and rise very
rapidly (west), 6:48 P.M. completely disappeared. Dark
again at 3:45 P.M. and lost permanently at 6:50 P.M.
(Weather was hazy nor'wester)

We had two witnesses.
