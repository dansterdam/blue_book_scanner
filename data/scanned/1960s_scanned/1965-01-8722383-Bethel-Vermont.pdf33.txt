5 More Report
They Saw Those
Bethel 'Saucers'

Five men have written to
Dr. Richard S. Woodruff, tell-
ing him they saw three
unidentified flying objects the
same time he did on Vermont
Rt Jan. 1.

Dr. Woodruff and a state
trooper, traveling between
Randolph and Bethel reported
they both saw three noiseless
objects, flying at treetop level,
carrying orangish-red lights at
4:30 p.m.

Dr. Woodruff, who is said
pedagogist and staff member
of the University of Vermont
College of Medicine, said four
men driving in one car had
written they also saw the UFO's.
Another man driving alone
also wrote to the pathologist,
confirming what he saw.

Dr. Woodruff said there
was absolutely no possibility
of the UFO's being collecting
power-readable mirrors from
window glass, or car lights, as
one writer suggested to him.

The correspondents said
they had no explanation for
the objects but would certai-
nly like to have the mystery
cleared up.
