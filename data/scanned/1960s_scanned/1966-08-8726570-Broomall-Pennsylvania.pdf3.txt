Johnsville Pa.

Dear Major King;

A friend of mine heard again speaks of the USAHC banquet one suggested I write to you.

On Thursday night August 4th, about 11:15pm I saw a UFO. When I first saw it, there standing still in the sky, then it moved away, and apparently then self-moved on. I was inside sunday, aole. saber, and I'm not a Kook!

I'm leaving for vacation King 11 and will return King 27. However I doubt that your people find it necessary to get in touch with me.

Very truly yours
Violette C. Palomela
(Lt, U.S.M.E.)
