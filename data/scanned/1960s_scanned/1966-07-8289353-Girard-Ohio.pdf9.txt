July 8, 1966

Dear Sir:

I would like to make a report on some U.F.O. observations which may be of interest to you. I am a high school language teacher with considerable scientific interest and astrology capacity. I have excellent vision and hearing and have never been subject to hallucinations.

As an infantry veteran in World War I, I observed many fearful and awesome sights. So I am not easily impressed or frightened.

I have followed scientific progress in the space age in magazines and newspapers. I have observed passing method day and night, the single engine biplane monoplane, helicopter, dirigible, propeller and jet fighters and bombers and the new passenger jet. I have observed the "Oct." balloon traveling high overhead and watch for T.V. the astronauts give at Cape Kennedy.

I have flown night and day in passenger planes since learning that U.F.O's have been sighted in various sections of the U.S. I keep scanning the sky in all directions whenever it goes outside, lately night.
