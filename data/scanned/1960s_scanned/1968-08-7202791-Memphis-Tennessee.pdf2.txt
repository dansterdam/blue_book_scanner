Lausanne Girls
Report a UFO

Twenty-six Lausanne
School students, and Mrs.
Dan Allen, head of the
school's History Depart-
ment, say they were witness-
es last night to an unusual
flying object.

Carrie Wimberly, Lau-
sanne junior, said students
outside the dormitory saw
the strange object between
8:30 and 9. "It was too slow
to be a plane, and it was not
elongated like a blimp. It
had red and green lights on
top. Near the top were large
windows. On the bottom half,
white lights were revolving.

"It was in view about 30
minutes. It would leave and
then come back. I saw it
four times. It would go down
and come back up. When it
came over us, I didn't hear
any noise. I've never seen
anything like it before."

Mrs. Allen saw it, too.
They reported it to the Sher-
iff's office.
