10. BRIEF SUMMARY OF SIGHTING
A white glowing object that looked like a spiked hovering over a wooded area at about 45 feet in the air for about 10 seconds. Heat could be felt coming from the object. It moved off and disappeared after a few seconds, and a muffled sound is jet a/c could be heard. Picture taken by a newspaper photographer. Negatives not received at ATIC.

11. COMMENTS
No analysis made of negative. Without which no evaluation can be made. It is possible that the witness had developed a leak and was being carried along by surface winds. The wings probably made up most of the story in order to create sensationalism, which in turn sells newspapers. The picture is not very clear and as such precludes a valid conclusion as to the nature of the object
