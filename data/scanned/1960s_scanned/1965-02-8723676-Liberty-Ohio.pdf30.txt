MR 12 February 1965

Ptl Pinkham returned call at 1005, 12 Feb 65, per request of Sgt Jones, relating to object he received from [redacted] residence on 6 Feb 65.

Ptl Pinkham said children were building a tree house and they saw the object and decided to get it and takt it to their house. Besides [redacted] a son a neighbor boy, both age 14, found it. The [redacted] boy took it to the neighbors and they said they better call the Air Force.

The boys said it has been there (in the water) since at least last summer at which time they said they first saw it.

Sgt Jones asked the Ptl if he asked the boys why they didn't get it then and he said he never asked them, but figured why they didn't, because the water was so deep for them.

Ptl Pinkham said where the boys found it was about 1/4 mile away from the [redacted] house.

Ptl Pinkham said the boys sounded like they were telling the truth.
