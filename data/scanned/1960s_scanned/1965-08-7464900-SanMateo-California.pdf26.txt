BRAZIL: Also on Tuesday night
August 3rd, observers in Curitiba,
Brazil reported seeing a squadron
of 18 flying saucers flying over
northern Brazil's Parana State.
(Still in the straight line, Brazil is
SE of Cape Kennedy.)
