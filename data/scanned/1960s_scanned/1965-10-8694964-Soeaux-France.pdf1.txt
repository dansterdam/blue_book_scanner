10. CONCLUSION
Astro (METEOR)
Vapor trail and duration of two to three seconds might possibly have been a meteor trail with the reflection of the sun's rays giving a reddish color to the object.

11. BRIEF SUMMARY AND ANALYSIS
Object was observed somewhat east of Paris at a high altitude. Dusk, with sun setting. Observer thought of Meteor then looked up again to study the object. The object had dropped or disappeared by that time. At the same time, the observer was able to observe a long trail of white vapor, thousands of meters in depth and almost perpendicular. The object was of a reddish color. The observation lasted no more than three min. Once the cloud of vapor had disappeared, nothing more was visible.
