October 12, 1966

Dear Sirs;
On Monday, October 10, 1966 I was playing without friends dog on the athletic field behind our house at about 7:20 PM. I looked up at the constellation of Pegasus and saw a very bright star yesterday. The square of Pegasus. A few minutes later I looked up again and the object had moved from a position on the south east horizon to a point just under Aries. I then walked down the field and watching the object all the time. A few minutes later, at about 7:25 PM, the object when below the north east horizon. The movement of the object was from south east to north east. The movement was slow and steady and the light given off by the object was steady. I did not see it explode or veer off or sound yet off and there was no burning effect as in a meteorite. During the time I saw the object three jets went over going to or from Newark Airport. They gave off no light like the object. I do not think it was a small airplane because I have seen many small airplanes land at and at the Princeton Airport and the object did not look like a small aircraft.
