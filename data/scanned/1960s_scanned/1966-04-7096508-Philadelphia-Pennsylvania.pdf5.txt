1. In accordance with the provisions of AFR 200-2, the following report on UFO sightings is submitted.

2. At 1830 on 9 April 1966, Miss Regina McFadden, 1838 N. 22nd Street, Philadelphia, Penna. contacted the Security Office of this installation and reported an object in flight over the North Philadelphia area which she said did not resemble a plane. Miss McFadden could not be contacted by this office for further details until the present date. When contacted, she provided the following information:

Shape - Circular
Size - Larger than the visual image of the sun
Color - Bright Orange
Number - One
No trail or exhaust
No sound

The object appeared to hover just over the buildings while moving slowly in a northerly direction. Object was observed at approximately 1430 zulu (1230 Philadelphia time). Object was maintained in sight for approximately ten minutes. No further details were available from the individual. Miss McFadden was walking in a direction generally north when she addressed at the time of sighting and was accompanied by a sister and brother-in-law. Addresses are not available.

3.
