10. BRIEF SUMMARY OF SIGHTING
Pumi. size of quarter, bright red with varying intensity. First, at an overcast. Later, at high altitude. Object sighted and hovered, went backwards & forth. First night it disappeared to E; second night it disappeared to N. First seen for 20 min; second time for 30 min.

11. COMMENTS
The most probable cause of this sighting is high flying balloon. However, in the statement by the witness that the object hovered is to be accepted this possibility must be ruled out (other than helicopters). This case is classed "Unidentified" for lack of data which points conclusively toward a solution.
