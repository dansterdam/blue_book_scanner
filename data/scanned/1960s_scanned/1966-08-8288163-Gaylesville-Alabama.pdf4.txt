Factual Observation:

(12) When the center "object" (light) was in its
     most forward position (see figure), there
     was a definite decrease in the intensity
     of the region where the leading object and
     the center object overlapped (dark spot).
     This occurred only once and then only for a
     fraction of a second but it was long enough
     that it was noticeable.

I didn't think that this was important until I was
discussing the event with some professors at
Georgia Tech. They seemed to think that it
could be important. One professor in the Physics
Dept. said that this could be a result of the light
being polarized and the decrease in intensity
caused by interference, however, this would
indicate that the "objects" were transparent —
possibly gases.
