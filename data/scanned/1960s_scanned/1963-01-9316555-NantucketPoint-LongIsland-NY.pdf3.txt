Dear Sir,

My wife and I own and operate a small motel here in Nantauk Point out on the tip of Long Island, N.Y. We operate in and out and in winter occasionally sent to transient workers or persons waiting at one of your radar bases here at the Point.

The Long Island Fish Road here at last station here about 3/4 mile from here. Adjacent to it Republic Aerospace has a small radar installation.

A Pvt. [redacted] who works for the [redacted] on car maintenance, and who resides here with his wife; came to see with a strange story one evening.

Incidentally this young man, has been with us about 3 weeks, is a good worker, able and he for as I can see, reliable.

His story was about the silence flying saucer ". I myself have must seen one and probably may still in the foreseeable future a Ufo. [redacted] told the story
