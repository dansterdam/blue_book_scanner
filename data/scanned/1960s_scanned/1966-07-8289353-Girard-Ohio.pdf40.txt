[handwritten: No Case Type ONLY, 7 Tolls, Ohio]

[handwritten: July 10, 1966]

Dear Sir:

I would like to make a report which may be of interest to the Defense Dept. I am a resident of Girard, Ohio which is in northeastern Ohio bordering Youngstown.

Thursday morning, July 7th, at 1 A.M., I witnessed, along a mysterious sight such as I have never seen before.

It was watering some newly planted shrubs when I noticed a light approaching silently, from the southwest at an angle of approx. 30° above the horizon. It moved straight on course at an altitude of 1000 feet or more.

As it came closer, and was silhouetted against the dark eastern sky, I had a better view. In its exhaust was a white fin shape, with a dark section running through the center. It would have been half a dollar bill, held at arms length, to have covered the exhaust. It was absolutely silent. I heard no sound whatsoever.
