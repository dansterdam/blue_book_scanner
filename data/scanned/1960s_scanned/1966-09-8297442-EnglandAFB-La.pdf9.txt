6 Oct. 66

Contact was made with the Lake Charles weather bureau (318-477521).
They normally launch balloons every six hours. A balloon was launched
at 2315Z 29 Sept. 66. 75 minutes after launch the balloon was report-
ing winds at 40 knots from 260 degrees. The balloon reached an
altitude of about 59,000 feet during this portion of the flight.
They suspected the balloon burst shortly after, but do not have
positive proof. They do not know if the balloon could have been
the culprit. The winds were blowing from the west and the object
was traveling with the wind.

11 Oct. 66

Contact was made with NCAR, Palestine, Texas (214-729-6921) con-
cerning balloon launches on or about 30 Sept. 66. Mr. Warrent
said that NCAR did not launch any balloons on the afternoon of the
29 SEPT. 66 to account for the observation. There were no balloons
launched from Palestine to account for the sighting.

13 Oct 66

Contact was made with the 6th Weather Group Major Steffer ex 601.
at Goodfellow AFB, Texas. There were no balloon launches on 29
Sept. 66 to account for the sighting. The last balloon that was launched
except the one that was launched 13 Oct. 66 was 26 Sept. 66.
