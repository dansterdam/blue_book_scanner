PLANE CAUSES CALLS

Strange Lights
Not Invaders

"Are you sure we're not being invaded?"
That was the question of one telephone caller last night after police received a call about strange lights spotted over the Wright-Patterson Air Force base and the Huber Heights area.

Told that it was a case of an airborne illumination system which may replace the use of searchlights in Vietnam, the caller still remained unconvinced.

Police received a call after the CISO there's a copter with 2 high intensity beam lamps around 1:30 a.m. over Dayton after 10:29 p.m. and then under the 53 x aircraft.
