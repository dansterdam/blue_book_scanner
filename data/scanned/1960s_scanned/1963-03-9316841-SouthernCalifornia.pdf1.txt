10. BRIEF SUMMARY OF SIGHTING:
Silver obj observed fm F-101's at 50,000 ft. Obj above at 80 dgr elevation longer off port. Sighting between Ventura & Santa Barbara. In sight 5-10 mins. 101's made turn & lost sight of obj. Estimated obj to be at 80,000 ft going 500 knots. 2. Bright light resembling star, white, with out features, no contrail or exhaust. High against clear blue sky. Object 30 dgr above horizon. Disappeared instantaneously to East like lightbulb going out. Reported at Santa Barbara, Vandenburg, Oxnard area.All sightings air visual.
