OFFENSE: SUSPICIOUS FLARES (UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECT)
                                                                     C.A: 29069

DATE: 9-29-65

TIME: 1:40 P.M.

Interviewed Mrs. [blackout], [blackout], Rio Vista.
She stated that she is a member of the National Investigations
Committee on Aerial Phenomena (NICAP). She gave this officer a
pamphlet issued by this organization citing numerous incidents of
visual sightings of flying objects including one at Rio Vista,
May 13, 1954. She stated that she had an 8x10 color
slide of these objects that she had taken herself and included one
that she took sometime ago that shows humanlike things in it. This
particular object had landed on the outskirts of Rio Vista and she had
taken this photo and when it had been developed she then noted what
appeared to be small windows on the side of this object. She states
that she and others had seen fairly well and that there appears
to be small people in the object. Both are dragged in some type
of flying suits or coveralls and that between. This officer was
shown some photos. However nothing could be made out other than
what appeared to be an elongated red glow. Mrs. [blackout] was thanked
for her cooperation in this matter.

CC: Report to Lt. Col. K. S. Berger Travis Air Force Base for transmittal
to proper authorities.

                                             JOHN CRUZ--RESIDENT DEPUTY
