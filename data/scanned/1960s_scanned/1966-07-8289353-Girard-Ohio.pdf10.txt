My first observation of a U.F.O. was on July 7th,
at 1 AM, in the morning.

On the night, visibility was excellent, the
sky was full of stars and the moon about 3/4s full
was about 150 above the horizon in the east.

As I stood in my front yard, looking toward
the east, I became aware of a light approaching
from the southwest, at the periphery of my vision.
As it became silhouetted against the dark
eastern sky, I had a better view. It was approx
45° above the horizon. The exhaust was a white,/tan
shape with a dark streak running through its center.
The exhaust was so large that it would have
taken me half a dollar bill held at arm's length
to have completely covered it. It seemed to be
more broad than long.

I could not make out the exact shape of the
dark body of the craft which noticed blacking at
the stars as it passed. There were no lights
visible in the dark body of the craft.

It was traveling at approx. the speed of a jet
plane, but there was no sound whatsoever, at any time.
The exhaust light turned completely off for a second or so
and then came back on, at a considerable distance from the
sling in the eastern sky. There was nothing to block the view;
