White spent seven months in Nazi prison camps. After the war, he came back home and picked up his flying where he left off as a freshman. Though White was no coward, he comes, he decided to stay in the Air Force. His cold, precise flying won him 15 years of experimental test-pilot training. Since 1955, White checked out as pilot on replacements; the F-86K, F-100H, F-102 and F-102B. The 1950 nearly did him in. Process of Education

While deliberately downplaying any idea that the U.S. might be on the brink of another recession—a task that becomes increasingly difficult as more disappointing economic indicators come out—it began to be "educating" the Congress and the people to the need for a tax cut, whether or a cut is advisable now.
