[handwritten: Sept. 4, 1966]

Dear Major Quintanville,

I have a confession to make. When I was in your office, Monday afternoon, I didn't tell you everything that I knew about the rocket shaped space craft which passed over Girard, Ohio at 11:11, July 7th. This confession will make me look bad, but if it helps in the defense of the United States, I'll just forget my feelings.

I believe that in telling you this, it may tip the balance scales as to whether the craft was from another planet or from Russia. I his account, I believe will seem to indicate that the scales are tipping more toward Russia as the owner of the probable nuclear space craft.
