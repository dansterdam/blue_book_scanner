GLOBAL SIGHTINGS:
ATL.-TIC OCEAN-Oct 3, 1962....From a Navy
publication dated Nov 10, 1962................
The sighting was made by Second Officer J.
Connors of the American SS Smith Tourist,
at 0745 GLT (11:45 AM EST)....Enroute from
Bahamas to Morocco, he sighted "what ap-
peared to be a rocket or space ship in the
sky." The 'unknown' stressed from W to E,
leaving a trail as bright as the sun and
the same color.
