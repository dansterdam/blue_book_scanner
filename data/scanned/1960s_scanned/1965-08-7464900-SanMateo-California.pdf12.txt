GENERAL COMMENTS: It is interesting that all witnesses
were quite convinced that the photo B had been
taken facing north, a complete possibility.
Even the photographer's records, written immediately
after the sighting, give no indication that a
photo was taken facing east that includes a star
and pole, although one is indicated for the north.
This sighting and investigation points up the need
for on the spot investigations as soon as possible
after the sighting to minimize the need of relying
on the memory of a witness. Even though the incident
is still vivid in my mind, it is most frustrating that
I can remember the star-pole photo taken facing north
(which was never returned by the newspaper) as if
I took it yesterday, but can not, even in the face of
this investigation, recall a similar photo taken facing
west. Perhaps had the investigatin been conducted
sooner after the sighting this photo puzzle could
have been solved, or perhaps it would never have
existed.

Judi Anne Hatcher,
investigator
report prepared 8/67
