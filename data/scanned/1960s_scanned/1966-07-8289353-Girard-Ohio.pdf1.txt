6. TYPE OF OBSERVATION: Ground Visual

7. COURSE: NE

8. PHOTOS: [ ] Yes    [X] No

9. PHYSICAL EVIDENCE: [ ] Yes    [X] No

10. CONCLUSION: Other (psychological)    [handwritten: Jry]

11. BRIEF SUMMARY AND ANALYSIS: Observer noted dark mass in front of exhaust. Object had indefinite shape. Exhaust light shut off completely for 5 seconds. After the exhaust light disappeared the dark mass was still visible. Exhaust light came on again at a considerable distance further toward the northeast. Object disappeared behind some trees. There was no sound of any type noted.

Personnel interview with witness revealed he had some type of ESP. He felt that the men in the craft were trying to communicate with him. He felt that the craft was not from outer-space but a result of ingeneous Russian design. Observer stated he had this form of ESP and could feel persons thinking about him from a distance. He definitely believed that the men in the craft were observing him. This wasomhat caused them to cut off the exhaust for the brief period.
