Conversation of Mr. [illegible] on 29 August 1956 on interview with Major R. Quintanilla, Jr and 1st Lt. William F. Marley.

Since I didn't have instruments my first opinion of the size of ½ the size of a one dollar bill is in error. Later in comparing it in my mind I discovered my mistake. On Friday the moon was at the same height and I found that the size should have been a penny. Exhaust was a little larger than the moon. Object passed right over the moon and was a little larger than the moon on 7 July. I have some basic scientific background. Now to get to the description:

Body size - 2 dimes held at arm's length - 75% accuracy

Using an object to cover object I would use two dimes. I figure this with 75% accuracy. Moon was 3/4 full, 15 degrees above the horizon. Moon threw some light on the object. Object passed over the moon. It was so close and low.

Now - off the subject. There have been accounts in the newspaper. So many people have seen UFOs that there must be something to them. In May I set shrubs out and watering them, and have been looking twice a week from 1100 (2300) - 0100 while watering these. I have had opportunities for considerable observations. I have been looking for it. Stars helped to illuminate it and so did the moon. Object was about one mile distant. Moon 3/4 full, I checked with the calender. Big exhaust threw illumination on the object. Only viewed it for about thirty seconds.

Body shape like a rocket. Couldn't be real definite.

Body color was dusky brown. Could tell from illumination. Couldn't be real certain.
