Ltr, AFCIN-4E2,25 March 1960,UFO Film

1st. Ind (AFCIN-4D4a)                                14 April 1960

TO: AFCIN-4E2 (Col R.R. Shoop

1. It is not quite clear what the originator of the film was
referring to in the description of the object as there was
nothing in the series of pictures taken outside which fit this
description. The portion of the film which was taken inside of
what appeared to be a barracks type building was extremely
under-exposed and the many small yellow lights are reflections
from items such as the lieutenants bars, the windows and
objects on the desk. Throughout this sequence the light bulb
in the ceiling mirrored light on the lens of the camera
creating many circular red and yellow halations on the film.

2. It is suggested that the originator be asked to view the
film, and indicate which frames he had reference to.
