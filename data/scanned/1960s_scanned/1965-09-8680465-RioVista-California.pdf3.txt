Source: Saucer News, Vol 13, #1, March 65                          21 September 65


CALIFORNIA YOUTHS SHOOT AT UFO: On the night of September 21st, in the
midst of a local saucer flap, two 17-year-old boys from Rio Vista, California,
shot a .22 rifle bullet at a flying saucer which had landed near a water tower
on edge of town. This occurred following a wild chase of eight miles, dur-
ing which the youths followed the UFO until it finally landed. The bullet made
a metallic sound as it bounced off the craft. The UFO was hovering just three
feet above the ground at the time. It was glowing, and appeared to be about
the size of an automobile hood, according to these and other witnesses. (San
Diego Union, 10/1/65.)
