May 18, 1967

Personal Comments:

1. After considering the facts as stated in the report to the Air Force, I have concluded that the unusual aerial phenomenon that we observed was an aero-electromagnetic phenomenon - most probably a result of natural atmospheric conditions combined with the electrical field near-ly on the ground and the magnetic field of the earth.

2. I believe that the "lights" were gases, electrically charged, which derived their motion from a force which was a combination of the nearby electrical field and the magnetic field of the earth.

3. I think others, working in the field of U.F.O. or U.A.P. (unusual aerial phenomenon) should be made aware of this observation - especially the "BLINK" which occurred when the two "objects" (lights) crossed. - This could have a definite effect upon the establishment of the cause of such phenomena.

4. NASA should not ignore the U.A.F. since it could possibly lead to new ideas for interplanetary probes for purposes of mapping the magnetic fields of other planets.
