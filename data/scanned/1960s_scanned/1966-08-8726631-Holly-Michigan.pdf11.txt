14. Did the object disappear while you were watching it? If so, how?

The object I saw did disappear. It seemed to get very large (one-half Saturn in size) and then it moved down toward the horizon. It then stopped just above the horizon and faded out of sight. The objects my friends saw flew out of sight.

18. We wish to know the angular size. Hold a match stick at arm's length in line with a known object and use how much of the object is covered by the head of the match. It would cover the object completely, I think.
