16 FEB, 1965

A Vermont UFO sighting by Dr. Richard S. Woodruff, State
Pathologist, and a State Police official, was dated Feb. 16, 1965,
not Feb. 17. The correct date is Jan. 4. Dr. Woodruff, the
police official, and other witnesses saw three low-flying objects
race above a passway at an estimated 3,000 f.p.h.
