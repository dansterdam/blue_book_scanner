[stamped/redacted section - text partially obscured but readable:]
in Jacksonville, Florida, a number of strange lights, accompanied
by power outages were reported on October 4th. One of the most unusual sta-
ries was told by Mr. and Mrs. J. P. Baker, who saw a ball of fire moving
through a field behind their house, just at twilight. Mrs. Baker said that she
looked out the window and saw a round ball with a pinkish light that almost
blinded her. It hovered outside her kitchen window, less than six feet away,
and then passed around the house, crossed the roof then flew on.
Another woman claimed that a formless glow moved right through her house,
greatly scaring her and her children.
