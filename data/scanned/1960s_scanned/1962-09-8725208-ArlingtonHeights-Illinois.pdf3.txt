[handwritten:]
This paper had caused a reaction on the surrounding things. When I looked at the moon which was bright and shell at night I didn't see a moth but a beautiful ornate cross with a trail of light extending downward. There was a blaze of light behind it, but no much glow. The lines of the cross were prominent, the light was behind the cross and it was bright yellow. This lasted a while and then disappeared. The moon was seen again but it seemed to be transparent and a much paler yellow than before. It didn't effect the stars. It was about a half hour later before we saw any airplanes and they were sleeping very high and at a distance. The local police reported no airplanes enroute at the time we called about the mysterious vapor.
