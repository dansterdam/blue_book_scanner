NUTTY SIGHTING

Persons making sighting;
Paul Nutty, age 17, 1634 Georgia Ave. Connersville, Ind. Phone 825 6154, Education
11th Grade now,

Date of Observation; Sunday Oct. 22, 1966 t 30 minutes until 1.CCAM

Location ; Just south of Milton Ind. on State rd 1.

Total time seen; Approx. 15 seconds.

Sky conditions; clear cold starry night, crescent moon in 1st quarter in western sky.

Narrative;

Paul was riding Honda in company of two other friends on motorcycles. As he approached
Milton on state road 1 he looked up to see if he could see the seven sistars star group.
His eyes caught sight of a rapidly moving (he said much faster than a jet.) pale orange
oval shaped object in the sky at approx. 80 degrees east. It appeared solid, sharp , smooth
edged, and was larger than the moon. It was about 3/4 the size of a dime held at arms
length. He yelled at the other riders but they did not hear him. Object disappeared at
30 degrees elevation when he went behind some trees along road.

Conclusion; Unidentified- Insufficient data.

Don Worley
Oct. 24, 1956
