VIENNA, MONDAY, MARCH 7--(UPI)--THE INDEPENDENT VIENNA, MONDAY NEWSPAPER "WIENER PCHTAG" TODAY PUBLISHED A FULL FRONT PAGE PHOTO OF A FLYING SAUCER WHICH IT TERMED THE "MOST SENSATIONAL PHOTOGRAPH OF OUR CENTURY."

THE NEWSPAPER SAID ONE OF ITS PHOTOGRAPHERS BY CHANCE SHOT WHAT SEEMS TO HAVE BEEN A FLYING SAUCER WHICH MOVED AT A HEIGHT OF ONLY 45 FEET.

IT QUOTED EDGAR SCHEDELBAUER, JR., THE NEWSPAPER'S CORRESPONDENT IN STYRIA PROVINCE, HAS ON HIS WAY HOME AT 1:30 A.M. LAST TUESDAY.

NEAR THE VILLAGE OF LIELTENTZ WE SUDDENLY NOTICED A GLOWING OBJECT MOVING AT HIGH SPEED TO THE EAST," THE NEWSPAPER SAID.

SCHEDELBAUER SAID, "ACCORDING TO WIENER PCHTAG NEXT THE ROTATING OBJECT MOVED SLOWLY AND REMAINED STADY FOR ABOUT 1.5 SECONDS OVER A WOOD.

"IT LOOKED LIKE A WHITE-GLOWING SPLUS," HE SAID. "IT RESEMBLED TOO MUCH AN 'UNKNOWN' PHOTOGRAPH AND MADE A FEW SHOTS. FORTUNATELY ENOUGH OF THEM WERE GOOD."

SCHEDELBAUER SAID HE CLEARLY FELT RADIATED HEAT COMING FROM THE UNIDENTIFIED OBJECT.

"WHEN THE FLYING SAUCER DISAPPEARED AFTER A FEW SECONDS' HEARING NOISE SIMILAR TO THAT OF A JET AIRCRAFT," THE NEWSPAPER SAID.

THE NEWSPAPER ADDED THAT IT DECIED TO PUBLISH THE PHOTO AND PICTURE ONLY AFTER CAREFUL CONSIDERATION AND AFTER EXPERTS HAD CHECKED THE NEGATIVE OF THE PHOTO.

IT SAID THAT NO SIGN OF A THICK-SHGA WAS NOTICED BY THE EXPERTST.

THE NEWSPAPER, ADMITED OUTRIGHT THAT AUSTRIAN SCIENTISTS CONSULTING WITH COLLEAGUES IN GERMANY AND AUSTRIA CONTACTED PRIOR TO PUBLICATION OF THE PHOTO, WERE "SKEPTICAL ABOUT" SCHEDELBAUER'S STORY.
