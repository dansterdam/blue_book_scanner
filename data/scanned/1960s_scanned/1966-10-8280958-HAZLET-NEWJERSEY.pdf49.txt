[handwritten: Hazleton, Ohio]

Dear Gentlemen:

I am the boy who with fourteen others saw the other night on October 10, 1966. The unidentified observation. The same thing appeared on Friday night October 21st. By now I think you should have the report but to add with it, I to saw it. I was going to report it to you instantly, but my cousin down in Pricetown Ohio phoned and told me latent over twenty minutes he had saw one come within 20 degrees from where he was. He told me it was about the size of a dime. Since then people from all over the county have called me on strange sightings over a few years.

The main reason I am writing is because I spent many hours at night watching these things, every clear night with maybe a few clouds is the best time to watch. Watching these UFO's play tag, or watch them do their crazy eight pattern is a sight to see. But the worst thing is watching airplanes coming over and being these UFO's come down a play with the plane and then disappear.
