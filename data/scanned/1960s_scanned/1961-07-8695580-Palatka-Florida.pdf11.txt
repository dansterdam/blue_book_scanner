Palatka, Fla
July 25, 1961

Pentagon
Washington, D.C.

Dear Sirs,

On the night of July 25, about ten minutes after eight, four flying objects were observed over Putnam County, Florida. They were moving from the west and about 85° off West horizon. They headed north. They went in every short distance in this direction. Then made a 90° turn and headed again in the west. At about 75° off West horizon the four objects seemed onto one. However to this day appeared blue white. After they converged they, then, disappeared behind tree. The incident was wittnessed by eight individuals. There was no sound from three objects.
