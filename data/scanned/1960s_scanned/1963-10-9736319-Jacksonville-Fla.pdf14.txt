Odd Shaped Object
Seen in Arkansas

Hunters and residents of Arkansas County, Ark. observed a strange-shaped UFO between 4:30 and 5:00 a.m. on October 1, 1963. As viewed from widely separated spots the object was described as shaped like a "question mark," "a cylinder with a shaft projecting downward" and a "boomerang."

Seeing conditions were good as the sky was cloudless. After appearing southeast of Dewitt, it hovered for several minutes and then sped away.
