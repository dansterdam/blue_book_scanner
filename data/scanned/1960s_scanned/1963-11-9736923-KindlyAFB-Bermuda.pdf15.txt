Dear Mr. Harvey:

The Air Force has completed an investigation made at your request, of an unusual aerial phenomena reported in the Fort Haron Times-Herald of November 12, 1963.

U.S. Air Force Technical Information Forms were furnished to Deputy Sheriffs Robert Baker and Maria Clark in order that they could provide as detailed an account as possible of the sighting. Upon receipt of their report, Dr. J. Allen Hynek, Director, Dearborn Observatory, Northwestern University, and civilian scientific consultant to the Air Force on the UAP program, talked by telephone with Deputy Baker. As a result of this conversation, and of Deputy Baker's report, it is believed that the object sighted was a helicopter. While efforts to identify a specific helicopter proved negative, one had been observed during daylight hours on subsequent days by other persons in the area, and their stories of the observations were carried in local newspapers.
