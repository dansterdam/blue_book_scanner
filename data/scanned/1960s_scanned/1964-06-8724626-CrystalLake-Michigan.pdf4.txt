9. [redacted] 0300 EST, 1 June 1964. 14-year old girl heard noise, saw bright flash on island in lake near cottage where she was staying. 10 minutes later heard noise again; loud roar with predominant nose at about 600 cps; became relatively loud and front yard brightly illuminated. Disc-shaped object from approximately behind and overhead, went north over lake, illuminating first water, then island. Noise and light continued until object went out of sight below treeline. Observy seen but hard to get details because of brightness of light. Crystal Lake, CO miles northwest of Lansing.
