35. Information which you feel pertinent and which is not adequately covered in the specific points of the questionnaire or a narrative explanation of your sighting.

I am a Deputy Sheriff, St. Clair Co. Mich. And my partner & I were on duty at this time, 4:30 AM Nov. 12, 1963 east patroling N E on 24 th Ave. ( Cut off ) & my pardner daid do you want to see somthing odd. I pulled off onto the shoulder of the rd. & stoped. then noticed out a light in the sky to the North of us. then this light i flashing ) came closer to us, appe. 200 to 300 yds away. & app. 300 ft. high. Officers,put the spot light on & pointed it at the object my pard. said the object with the light. At this time the object took off to the N.E. Patrol want in on 24 th Ave. App. 1 & miles at lakeshore Rd. Officers lost sight of object due to street lights. it turned North on Makeshore app. 2 miles to & turned West. West appe. limited & stoped on Rd. & turned out the lights of the patrol car. Spotted object again to the North & West of us at this time. While stoped here we turned the spot light on the top of the patrol car on & 2 object in the sky with the white flashing light also turned on a red flashing light. We turned our's off & then the object turned there red light off also. But at no time did they turn the white light higher. Patrol stoped on the rd. & the object continued on its way into the North. It was just about out of sight when it turned back to the South. As it came S. it passed to theWest of us. We started up & went 1/2 mile West to North Rd. & turned South. At thistime the object was, on & too the W. of us. We went S. 1 1/2 miles to Carrigan Id. & turned West. Went one mile & stoped at the intersection of Merriman & Vincent rd's.
This time patrol turned out the lights & also turned off the car hy. & got out of the car. The object was over the field if W corner. Its hhrs sounded like a Jet & seemed to b in a Idle. Object at a standstill. Turned the spot light on the object reval up the hhrs: & took off to the East. we gave up at this time & returned to P. Huron. time 5:15 AM.
