[handwritten: Wells Maine
october 24, 1962]

Department of the Air Force
Unidentified Flying Objects Department
Washington D.C.

Dear Gentlemen:
     On the night of October 20th at approximately 11:30 p.m. I was following a woods road through a small field in Wells Maine when I saw a light light-ened up and saw something on the sky that appeared to begin with to be a meteor leaving a very distinct trail behind it. In fact it appeared to be leaving an trail one from each side, which moved at once. Then suddenly it opened up like a object about the size of a dome and stayed thus way during most of the duration of the flight. The object appeared to me, or at least, about the size of a dome, circular in platform, and with high points of light on it. It was preceded by a small "bow" wave of light and tail of light at the aft of which extended down (keel width) until the object disappeared and went made five second afternoon. It did not glow out greatly could the body of meteor I have seen. It climbed and taking about 2½ second to elk so. The object due flew invisible about 3 seconds, and there were a few sparks.

(over)
