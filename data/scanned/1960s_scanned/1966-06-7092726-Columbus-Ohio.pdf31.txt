11. BRIEF SUMMARY AND ANALYSIS
Observers noted object that appeared as a light comparable with the brightest star. On both accounts light was whitish in color and no sound was noted.

6. TYPE OF OBSERVATION
Ground Visual

Aircraft were in the area; however, Echo II was passing overhead at 0244Z and believed responsible for the sighting.
