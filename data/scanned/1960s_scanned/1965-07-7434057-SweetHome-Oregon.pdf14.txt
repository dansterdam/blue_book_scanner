Dear Dr. Hynek,
     I would like to report the sighting of a UFO on the
night of July 15 at 9:55 till 10:30 approximately.
     Three boys plus myself, were lying on a rock ledge
listening to a baseball game, when I was looking towards
the big dipper and noticed an object moving slowly, think-
ing this was an old Echo Satellite I motioned to where it
was and said, "Look, there's an Echo satellite!"
     I first noticed it as it headed east between the second
star on the handle of the big dipper and the third star.
As we're surrounded by low mountains, the big dipper was
low in the Northwest.
     As we traced its path along the stars keeping it parralel
or perpinicular to star clusters & parts of constellations,
we noticed that it changed its path twice. First, towards
the Southeast and then east again. Everytime it changed its
course, it would stop for about five seconds before continuing.
     As it was twenty degrees (approximately) from the horien,
it stopped. At this time we could see it change colors from
yellow to red to yellow to red, oct. like an airplane beacon
at an airport but sometimes slow and sometimes fast.
