Source: APRO Bulletin  July 1963


Saucer lands in Italy

In the early hours of the 28th of De-
cember, 1962, a watchman on duty in
the courtyard of a suburban factory in
Milan, Italy, reported to police that he
had seen a 12-foot, 1-inch man climb out
of a "flying saucer" after it landed in
the courtyard. The little creature beck-
oned to the watchman, then another
small man gestured the first one back
into the ship which took off. Police
frowned upon the report, but launched
an official investigation. More will be
printed on this incident when more de-
tails are received.
