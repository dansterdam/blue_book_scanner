11. BRIEF SUMMARY AND ANALYSIS:

Observers were driving along on highway 35 when one observer noticed a small cluster of star-like objects. She rested her attention on them because they were brighter than other stars, and were clustered so closely together. A few seconds later she noticed that they were moving relative to several near by stars, seemingly at a slow rate of speed. It was at this point she mentioned the objects to the other observer. He made a few comments and continued driving, then he looked up and saw four circular, orange-yellow, glowing objects moving from E to W. There were at the vertices of an obtuse triangle with one more W in the obtuse vertex in the direction of flight. The fourth object seemed to be at equilibrium in the center of the triangle and moved relative to the other three with sporadic motion fore and aft along the direction of motion. Once leaving the vertices of the triangle formed in the two direction. A bright 3/4 full moon.
