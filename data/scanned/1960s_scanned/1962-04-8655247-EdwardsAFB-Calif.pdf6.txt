Flight Brings White New Laurels

[NON-TEXT PAGE: black-and-white photograph of two men in military flight suits, one seated and one standing, examining what appears to be flight equipment or controls]

Major Robert M. White is met on Rogers Dry Lake by his twelve-year-old son, Gregory, after the historic flight to an altitude record for winged craft. The famed X-15 pilot, a charter member of the Af-C Aerospace Primus Club, soared to new altitudes of almost 50 miles in a recent flight. The accomplishment earned the major the distinction of becoming the first man to earn astronaut wings in a completely pilot-controlled vehicle.
