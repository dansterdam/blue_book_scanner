UFO Observation, 11 October 1967

2nd AF. (SAC Scheduling)
Barksdale, AFB, Louisiana (LLIU)

1. The Aerial Phenomena Office is in receipt of a reported unidentified flying object observation over the Lake Charles (town), Louisiana area. The observer saw a series of bright fiery flashes, one flash approximately every eight seconds.

2. A check of our Low Altitude High Speed Training Route Chart indicates that your group is engaged in training flights over the area in question. Request information on whether there was activity on the Matagorda, Texas (Water)(03-12) route on 11 Oct of approximately 2100 hours, that could have accounted for this sighting.

FOR THE COMMANDER

[signature] HECTOR QUINTANILLA, Jr., Major, USAF
Chief, Aerial Phenomena Office
Aerospace Technologies Division
Production Directorate
