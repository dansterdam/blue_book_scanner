No Case, Information Only
Source: Saucer News, Vol. 12, No. 4
Dec 65

Asuncion, Paraguay
13 Jul 65

SAUCER WITH LIGHTED WINDOWS IN PARAGUAY: Of the dozens of good sightings
from South America of recent date, space permits us to print only a few of the
most sensational. One of these occurred near Asuncion, Paraguay, on the night
of July 13th. Five people traveling in an open jeep saw a group of three UFOs
in the sky. They remained in view for about five minutes, and were close
enough for the witnesses to distinguish clearly, on each, a row of windows or
all its lights according to one of the startled witnesses. The objects
the objects alternated between blue, white, orange, and red. No sound whatso-
over was heard.
