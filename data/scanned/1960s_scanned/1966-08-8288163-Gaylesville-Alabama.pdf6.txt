23 Nov 66

The Flip Low Altitude High Speed Training Route Chart -Eastern
U.S. was used to try and determine if any aircraft on a low
level training mission could have been responsible for the
observation. There are several low altitude high speed routes
in the area of the sighting. The most logical route was looked
at but according to the Military Training Routes Flight Information Publication it is only used during daylight hours.

[handwritten: D-68]
