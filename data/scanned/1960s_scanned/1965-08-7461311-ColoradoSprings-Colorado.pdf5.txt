10. CONCLUSION
Astro (ARCTURUS) ✓
Orbit characteristic of Astro body. Imagination of youths add'l details to the object. Estimation of elevation probably in error. ARCTURUS at 15 deg elevation setting at time of disappearance. Slight movement attributed with ARCTURUS and disappearance coincides with setting of this star.

4. NUMBER OF OBJECTS
One

5. LENGTH OF OBSERVATION
1 1/2 Hours

Object changing colors from red to green to white. Described as a round ball. Appeared to have oblong windows. Also appeared to have lights on clouds. Observed at 20 deg elevation in West disappearing at 25-30 deg in West. Observed for 1 1/2 hours. Witnesses two 14 year old youths.

Ground-Visual
