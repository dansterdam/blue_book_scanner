11. BRIEF SUMMARY AND ANALYSIS: Object with erratic movements thought to be gaseous vapors, as bright as an arc light. Exotic movements changed brightness, flashing and flickering, disappearing and reappearing, white and yellow at edges. Moved behind trees. Observed from moving car. Object initially low on horizon in East. First sighting lasted approximately 45 deg elevation. Second object appeared at 0350, blue appeared at 0407 est when object over to bottom and a larger object appeared to be forming on north side.

COMMENTS: VENUS in East rising. Main object reported evaluated as VENUS. Associated objects attributed to illumination of the watches of distortions due to atmospheric conditions.
