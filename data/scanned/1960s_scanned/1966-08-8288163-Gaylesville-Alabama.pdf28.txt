NOV 29 1967

Dear [redacted]

This will reply to your letter of November 5, 1967, in which you inquired as to the identification of your UFO sighting that you reported sometime ago.

We regret that we failed to notify you that your sighting could not be identified. You submitted a good report, but it was the only one received on this particular incident, and it could not be correlated with other known objects sightings in the area at the time.

Should you ever sight another flying object you cannot identify, if you will report it immediately to the nearest Air Force base, you will have a much better opportunity to have your sighting identified.

We hope the enclosed Project Blue Book report will be of interest. Thank you for your interest in the Air Force UFO program.

Sincerely,

DAVID L. STILES
Lt Colonel, USAF
Chief, Civil Branch
