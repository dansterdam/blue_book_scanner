WHOLESALE FURNITURE

AND

FLOOR COVERINGS

November 19, 1962

Captain Raymond Henry
Base Information Officer
MacDill Air Force Base
Tampa, Florida

Dear Captain Henry:

This will certify my telephone conversation with you a few months ago in relation to the unusual sight I witnessed this last Saturday evening at approximately 9:00 P.M.

The sight was of such unusual proportions that it is my opinion that it will bear some explanation and some thought and some investigation on the part of responsible men within our government.

I was sitting on my porch swing facing the East, my wife and I were having a quiet about shooting stars. We decided we would try to identify an airplane flying at an extremely high altitude approaching the house from the Northwest. This object appeared to glow very bright. The three lights came in a single line directly in line of the handle of the sky rear on my front porch and observed their movements, and while we could clearly see them three lights started gov- ing it. an extreme high speed in the direction of the Southeast. The lights, but varied slightly in the distance between each other as they were in that formation of three different objects. They were maintaining an approximate formation as they flew. They did fly in a completely straight line which would fly, but they seemed to bounce up and down some what as they varied the distance from each other. They were out of sight in approximately three to four minutes.

Our farm is situated on State Road 581 in Pasco County midway between Brooksville and Dade City.

If it should be within your power to offer some explanation, we certainly would appreciate hearing from you.

Very truly,
