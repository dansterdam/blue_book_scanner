35. Information which you feel pertinent and which is not adequately covered in the specific points of the questionnaire or a narrative explanation of your sighting.

At 11:30 I was awakened and told about the sighting. They told me they saw three objects come down from the sky (by coming down I mean going towards the ground) and one stopped. The other two flew off. They know I liked Astronomy and asked me to come out. I hesitated because I thought it was a practical joke. However, they were willing the truth. In the Sky about 20 or 25 degrees above the horizon it was rather small at first and seemed to be disappearing rapidly. However it change and it became brighter. It seemed to pulsate in slow. But when it became pulsating it stopped pulsating. It seemed to be flat yellowish star. However it was good deal large: that a star about twice as large or about 1½ the size of Saturn. I stopped looking at it in about five minutes. I talked with some friend. I came back in a few minutes to notice that it had moved a great deal down and now more, I also remember that it was cold. This might have been because of a possible dampness in the air. However there wasn't a halo around the moon.

I just figured out a way to tell you in more exacterms when the object was sighted. I drew just after the sighting some pictures of the constellation and their relation to the object. By using a star chart ifound that the about 10:00 PM the big Dipper was in the west, where it is shown in the drawing which am going to supply. I will supply the original on that makes a much more detailed one owith showing the bright stars and where they were.
