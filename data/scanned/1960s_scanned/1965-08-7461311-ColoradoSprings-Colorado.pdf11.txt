Source: FIR, Vol 1, No 3, September 1965                              Colorado Springs, Colo.

Colorado Springs Photo

The following photograph appeared in the Colorado Springs Gazette Telegraph on Saturday, August 14, 1965. The photo was taken by Mr. Guy Burgess Jr. The UFO was not visible when the photo was taken. A blueish in the emulsion of the film, the object is on the negative and it is not superimposed, scratched in or double-exposed. The photo was taken on Saturday, August 7, 1965, between 1 and 2 p.m. with a 35MM camera.
