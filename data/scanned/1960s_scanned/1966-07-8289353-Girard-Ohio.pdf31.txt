[handwritten: Sept. 12, 1966]

Dear Major Quintanilla:

I haven't stopped thinking yet about that space craft which passed over Girard, Ohio on the 7th and 10th of July. I keep trying to write out of my mind every last bit of information which might contribute something of value toward solving this puzzle. I have noticed many mod jet fighter planes passing over this area night and day, than I have seen since they took the 10th Fighter Squadron from the Youngstown Air Port. Just tonight it approx. 7:30 P.M. (Daylight time) a jet fighter passed over heading South and staying rather low. I luckily was hazy. I was talking to a highschool teacher in the front yard.
