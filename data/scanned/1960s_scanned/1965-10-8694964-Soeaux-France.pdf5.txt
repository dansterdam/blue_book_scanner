Memo Hayt/SAPUICE/7286/24 Jun 65

Dear [redacted]

This is in reply to your letter concerning unidentified flying objects.

I am enclosing the current report on Project Blue Book, the Air Force Project on unidentified flying objects. This report will show, to date we have received no evidence which indicates the existence of "flying saucers". However, should you still desire to submit your report to us after reading this, it will not be necessary to have a translation made by the Ambassador in France.
