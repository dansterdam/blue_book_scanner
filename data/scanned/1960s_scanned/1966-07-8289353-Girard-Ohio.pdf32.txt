The emotional impression which I received from the Observer, and the Commander in the space craft were quite different from the L. of the young American pilot in the jet fighter. I don't believe that I have ever had an emotional impression hit me so hard (though extra sensory perception) as did this emotional impression, which I received from the Observer in the space craft.
I received an impression that the Observer was looking down at the ground through something. I believe that he was looking at the ground through a high powered telescope rather than through a window in the craft. I believe that the high powered telescope must have greatly increased the power of this emotional impression which descended, like something like turning up the volume on a radio until you can't handle stand the noise.
That shy amused, self satisfied expression of the observer, came through so overwhelmingly strong, that by given white head, I believe al— that the telescope magnified his head considerably.]
