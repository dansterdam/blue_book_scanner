OFFENSE: SUSPICIOUS FLAGS (UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECT)
CSI: 29059

DATE: 9-29-65

TIME: 1:00 P.M.

PLACE: RIO VISTA HIGH SCHOOL

At above time and date interviewed [redacted] and [redacted] in the office of the school superintendent MR. [redacted]. Both boys were emphatic in their stories regarding the object they had observed on the evening of 22 September. They both stated that this object gave off a red pulsing glow and that they had shot at it from a distance of about 100 feet and that each time they hit it, the object would give off a brighter glow. When the bullets struck this object, they sounded like they hit metal. Neither boy could give a definite description of this object other than that it appeared to be flat and about five feet across.

MR. [redacted] School Superintendent grudgingly admitted that he had gone out to where the object had last been sighted on the following night and apparently a large number of Rio Vista residents who had heard about this object were also at that location. MR. [redacted] stated that he saw this object but thought that it may have been the planet Venus on the horizon. His description of the object is the same as the one given by the boys. The object gives off a red pulsing glow, moves swiftly, then suddenly disappears. He states that possibly as many as four hundred people in Rio Vista had observed this object on this same night, September 23, 1965.

Police Chief Manuel Cardoza who was present stated that a woman in town belonged to some organization that investigates unidentified flying objects and claimed to have pictures of some airships that she has made.
