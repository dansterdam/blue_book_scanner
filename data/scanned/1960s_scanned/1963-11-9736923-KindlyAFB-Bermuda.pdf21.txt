Request for Information on UFO Sighting

Commander
Self ridge AFB, Michigan

1. We have received a report of a UFO sighting, by Deputy Sheriff
Robert Baker of Saint Clair County, Michigan, which took place on
the night of 12 Nov 63 in Port Huron, Michigan. This sighting was
reported to the USAF for investigation in accordance with AFR
200-2. However, various articles in the local newspapers reached
Congressman James Harvey and he requested an evaluation of this
sighting. FTD Form 164 was sent to the principle witness for com-
pletion and the form was returned to FTD on 13 Dec 63.

2. On 19 Dec 63 Doctor J Allan Hynek, from Northwestern University
and consultant to the Air Force's project on UFO's, contacted Deputy
Baker by telephone. During this conversation it was determined that
the cash probably cause for the sighting was a helicopter. Deputy Baker
also indicated that the object he sighted had been observed on
succeeding days by various persons in area and their observations
were reported in the local newspapers. Deputy Baker indicated that
he would send copies of these newspaper items, however, as of this
date they have not been received by this office.

3. We are anxious to complete our evaluation of this case and re-
quest our findings to Congressman Harvey through his USAF (SAFOI-PS).
Although there has been a delay of more than two months in request
that an effort be made to identify a specific or any possible
helicopter activity in this area during the reported period of the
sighting.

4. We are attaching copies of pertinent correspondence.
