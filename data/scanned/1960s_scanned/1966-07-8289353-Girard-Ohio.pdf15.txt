Exhaust was silhouetted against the sky. Big bell shaped coming around and a dark streak through the center. Perfect silhouette against the sky. One penny would cover it.

One thinks it takes a larger object to cover it when its low.

When it gets high in the sky you don't need anything so big.

There was this white with a dark streak. Pretty sure of the exhaust size. Can compare it with the moon. Looking straight East and off to the right saw a light coming from south sky and steadily coming closer. This object passed in back of a street light. Then I could see what was coming. Could see the body shape then. It was at 30 deg first then at a 45 deg angle. No sound. Have heard helicopters, jets, aircraft, etc before. Just no sound. Maybe if it was closer I would have heard a noise. A piper cub at that distance would have made a noise. No sound on this. Strange thing when it came directly in front of me at a 45 deg. the exhaust went off like a light switch. Then when it came back on 5 seconds later it was just like somebody had turned on a light switch. No change in the speed. For the time viewed I had a watch on but I was so awe struck, just froze. I saw aircraft overseas. I just turned my head. I was so awe struck. Didn't have light enough to look at my watch. I retired it later. Object didn't disappear. High trees 150 ft high blocked it off. I froze so that I didn't move, guess if I would have moved I could have saw it again but I just froze. Estimated speed of 300mph. Went considerably slower than a jet. Similar to a prop plane. It was slow and low. Think it was doing some observing.

I feel that the 1000 ft alt is about 50% accurate. Not real high, possibly 2000 ft alt. Everything was so plain. Don't think it was real low
