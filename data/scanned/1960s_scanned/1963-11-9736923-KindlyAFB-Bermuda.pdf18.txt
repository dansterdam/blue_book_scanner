Dr. J. A. Hynek
Dearborn Observatory
Evanston, Ill.

Dear Sir:

Enclosed are the clippings that have appeared in The Times Herald in regards to the strange lights which have been sighted by reputable persons in St. Clair County.

These sightings have occurred periodically since Nov. 12.

To date there has been no explainable reason given for these mysterious objects.

Our interest is just as great as yours, and if possible I would like to be informed of your studies and findings regarding these sightings and other data that you have investigated concerning this matter.

The Times Herald will be glad to assist you wherever possible and for further help or assistance do not hesitate to call upon us.

Sincerely

[handwritten: John F. Brown signature]

John F. Brown
Reporter
