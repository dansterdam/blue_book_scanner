10. BRIEF SUMMARY OF SIGHTING
Series of blue flashes spaced about 3 seconds apart observed to the right of overhead moving toward 3 o'clock and the right extremity of the horizon. No. 15 or 20 flashes. Flashes similar to a flashbulb. No sound. Stars visible with scattered clouds. Flashes evenly spaced.

11. COMMENTS
Check with WP Operations revealed that a T-33 from Minneapolis with Home Base at Maxwell landed at 2223 (0323Z) and was over Xenia at the time in a normal landing pattern. No a/c was noted by the witness. Since the witness did not observe any other object it is assumed that this a/c is the cause of the report.
