APRO-May, 1963
ARGENTINA-BUENOS AIRES-12/23/62..12 PM.
A glowing football-shaped object sat down
on the runway at the Ezeiza Intl Air-
port. Mr Alora, a technician, and Ba-
sutti, tower operator, observed the land-
ing of the unconventional aerial object
from the control tower. The object marked
approx 1-1/5 mi from the tower. The wit-
nesses described the object as flowing
and football shaped.
At the time of the sighting, Alora
and Alora were controlling the landing of
a Panamerican DC-8, and paid close at-
tention to the object during the landing
of the plane. Visibility was excellent.
When the object left, it ascended verti-
cally at high speed, to an altitude of
1500'; then disappeared over the horizon
in level flight.
