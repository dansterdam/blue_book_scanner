Dear Sir;

My wife and I were in our back yard at 9:30 p.m. on August 5, 1968 waiting for the opening of the Echo Satellite which was due at 9:35 p.m. in the western horizon standing nearly South at 32°. (Aligning to B Surveyed.)

I had in my possession a pair of 10x50 binoculars. I saw an airplane in the north, however and decided to look at it, waiting had five more minutes before the satellite appeared.

While viewing this plane I immediately noticed what I first thought to be a star then it got brighter and showed in and directly direction, slowly at first and then decided to triple its speed. It soared in faster than the Echo Satellite I had I seen later and seemed to be of the sight in include. It traveled in straight line and about four seconds and then shot up like a single. The sight again dimmed]
