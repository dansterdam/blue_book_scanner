TITLE

Sighting of Unidentified Flying Object on 30 September 1949 at Honolulu, T.H. by 1st Lts FREDENDCK J. NORMAN and CLARENCE R. MARTIN

PERIOD 30 September; 1,2,3 and 4 October 1949

OFFICE OF ORIGIN STD (IG) PACD MATS, Hickam AFB

STATUS Closed

CHARACTER

SPECIAL INQUIRY - Unconventional Aircraft

REFERENCE

None. This is an initial report

SYNOPSIS

Inquiry requested by Major NOAH A. LEARY, Air Provost Marshal, 1200th Air Transport Wing, Hickam Air Force Base, based upon reports of 1st Lts FREDENDCK and MARTIN, of the sighting of a small, brilliant, white, round (on occasion, elliptical) shaped airborne object, floating high in the sky in a general northerly arc from Hickam Air Force Base at about 1700 hours, 30 September 1949. Check of local police, radar facilities, CAA and military flight activities negative. Object believed to be white colored radiosonde weather balloon released 1636 hours by US Weather Bureau station at Honolulu Airport, and which remained in flight for 48 minutes following the course described by the officers who made the initial report. AFOSI Letter No. 85, 12 August 1949, (Classified Confidential) has been utilized in compiling data.
