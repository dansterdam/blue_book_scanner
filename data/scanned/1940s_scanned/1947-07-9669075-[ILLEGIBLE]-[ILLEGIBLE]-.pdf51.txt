1. CFS. : 1920th A.F. SQ., FLD, ATLE, ATC. Harmon Field, Newfoundland.
2. Sighting : Flying Disc or other airborne object.
3. Place : Approx six (6) miles S/W of Harmon Field.
4. Time : 2200/5, 10 July 1947
5. Altitude : 2- 15,000 ft.
6. Weather : Clear. Scattered Cumulus 3 - 10, 000 ft.
7. Heading : NNE on horizontal course.
8. Speed : Very high velocity.
9. Shape & Size: Similar like a vessel, estimated to be same size as a C-54 as seen from 10, 000 ft.

10. Color : Translucent or Silvery, left a "bluish black trail approx 15 miles long".

11. Photographs: [redacted] reported that he took two (2) Kodachromes snapshots of the trail.

12. Reported By: [redacted] T. Mechanic; T. [redacted] PAA mechanic; and T. [redacted] PAA mechanic reported the above Facts to the Intelligence Officer on 15 July 1947. A flash report was forwarded to: Action now Commanding General, I.D., A.T.C., Attn. Asst. Chief of Staff, Intelligence. Info copies: Commanding Generals HQ AAF.

13. General : Visitor's [redacted] and [redacted] were enroute from Stephenvielle crossing to Harmon Field at the time of the sighting. They stated they were the first to see the Disc, it appeared to rear or split the clouds thru which it passed and left a bluish black trail approx. Fifteen (15) miles long behind it. The trail was similar to the afterglow of a powerful landing light or search light bean after it is suddenly switched off. The object maintained a straight horizontal course according to T. [redacted] and T. [redacted]. T. [redacted] stated it appeared to be on a great curved course on a horizontal plane. The object disappeared into the North Northeast. T. Leidy says he took two (2) Kodachrome photographs of the trail. The film has been transmitted to the Asst. Chief of Staff, Intelligence, Newfoundland Base Command for processing.
