4. REPORT OF FINDINGS

(1)     On 1 August 1949, the following article concerning the sighting of unconventional aircraft was published in the Ohio State Journal, Columbus, Ohio:

"Lmacs Ship over East Side"

"The sky over the east side of Columbus was full of strange stuff last night, according to reports to police, who interested themselves sufficiently to start investigation.

Army control, told police he saw a silver 'space ship' with red stripes and portholes hovering overhead between 7:15 and 7:30 P.M.

"Later, [redacted] told roller he had seen to 'flying saucs' in the sky. Pretty soon one light blinked off, then the other he said. The time? Between 7:15 and 7:30 P.M."

(2)     On 1 August 1949, [redacted], motor oiler, [redacted], Avenne, Columbus, Ohio, was interviewed, concerning a strange ship which he saw in the sky, and stated in substance:

On 31 July 1949, at approximately 1:15 p.m. guard, I saw an object in the sky, very high, at an altitude of about five thousand feet. The object was due west and was traveling in a westerly direction. The sky was generally clear, but there were a dull 'silver' elemental aspect in the vicinity of the object. The object was oval-shaped, provided bluish in color and about five times to size of the United States Navy airplane, the "Constitution." I did not see an exhaust trail, and I heard no sound. When I first saw the object, I called to a U[redacted] who was standing on his property at [redacted], talking to his sister, [redacted], Columbus, Ohio, and asked them if they could see the object.
