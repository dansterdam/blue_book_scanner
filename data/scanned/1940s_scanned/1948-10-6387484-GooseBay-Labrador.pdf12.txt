SUBJECT: Unidentified Flying Objects

TO    : Commanding General
        Air Materiel Command
        Wright-Patterson Air Force Base
        Dayton, Ohio
        ATTN: MCIAXO-3

1. Reference is made to letter from your headquarters, dated 9 March 1949, subject "Unidentified Flying Objects," and first indorsement thereto by this headquarters, dated 31 March 1949.

2. The incident summaries 173 through 233 have been screened against routine weather-balloon ascents made by the Air Force, Navy and Weather Bureau. Comments based on this review are inclosed.

3. It is recommended that the "Guide To Investigating Unidentified Aerial Objects" be charged as follows: Item 13, "Direction of Flight of Object," should be clarified so that vertical and semi-vertical motions are distinguished if possible, and so that action is specified at the direction towards which the object appears to move. "Relative to Radar Settings" should include a statement of the type of radar set used.

FOR THE CHIEF, AIR WEATHER SERVICE
