SYNOPSIS

Investigation in accordance with AFOSI Letter #35. At approximately 1015, 28 September 1949, a box-like object, without appendage, rectangular, metallic and silver in appearance, and the size of an airplane fuselage, was observed falling or diving slowly from considerable altitude over Winchenden, Mass. Descent was vertical, diving to near horizontal. Trail was from approximately Northwest to Southeast. Grey smoke trailed behind in rapid puffs. Object reportedly disappeared just above horizon line over water approximately ten (10) to fifteen (15) minutes after it was first observed in vicinity of Ashburnham, Mass., six (6) miles distant. No report or evidence of contact with earth obtained.
