SYNOPSIS

On 29 September 1949 at about 0605, Mr. [redacted]
[redacted], Jr., Humboldt, Nebraska, saw an unidentified object, size, height, speed, composition and propulsion unknown. It was oval shaped and apparently composed of luminous rings of some translucent material, moving southward in the sky near Humboldt, Nebraska. [redacted] first observed the phenomena when it was directly overhead and continued the observation until it faded from view. Object was first observed at 0605 and faded from view at 0620. Mrs. [redacted], wife of [redacted], verified sighting of object. Prevailing ground wind at the time was from south to north.
