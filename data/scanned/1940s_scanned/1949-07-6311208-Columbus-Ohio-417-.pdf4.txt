(3)     On 1 August 1949, [redacted], machinist, [redacted]
Columbus, Ohio, was interviewed concerning an unidentified object he
saw in the sky, and stated in substance:

At approximately 1207 hours, 21 Jul 1949, [redacted]
Columbus, Ohio, called to me and asked if I
could see a strange object in the sky. I looked to where he was
pointing, the west, and I saw the objects or lights. I cannot
judge distance in or in heavens, but the objects were very bright. They
were roundly, and approximately the size of floodlights and gave out
a brilliant light similar to white hot or solder metal. The sky
was generally clear, but there were some clouds in the west. The
objects or lights were traveling slowly in a southerly direction.
I heard no sound and did not see any exhaust trail or evidence of
locomotion. I have no further information concerning this phenomenon.
(U-C)
