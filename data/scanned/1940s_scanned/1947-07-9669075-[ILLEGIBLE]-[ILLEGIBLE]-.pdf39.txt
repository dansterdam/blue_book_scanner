On the evening of 10 July 1947 we were coming up over the mountain between here and Stephenville Colorado. The car was going up hill and we could see the sky through the windshield. He [illegible] said "Look at the cut in the sky". I looked and saw a bluish vapor trail. No clouds were scattered and very smooth 2000 ft 19,300 feet, the object passed through and cut the cloud leaving a gap where you could see the blue sky, like a knife had cut it. The edges were feathered similar to a wild, as if you cut a cloud in half. The estimate course of the object was approximately North North East. The trail was bluish black in colore, very easily distinguished from the blue sky. The trail was more or less a band across the sky in a straight path. There was no noise.
