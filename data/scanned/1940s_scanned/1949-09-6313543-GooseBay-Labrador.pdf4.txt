Major DRUM stated thereby that he did not observe any exhaust smoke, flame, vapor trails, or accompanying noises. The informant indicated that he was certain that the object was not a conventional type aircraft. At 1240 hours, on 19 October 1948, this Agent and 3/A RIDLEM began a visual search of the sky area pointed out by Major DRUM. This search from the lawn of Major DRUM's residence, occasionally employing field glasses, was constant until 1200 hours, when solid, low, cumulus clouds made further observation of the sky area impossible. During this period, nothing that night even resemble an airplane or "disc" was observed by these Agents.
