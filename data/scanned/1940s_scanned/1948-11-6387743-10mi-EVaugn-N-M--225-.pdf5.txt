COUNTRY OF INFORMATION

The following information has been received from the Fourth Army Intelligence Liaison Officer, Sandia Base, Albuquerque, New Mexico, dated 6 December 1948:

"Colonel [redacted], Inl., Executive, New Mexico Military District reports as follows:

"On 3 or 4 November 1948 at approximately 2350 hours, I observed a ball of light, distinctly white in color, I foot in diameter, falling vertically. The ball burst 100-300 feet from the ground in a spray of reddish solar which illuminated surrounding ground. This occurred in the area of the road on which [redacted] The location was approximately 10 miles east of Vaughn, New Mexico, on Highway 26. (S-2)

[redacted] "On 23 November 1948 at approximately 2130 hours, I was driving west on Highway 60, about 10 miles west of Vaughn, New Mexico. I observed a ball of light, somewhat vertically, which burst 100-300 feet from the ground. The conditions and appearance were the same as occurred on 3 - 4 November." (S-2)

"Colonel [redacted] stated that he did not report the incidents as he thought they might have been connected with star Air Force exercises. He further stated that he heard or saw no aircraft during either incident, which is understandable as he was travelling in a closed automobile. Weather conditions were clear." (S-2)
