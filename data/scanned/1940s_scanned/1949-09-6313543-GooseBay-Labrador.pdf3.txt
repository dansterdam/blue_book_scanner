a. Agent Report dated 20 October 1948, SUBJECT: "Flying Disc" by SI BARNE P. SCUTCHEY, 1st Counter Intelligence Unit, Pacific Air Command, AFO 953, as follows:

On 19 October 1948, this Agent, accompanied by Special Agent RIDLEY, drove to 2737 Pacific Heights Road, the Honolulu residence of Major ROBERT C. DRUM, AO-44323, Deputy Chief of Civilian Components, Pacific Air Command, AFO 953. This trip was made with the hopes of observing a similar incident observed by Major DRUM the previous afternoon from his home, that of "flying discs". Major Drum stated that on the afternoon of 18 October 1948, at approximately 1705 hours, while lying on the lawn with his 8 yr old daughter, he observed what appeared to be a strangely shaped, silver, glistening object high in the sky, in a northerly direction. The disc-shaped object was traveling in a northeasterly direction estimated at 10,000 feet altitude. At this point the flight of day, the Major stated, a status layer of clouds was situated above the flying object at an approximate altitude of 14,000 feet. The "discs" were moving below this layer of clouds. Because of lower broken cumulus clouds at a very low altitude, moving in a southwesterly direction, the Major was not certain whether he saw the same "disc" on four (4) different occasions or four (4) separate "discs". Covering a period of ten (10) minutes the "discs" (or disc) were observed each one for approximately ten (10) seconds. On first observing the strange objects, Major DRUM called it to the attention of his wife and 8 yr old daughter.
