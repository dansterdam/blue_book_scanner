LBF, TDEM, Evaluation of Photos

1st Ind (TDDPA/Mr. Davis/74136                                16 March 1964

TO: TDEM/Test Moody

1. This indorsement answers the letter request for evaluation received by this office on 5 March 1964.

2. The unavailability of the original negative has precluded an accurate evaluation of subject imagery. Possible causes of the image could include:

    a. Double exposure

    b. Circular objects cast before the camera during exposure.

    c. Placement of objects on printing paper during printing or enlarging process.

3. Please submit future photographic evaluation requests to TDDP on the standard FTD form 462, Engineering Support Work Request.
