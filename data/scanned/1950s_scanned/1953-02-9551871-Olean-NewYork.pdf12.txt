Report in accordance with par 7a, AFI 200-5, 27 Apr 52
A. Report number 1 was made on 11 Feb 53.

1. One object, blue-white in color, with no sound was sighted circling over CCC post. No information given as to shape, size, aerodynamic features, propulsion system, speed or manner of disappearance.

2. 0540Z to 0547Z. In sight for 7 minutes.

3. Visual from surface.

4. Observer was stationed at LI3505, in Olean, N.Y. The object was circling overhead at an altitude of from 6 to 7 thousand feet. No information given as to distance, direction or direction heading from observer was given.

5. SOURCE was WALDO BRADY, Olean, N.Y., telephone 22275. Mr. BRADY is a qualified ground observer. G-2.

6. No wind, one-third to one-quarter overcast.

7. Unknown.

8. None.

9. None.

10. None.

B. Report number 2 was made on 16 Feb 53.

1. One object, oblong in shape and a clear white color, was seen moving slowly and quietly in a straight line. It had no trail or exhaust or sound or any unusual features. It was much larger than a star (about 4 inches in length) and it disappeared behind a cloud.

2. Sighted 0120Z and in sight for 5 minutes.

3. Visual from surface.

4. Observer was located 4 miles south-west of [illegible], Erie Co. Pa., LL1955. Object was 6 miles south-west of observer bearing south-west, high.

5. SOURCE was [redacted], Corry, Pa. telephone Corry [redacted], [redacted] is Chief Observer of CCC Post LL223. G-2.

6. Weather was clear and cold.

7. Unknown.
