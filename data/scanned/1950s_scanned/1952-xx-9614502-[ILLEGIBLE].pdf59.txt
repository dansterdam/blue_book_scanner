SUMMARY: (Under enable summary of report. Give significance in final one-sentence paragraph. List indexees at lower left. Right half of report in AF Form 112—Part II.)

One oval object, described as being very bright, smooth, highly polished silver in color with a definite blue haze surrounding it for the first 25 - 30 minutes of sighting was observed from O'Hare Int'l Airport. Size estimated at 30 feet at 1200 feet distance. Object moved at a very high rate of speed and disappeared "like shutting off a light".

Two scientists consulted at Adler Planetarium, Chicago stated that object could not have been caused by temperature inversion.
