No Case (Information Only)                                2 December 1956
                                                                    San Luis Obispo, California


Case 278, San Luis Obispo, Cal., Dec. 2, 1956—Reported the San Diego Union, "Five mysterious lights were seen in the sky. One observer said they were too bright to be stars. Three policemen and a newspaper reporter watched the lights for 45 minutes and described them as having a pulsating red glow, then changing to blue and finally to white."

Ed. San Luis Obispo made note of a sighting which took place on the U.S. 101 south of the Martian in contact earthings from his association. According to that saucers did appear over the Obispo on the designated date, but authorities counterclaimed that the objects seen were balloons. However, the predicted radio contact never took place—and the affair was lagged a hoax by most civilian researchers. With so much emphasis put on San Luis Obispo, it seems in line that other sightings, such as the one of Dec. 2, should take place and be published.

2 Credit, J. W. Frazier, San Diego, Cal.
