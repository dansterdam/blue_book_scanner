IDENTIFYING INFORMATION: This phenomena was witnessed by two other observers at the same location as myself. They are [redacted] and [redacted] of Murray, Utah. I feel that my observations are fairly reliable, because I have studied and am interested in astronomy. I am a college graduate and familiar with the necessity of accurate data.

WEATHER CONDITIONS: The sky was cloudless. There was a full moon. It was an ideal night for observation of such a phenomena.

I would like to state that the object seen was not a batter. I am well acquainted with the appearance of a meteor.

One of the observers, Keith Horsgren described the phenomena as follows: As I watched the object it seemed to increase in brightness. It was the same color as a star shell. About 3 the size of the moon. When the brightness disappeared sparks seemed to fall and I watched them as they fell. I don't remember a tail.
