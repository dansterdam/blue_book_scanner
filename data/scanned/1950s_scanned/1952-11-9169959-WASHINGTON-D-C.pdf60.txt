At 2220 MST hrs on 11 Nov. 1952 I received a radio report from a patrol in the field of a flashing light which he believed was a UFO. Other reports were received, some of which stated the object was a star. Upon being relieved from duty at approx. 2300 MST, I came out of the building with Sgt [redacted] and we observed an object in the east flashing red & green. When I arrived at my home, I pointed this object to my wife and we both observed for a period of about 15 minutes. After this period of time, while my head was turned for a moment, the object disappeared and we did not sight it again before retiring, approx 30 minutes later.
