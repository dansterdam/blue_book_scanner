On 19 August 1953 at 2235 hours local time, [REDACTED] and [REDACTED] were looking for the planet Jupiter on the Pleydes object. They observed a reddish glow heading from the North to the South and parallel to Libby I-7, on the East side. Object was traveling at a high rate of speed and at a fairly high altitude. Both observers agreed that this object far exceeded the speed of any aircraft they had been in this area. Duration of the sighting lasted for approximately three seconds. Neither of the observers say or heard any aircraft in the area at the time of the sighting. There was no sound associated with this object nor did it appear to be a meteor or a star. Location of the observer at the time of sighting was in Satsuma, Alabama, latitude 30 degrees 31 minutes North, longitude 88 degrees 03 minutes West. Reliability C-.
