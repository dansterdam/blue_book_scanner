2nd Lt J. T. Ludlum Jr. Asst Intel Officer  Crew Personnel


AS PER T-1, 2 February 1953, two (2) cameras on a L-50 type aircraft observed
what appeared to be a silver ball in the wake of a L-50 type aircraft. They attempted
to obtain pictures of this ball with Scale U.S.A.F. camera but subsequent film assessment failed to show conclusive evidence of this object. This film from the gunnery
camera has been forwarded under separate cover to Chief, Air Technical Intelligence
Center, Wright-Patterson AFB, Dayton, Ohio. [handwritten: The film has just arrived at office.
