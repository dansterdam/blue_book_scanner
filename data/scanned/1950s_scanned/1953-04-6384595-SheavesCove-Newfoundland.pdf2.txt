1. Reference is made to an intelligence report dated 1-Apr-53, and to Incl 1 hereto, concerning a sighting of an unidentified flying object near Sheaves Cove, Newfoundland on 22-Mar-53. At that time, it was suggested that investigation be undertaken. Investigation has now been completed (Incl 2, hereto) and it is believed the object was identified as a weather balloon.

2. Inclosure 2 is a detailed report of investigation conducted by the Air Defense Command, through the 26th Air Defense Squadron. In brief, the conclusion reached is that the object sighted was a weather balloon which had drifted from the balloon release area. Further interrogation of the object has verified it.

3. It is noted that meteorological data and balloon release records have been checked and it has been established that a balloon was released from Goose [redacted] on 22 Mar 53 at [redacted] hours. The trajectory of this balloon, when computed, showed that it would have been in the vicinity of Sheaves Cove at approximately the time of the sighting. The balloon carried reflectors and this accounts for the radar contact and visual sighting.

4. There were no unusual happenings in the vicinity of the sighting which would indicate any unusual phenomenon. Interrogation of witnesses and a review of all available information leads to the conclusion that the object was a weather balloon from Goose [redacted] that had drifted in the upper atmosphere and was sighted near Sheaves Cove.
