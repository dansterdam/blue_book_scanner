STATEMENT

"On 29th of May 1952, climbing to 3,500 feet, enroute to Shina Range, on
a heading of 190 degrees, at approximately 1925 hours, the co-pilot of my crew
remarked that he saw an odd shape in the sky at about 4 o'clock.

"It was flying the aircraft and raised up out of the left seat to get a look
at what the co-pilot offered. To me, it looked like nothing more than a
dark cloud, round and towering. It was approximately 60 to 70 miles from us
reaching to about 15,000 feet.

"Since I was satisfied that is was only another cloud formation, I did
not deem it necessary to contact a GOI station and busied myself with flying
duties.

"I have enclosed a sketch of phenomena observed."

"I hereby certify that I have made the above statement, which I have re-
read for accuracy, in the 61st Troop Carrier Group Intelligence Office, AFO 704,
this 7th day of June 1952."
