Flight 2-D, 4602D AISS

COMMENTS of Preparing Officer:

1. In the opinion of the Preparing Officer, this sighting was probably caused by a meteorite for the following reasons:

a. All sources stated that the lights or objects sighted were round in shape.

b. Sources stated that the sizes of the lights or objects varied from that of a pinpoint to the size of a half dollar.

c. Colors of the lights or objects were yellow, red, white and olive green.

d. Speeds of the lights or objects were very fast, erratic and crossed large portions of the sky in a few seconds.

e. The formation was that of a small group when viewed through field glasses and when sighted visually, only one object was sighted.

f. To their knowledge, no sources observed a trail and no sound was present.

g. The time of any of the sightings did not exceed 90 seconds and the observation occurred at 0400Z 21 June 1955.

[handwritten signature]
DEAN S. JAMESON
1st Lt., USAF
Officer in Charge

COMMENTS of Approving Officer:

1. This headquarters does not concur with the conclusions of the Preparing Officer for the following reasons:

a. The sources' descriptions of the flight characteristics and flight path of the object do not compare with that of a meteorite.

b. The duration of sighting is too long for a meteorite.

c. Sources stated that they believed they saw the same object three separate times rather than three separate objects, with a time lapse between sightings.

d. One reliable source stated that the object was not a meteorite.

2. This report is forwarded to ATIC as unresolved.
