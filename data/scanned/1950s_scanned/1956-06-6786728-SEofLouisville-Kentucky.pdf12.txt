No Case (Information Only)                1 June 1956
                                                    Los Angeles, California


June 1--Los Angeles--A brilliant meteor-like object--"brighter
than Venus"--was noticed by local residents at 8:39 p.m.
A visiting amatuer astronomer, said it displayed "a red-
dish glow" and "seemed to have a tail."
The object seemed to brighten slightly before it terminated about
30 degrees above the horizon. he said. Other witnesses described
it as having "a bright blue tail that faded into blue, yellow and
red shades." (Los Angeles Examiner, 6/3.)
