THE KALISZEWSKI-MOORE STATEMENTS

A U.P. DISPATCH, DATELINE, MINNEAPOLIS, MINN., APRIL 13, 1951 QUOTED THE STATEMENTS OF TWO BALLOON EXPERTS. THE STORY WENT LIKE THIS: EX. KALISZEWSKI, SUPERVISOR OF BALLOON MANUFACTURE FOR GENERAL MILLS, CLAIMED TO HAVE SEEN SEVERAL HIGH-SPEED UNIDENTIFIED OBJECTS WHILE MAKING TWO OBSERVATION FLIGHTS OVER MINNEAPOLIS AND ST. CROIX, WIS. ON OCT 10 AND 11, 1951.

KALISZEWSKI WAS IN A PLANE DURING A BALLOON FLIGHT WHEN HE SAW THE OBJECTS. HE WENT UP TO STATE:

"I WE SAW NO VAPOR TRAIL, AND FROM PAST EXPERIENCE I KNOW THAT THIS THING WAS NOT A BALLOON, JUST CONVENTIONAL AIRCRAFT OR A CELESTIAL STAR..."

[ACTUAL QUOTE - EDS.]

INCIDENTLY, GENERAL MILLS, WORKING ON ARMED FORCES CONTRACTS, WAS AT THAT TIME ATTEMPTING TO CONSTRUCT A BALLOON CAPABLE OF RISING 100,000 FEET IN THE AIR WITH INSTRUMENTS SENSITIVE TO COSMIC RAY ACTIVITY. MOORE CONCLUDED WITH:

"SCIENTISTS DON'T HAVE ENOUGH DATA TO SAY MORE THAN THAT THE OBJECTS ARE OF UNDETERMINED ORIGIN."

KALISZEWSKI'S OWN WORDS WHEREIN HE SAY WHETHER THEY WERE SPACE SHIPS, FLYING SAUCERS OR WHATEVER ELSE I DON'T KNOW. I HAD NEVER SEEN THEM BEFORE. THEY WERE STRANGE, TERRIFYING? FAST. THEY HAD A PECULIAR GLOW."

GROUND CREWMEN AT THE GENERAL MILLS TRACKING STATION, DOUG SMITH AND RICHARD DOMBAIN, BOTH CONFIRMED SIGHTING THE OBJECTS [WHILE KALISZEWSKI WAS IN THE AIR] IN A THEODOLITE."

CHARLES B. MOORE, AN AERONAUTICAL ENGINEER IN CHARGE OF BALLOON OPERATIONS FOR GENERAL MILLS,

THE GOVERNMENT OUGHT TO STOP DECEIVING OR APPEARANCE OBSERVERS AND SET UP A 24-HOUR WATCH UNTIL THESE STRANGE OBJECTS ARE IDENTIFIED. THEY'VE BEEN SEEN AT LEAST 20 TIMES BY PERSONNEL WORKING WITH THE FIRM'S BALLOON OPERATION.

*A TELESCOPE-LIKE MEASURING DEVICE - EDS.

BUT I SUSPECT THAT SOME PRETTY INTERESTING THINGS WOULD TURN UP IF THE GOVERNMENT WOULD ONLY SET UP AN OBSERVATION SIGHT.

J.C. KALISZEWSKI AND C.B. MOORE WERE EXPERTS IN "THE FIELD OF BALLOON RESEARCH."

THE AIR FORCE'S BALLOON EXPLANATION WAS NOW A COMPLETE BUST!
