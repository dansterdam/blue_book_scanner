K. Position, title, and comments of preparing officer:

(1) Rudolph H. Pestalozzi, Captain, USAF, 231st Air Division Intelligence Officer.

(2) Comments:

(a) Mrs. [redacted] had heard of Unidentified Flying Objects and knew vaguely that the Air Force was interested in these reports.

(b) She had never before seen anything like this object. She was absolutely certain that this object was not an airplane and gas as certain that it was not a meteorite. The objects speed was too fast and silent to be an airplane and too slow to be that of a meteorite.

(c) Mrs. [redacted] appeared to be sincere in her effort to report factually and was convinced that what she saw does exist.

(d) She denied wearing glasses and stated that upon last examination her eyes were normal.

(e) 1 There were no other reports of UFOs at or near the time of Mrs. [redacted] observation.

2 Personnel in the George AFB Air Traffic Control Tower, 2½ miles north of observers position, saw nothing unusual that night and had no air traffic in their system for about two hours before or three hours after the sighting time.

3 The tower personnel reported no astronomical or meteorological phenomena that could account for this sighting.

(f) Mrs. [redacted] seemed to be a lucid, honest person. She firmly believes she saw what she reported and reported that she saw.

(g) There is no evidence, pro or con, other than Mrs. [redacted] word to substantiate this report.

l. Existance of physical evidence: none.
