On the 27th of April, 1952, there was an astronomy club meeting of an astronomy club of Furman University in Greenville, S. C. We were on the Campus between 9 and 10 PM talking with all the regulars about various astronomical observations, including that of Mars, Venus, Saturn, and Uranus. We had completed our study of Saturn. We next turned to the observation and study of Saturn. We found the atmosphere to be quite thick, and the markings to be clear and distinct on Saturn and in rings. I got the idea from what we had seen that it had been able to work normally.

At approximately 10:35 P.M. one of the group called attention to an object passing through the Zenith.

This intrigued us to be quite out of the illuminated of the color of reddish yellow. It seemed like some distance away. The object moved upward through and the light from the object in form. I had advanced to the extreme closed it went up at an angle of 45 degrees up and to the right. I would say it had some form of deviation off which included a certain amount of descent. When I further questioned those of higher authority there about the incident I got a hazy reply. I myself am just as puzzled as anybody else.

At 11:17 P.M. We saw several of the same type of objects.

From the City of Greenville, S. C. to points unknown in a Southwesterly direction and it seems of various sizes. These objects were very bright, and the most brilliant I ever saw. Of all the stars.
