11. On 8 March 1954, Captain ROBERT H. ZADNIK, AO 676110; Lieutenant PAUL R. FISHER, AO 2223179; and Lieutenant GEORGE A. KERR,
AO 2223206; Pilots, 301st Air Refueling Squadron, 301st Bomb Wing,
crew of AP AC-97 #6529, during evening of 5 March 1954, furnished the
writer with the following information concerning their observations:
While under GCA control, on downwind leg, at approximately 1900 hours, they observed a white light at approximately the same altitude and to the left of their course. The light appeared to be moving in a westerly direction and crossed their flight path. As the light crossed their flight path, they observed, the one (1) light house (2) lights.
They reported the incident to GCA and completed landing. No further
observations made.

The above crew stated that they believed the lights to be
jet aircraft in formation flight. However, they could not furnish
any identifying data as to size, shape, speed, etc. No aircraft running lights were noted.

AT SIDI SLIMANE AIR BASE, FRENCH MOROCCO, AFO 117, USAF

12. On 8 March 1954, Captain WILLIAM T. MCBRIDE, AO 823907, 3906th
Air Base Group, AFO 117, USAF, Pilot of USAF C-47 #5289, 5 March 1954,
advised S/I HUNGERFORD that the unidentified object near Nouasseur
Noncommission Tower had been misidentified as USAF C-47 #2844.
MCBRIDE stated that no other observations were made.
