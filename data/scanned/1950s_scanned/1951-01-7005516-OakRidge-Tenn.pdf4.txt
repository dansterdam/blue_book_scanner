TENNESSEE
(15 & 21 January 1951)

SUMMARY OF INFORMATION

(Refer S/I Subject as above dated 20 January 1951 pertaining to the sighting at 2345 hours on 15 January 1951)

At approximately 1945 hours on 15 January 1951, an unusually brilliant object was sighted by five persons eating in the "Control Zone" of the Atomic Energy Project, a utility plant, Tennessee. Attached hereto is the statement concerning this phenomenon. The weather at the time of this sighting is substantially the same as reported in the previous report for 15 January 1951, as observed to above.

Because of the 653rd A C & W Squadron, (Radar), McHess (sic) town airport, Knoxville, Tennessee overall report that no aircraft were in the vicinity at the time of the observation, and the radar site was receiving no interference.

At 1220 hours on 21 January 1951, a radar sighting of an unidentified object over Oak Ridge, Tennessee was made by the airborne radar equipment installed in an F-82 fighter aircraft of the 319 th Fighter Squadron, 52nd Air Force Group, Lockes Tyson, airport, Knoxville, Tennessee. The radar interference of this target from various angles and found that the source of the interference originated from the area of the X-10 Plant. No visual reference to anything in the sky was reported by either the pilot or the radar operator of the F-82 aircraft.

The weather at the time of this incident is reportedly: "Partly cloudy, with fast winds at seven (7) miles per hour. Temperature 53 degrees, and dewpoint 19 degrees. No billows or other weather apparatus was in the sky at the time. Between the hours 1945 and 1315, the wind was variable from south to northeast at from calm to eleven (11) miles per hour.
