Subject: Unidentified Flying Objects

(a) Other pertinent or unusual features. Objects Nos.
1 and 2 appeared to be illuminated with a white
light. Objects Nos. 3 and 4 appeared dark with
only white side lights showing, but would intermittently light up a bluish-purple color. Size
and shape of Objects Nos. 3 and 4 were determined
during periods of illumination in bluish-purple
light.

b. Description of course of objects.

(1) What first called the attention of observers to
the objects: Objects first observed while looking
from car window.

(2) Angle of elevation and azimuth of objects when first
observed. Objects Nos. 1 and 2 appeared to be approximately ten (10) feet above the horizon. Objects Nos.
3 and 4 appeared to be an undetermined height, a considerable distance above the horizon.

(3) Angle of elevation and azimuth of objects upon disappearance. Objects Nos. 1 and 2 appeared to ascend
slightly and disappear from sight. Objects Nos. 3
and 4 began a southward ascending movement and went
out of sight in approximately ten seconds.

(4) Manner of disappearance. Objects Nos. 3 and 4 disappeared from sight due to height and distance.

(5) Length of time in sight. Objects Nos. 1 observed
approximately five to six seconds, then disappeared.
Objects Nos. 1 and 2 then appeared for approximately
ten seconds. Object No. 3 appeared and was observed
for approximately five minutes. Object No. 4 appeared
to move into sight along side No. 3, moving in a
westerly direction.

(6) Manner of observation. Observation visual from ground
level. No electronic equipment or optical equipment
used.

c. Time and date of sighting.

(1) Approximately 2245 hours, 22 May 1955 to 0030 hours,
23 May 1955.

(2) Light conditions. Night.
