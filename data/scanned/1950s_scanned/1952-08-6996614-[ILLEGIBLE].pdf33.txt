Mrs. [redacted], Middletown, Pennsylvania, reported seeing an unidentified flying object on 23 August at 0235Z.

Mrs. [redacted], an employee of this Base, sighted a light described as a "protioscope" northeast of Middletown. At first the light was stationary, but later it "moved in a circle." The observation continued for ten minutes. No sound or exhaust was noted. The sighting was verified by Mrs. Kersey's husband.

Weather at the time was clear with 15 miles visibility.

Mrs. [redacted] was unavailable for further comment, and the lack of a more detailed description makes the report of dubious value.
