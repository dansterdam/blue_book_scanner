TITLE
SIGHTING OF EIGHT (8) UNCONVENTIONAL TYPE AIRCRAFT IN VICINITY OF LAMELSY AFB, MOROCCO, BY TWO (2) PILOTS, PAN AMERICAN WORLD AIRWAYS 2012 HOURS, 27 JULY 1952

CHARACTER
SPECIAL INQUIRY

REFERENCE
AFCOI 1st No. OF, dtd 24 Oct 1950

SYNOPSIS

Investigation initiated by District Commander, 7th OSI District (IN), USAF, Knochill Air Force Base, Tampa, Florida, upon receipt of information from Commanding Officer, USAF Troop Carrier Wing, Kindel International Airport, Miami, Florida, that two (2) pilots of Pan American World Airways had sighted eight (8) unconventional type aircraft. First Pilot [redacted], Biscayne Rd., Florida, and Co-Pilot [redacted], Miami, Florida, both employed by Pan American World Airways, in DCM number 2301, en route from New York City, New York, to Miami, Florida, observed one (1) formation of six (6) unconventional aircraft and two (2) unconventional type aircraft which joined original formation at 2012 hours (FST), 11 July 1952. Pan American Airways DCM, at 8030 feet, 21 miles north of Norfolk, Virginia, flying true heading 200 degrees. Unconventional aircraft originally observed ten (10) miles out at 2000 feet with sixty (60) degrees true heading. Continued course to approximately under D-445 varied ninety (90) degrees than recorded and changed direction instantly by one-hundredth (1/100) degrees with true heading two-hundred-seventy (270) degrees and were climbing when they disappeared. Approximate speed estimated 1000 plus miles per hour. Objects had a brilliant radish amber glow, were circular in shape with estimated diameter of one-hundred (100) feet and thickness of ten (10) feet. Length of sighting time approximately fifteen (15) seconds.
