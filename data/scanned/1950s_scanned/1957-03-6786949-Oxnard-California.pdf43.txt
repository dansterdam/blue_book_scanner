Dear Colonel Carey:

As the Director of the National Investigations Committee
on Aerial Phenomena, I am writing to you in regards to a
UFO sighting near Oxnard Air Force Base on March 23. We
have been reliably informed that four UFOs were tracked
by radar in the area, shortly after midnight on March
23. The indicated speeds were as high as 3500 mph.

We also have been informed that an air base jet fighter
pilot under some condition was vectored to intercept the
object. What he sighted gave the base operations and in-
terception personnel a detailed description of the in-
tense red light.

It is requested that since a copy of this report that eight or more
sightings have been immediately thereafter and that we have
been asked to contact, available and furnish authentic
information on the subject of unidentified flying objects
and we believe the March 23rd report to be in the public
domain.
