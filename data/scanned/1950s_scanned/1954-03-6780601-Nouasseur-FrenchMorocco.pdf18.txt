SUBJECT

SUMMARY (Brief concise summary of report. Give significance to find non-routine paragraphs. List indexers at lower left. Apply list of report on AF Form 112a.)

1. At 0935E, 12 March 1954, 1st Lt. Robert Johnson, 397th Fighter-Interceptor Squadron, while airborne at approximately 30,000 feet in the approximate area of 33°31'N - 07°11'W, flying on a heading of 010 degrees. The object was estimated as being approximately 2000 feet below his aircraft's altitude "N" and was described as having generally the same size of a fighter jet aircraft but was observed without wing tanks or visible wings. At approximately 370 mph at the time of first sighting the object. Lt. Johnson increased the speed of his aircraft to approximately 530 mph but did not overtake the object. Although the object was directly following the object, Lt. called stated that the object simply disappeared from his sight after approximately thirty seconds. The weather at the time of the sighting was clear above 22,000 feet.

2. The AGEM site at Mediouna was contacted for information concerning radar targets noted prior to the reported sighting with negative results. Captain Frederick Flam, element leader of Lt. Johnson's flight, sighted the object with Lt. Johnson after the object disappeared but with negative results.
