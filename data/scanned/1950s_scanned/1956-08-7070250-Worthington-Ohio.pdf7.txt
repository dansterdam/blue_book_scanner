Case 205, Cincinnati, Ohio, 10:05 p.m., Aug. 30, 1956—
A prominent lawyer phoned the writer describing a single
white light zigzagging to the north as though it were
"following a low flight heavenly saw". Its flight was jerky, he said,
like a "boat being rowed in thrusts upon the water" and
rythmically it flickered off and on—making no sound.
The object disappeared into infinity. . . . .
