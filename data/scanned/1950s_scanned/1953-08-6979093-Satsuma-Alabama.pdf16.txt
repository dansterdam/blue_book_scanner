DETAILS: At approximately 1900 CST, 26 August 1953, the 1702d Air Intelligence Officer was notified by the Ninth Intelligence Officer at the report of five separate sightings and sightings observed by Mrs. C. O. Matlock, R. Pell and Roy Pustley, and Mrs. Willis Hughes. The dates of the sightings were from 31 July 1953 through 17 August 1953, inclusive.

The Intelligence Officer of the 1702d AFT conducted an investigation and obtained the following statements from the observers:

On 31 July 1953 at approximately 2000-2030 hours local time, Mrs. Norman O. Listor, a grocer in Oneala, Alabama, was on his way home from Oneala Oneonta on July 31 when she sighted the flying object. The object crossed two highways and disappeared to the Northeast. The object was trailing a cone of approx. of a relatively high altitude. The object, a round object of minor and duration of sighting was for only a few seconds. No sounds associated with the glow and did not appear to resemble a motor or a shell and the tower location of the object was from the North of Oneala, Alabama, Latitude 30 degrees 54 minutes North, Longitude 85 degrees 02 minutes West. Reliability: E-3.

On 1 August 1953 at 2030 hours local time, Mrs. Ada Belle Lister was getting into her vehicle when she sighted the flying object. This object was on the West side of Ivy Heading from the Northeast to the Southwest and it appeared. She could not estimate the altitude other than stating that it was at a fairly high altitude and that the object was not too far away at the time she was standing. She stated that the object required to place a pointed edge in the leading part and a dull amber color in the remaining part. Light did not resemble a motor or a engine type and no sound was associated with the light. Duration of the sighting. The exact location of the observer at the time of the sighting was in Oneala, Alabama, Latitude 30 degrees 56 minutes North, Longitude 85 degrees 02 minutes West. Reliability: E-3.

On 2 August 1953 at 0930 hours local time, Mrs. C. T. Mathison, Patriotis Preacher, was on his way home from Texila to Satsuma, Alabama when he sighted the flying object. It glowed an amber color overall with the brightest color on the leading edge which appeared to curve like the circumference of a basketball and gave the appearance blurred and turned toward the rear. The object was heading from the North to the South and level. E-3, parallel to lying E-2 on the East side, at a high altitude. Subject stated that he could not estimate altitude so he guessed that it was 6-5000 feet high. It appeared to be steady in size with no sounds associated or traveling at its true speed spread. Spread was estimated to be much faster than the jet aircraft used in this area. Individual stated he has knowledge of meteorology and that the object was neither a meteor nor a shooting star. Observer did not see any aircraft in the area at the time of the sightings. Location of the observer at the time of the sighting was at Greehlan Creek, approximately one mile South of Oneala, Alabama, Latitude 30 degrees 54 minutes North, Longitude 85 degrees 02 minutes West. Reliability: E-2.
