Following report is quoted in entirety from FEAF Bomber Command Daily

Summary for 30 January 1952 and covering operations during the night of 29 -
30 January. At [boxed: 0230Z] one unidentified object was observed by the G/O, left
scanner and tail gunner of aircraft No. 7232 at [boxed: 0L-4030]. The disc shaped

object, size estimated by observers as 3 ft. across, was first observed
parallel on course, at 8 o'clock level, a distance from B29 unknown. This

object remained with the B-29 for approximately five minutes. The object
appeared at 8 o'clock level going away from 29. B-29 was at 22,500 ft.,

heading 274°, 143 knot ground speed.

Object

At [boxed: 0232/T] one disc shaped object was observed, by the left and tail gunners
[handwritten: Spitz 313]
of aircraft [boxed: 7232], at [boxed: 00-0430]. The unidentified object was observed shortly

before receiving flak. The object approached the 29 from between 8 and 9
o'clock level then withdrew and disappeared at the same clock position.

The observers estimated the object to be approximately three feet across
when at its nearest point to the B-29. The object was observed for one minute
at which time the weather was CAVU. The object appeared to be spinning and
a color resembling the sun.
