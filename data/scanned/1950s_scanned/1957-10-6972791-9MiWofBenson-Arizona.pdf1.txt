10. BRIEF SUMMARY OF SIGHTING:
A group of civilians had set up a camera to view and photograph expected Russian satellite. Satellite was not sighted, but a bright object appeared over SE horizon and traveled rapidly across the sky disappearing over the NNW horizon. The object was brighter than any star, but less bright than Venus. The light exhibited a yellowish hue giving the impression of reflected sunlight. Three photos were taken.

11. COMMENTS:
Around this time artificial meteors were being shot into the upper atmosphere to heights of between 35 and 50 miles at which height they glowed brightly enough to be photographed.
