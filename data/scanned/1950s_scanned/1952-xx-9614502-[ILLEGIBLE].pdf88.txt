IR-J/46-52          2          2

1. An unidentified flying object was visually observed from the ground at the intersection of Stanbridge and Parkwest in Norwalk, California 25 July 1952. Object was sighted by two Charlotte Office employees.

2. Object was first believed to be a star. The object was round, very bright and motionless until 1005PST when it tilted or turned to one side and changed to a dull orange color. As the object turned or tilted, propulsion was applied and a puff of smoke or 6 times as large as the object appeared and the object shot ahead at a high rate of speed. It then climbed westerly for about 40 seconds after which it disappeared at a very high altitude.

3. Weather was clear, visibility 12 miles, winds aloft from 20,000 feet to 60,000 feet were approximately varying from 10 to 28 knots.

4. Witness: Mr. E---, an employee of the Sheriff's Office, Norwalk, California, and his partner, name unknown.

5. Air traffic in the area at time of sighting was two B-29's; AP 6765 and AF 6840. These two aircraft were at 17,000 feet, mission unknown.

6. Report was called to Los Angeles Control by the observer and was passed by that agency to March Flight Service Center via Plan G-Interphone at approximately 2130 PST 25 July 1952.

Comments of Preparing Officer:

1. Insufficient background information on the primary witnesses is available to determine aerial observer qualifications. Witnesses were not interrogated.
