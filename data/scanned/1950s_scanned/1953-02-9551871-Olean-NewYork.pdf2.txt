A. Report number 5 was received on 2, Feb 53.

1. One comma-shaped object, size unknown had sighted while moving up and down, headed north. It was turning from green to red to white in color. It had no trail or exhaust and made no sound. The propulsion system and manner of disappearance was unknown.

2. Time of sighting 0350Z. Length of time in sight unknown.

3. Visual from surface.

4. Observer was at NN27LL, object was northeast of observer, heading north. Distance, direction and altitude from observer unknown.

5. SOURCE was USNO AIRVD, Glenn, NY. Phone 6174. Wx power is a qualified ground observer at CCD Post USCO, phone (blank 26179), S-2

6. None.

7. None.

8. Mr. (name LIAR), Glenn Time-world was taking pictures of the object at the time of sighting. He was advised by the filter center to pass negatives to 3rd ARDC.

9. Senior Rad was scrambled and directed to the area. No contact was made or any unusual sightings reported.

10. None.

F. Report number 6 was made on 24 Feb 53.

1. One disc-shaped object, silver in coloring sighted moving very fast. It made no sound and moved up and down and back and forth. No information as to aero-dynamic features, propulsion, system or other unusual features was given.

2. Observer was at NN350L, Glean, NY. Object was 6 miles west of observer, one-half mile high, heading east.

3. Time of sighting was 0312Z. Length of time in sight unknown.

4. Manner of observation was visual from surface.

5. SOURCE was DILLS PHARMACY, phone Glenn 783, Glen, NY. Dr. [illegible] is a qualified ground observer at CCD Post Olean, Glenn, NY, phone 2669, O-2

6. Weather consisted of Ground Fog.

7. Unknown.

8. None.

9. None.
