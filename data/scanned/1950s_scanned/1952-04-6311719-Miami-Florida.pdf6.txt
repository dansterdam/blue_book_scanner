The report contained herein is an intelligence debriefing of combat crew returnees from Korean Combat Theatre as set forth in USAF Debriefing Requirements ltr hq USAF AFOIN-C/CC-9 dtd 19 April 1952. Salient features of this report are namely:
(1) One encounter with unidentified enemy fighter. (2) Encounter with enemy anti-aircraft fire on missions on primary target to include one encounter with guided rockets. (3) Observations of unidentified flying objects at Haha AFB and Kadena AFB, Okinawa, and (4) Recommendations and comments USAF equipment procedures and training.
