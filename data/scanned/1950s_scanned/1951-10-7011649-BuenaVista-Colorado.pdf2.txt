SUBJECT: Sighting of Unidentified Object C/R, 28 Oct 51

DATE REPORTED ON: Colorado | FROM (Agency): HQ Air Defense Comd, Ent AFB, Colorado Springs, Colorado

DATE OF REPORT: 14 Nov 1951 | DATE OF INFORMATION (EVALUATION): 10 Nov 1951

PREPARED BY (Officer): T. Fredenburgh, Major, USAF | SOURCE: [illegible], Major, USAF, 21031 Air Weather Gro, Peterson Fld, Colo

REFERENCES (Control number, direction, previous report, etc., as applicable):

SUMMARY: (Concise summary of report. Give significance in final one-sentence paragraph. List inclosures at lower left. Begin text of report on AF Form 112—Part II.)

A greenish-blue brilliant light with an iridescent glow in the form of a tail lasting about 15-20 seconds, traveling North to Southwest in a descending curve at an angle of about 30° was seen on/or about 2000 - 2030 hours, 20 October 1951, approximately seven (7) miles from Buena Vista, Colorado.

The observer is a rated pilot of the Weather Squadron indicated above. His recital was simple, direct and complete as to details.
