Statesman of [illegible], [illegible] This is a debriefing, Ellington
Air Force Base, Houston, Texas.

I was with [illegible] outside the orderly room of the High [illegible] Squadron, When
this thing passed nearly overhead. It was round, appeared slightly luminous
and was about [illegible] feet in diameter, although a lot more yellow. It moved at first in
a harbor, very fast from a south to a northly direction. There was a exhaust
trail or tail as there usually is with a meteor. Then it became erratic and no features or characteristics of modern
aircraft were visible. It traveled at a terrific speed, until direct over us,
then slowed almost to a stop. It then assumed a pattern of erratic-ring at a
terrific rate, slowing almost to a halt and then shooting off at angles almost
perpendicular to its previous course north to south course. It then in circling
it around, It landed to keep its same Northerly direction. It continued this
course for some time, and as it appeared to be moving directly away from us,
with out and aft the from and maneuvered at the tremendous height it would
be traveling with each it and, if it were so high it tremendously. It appeared
to be high in the stars. It then ascended and began whirling around in
at about 2 secondes intervals. We counted aloud to check the intervals. Instead
of coming passed the horizon like a meteor or smaller thing I continued to ascend
finally fading from sight. I observed this on the 25 Aug at 0110 hours.
