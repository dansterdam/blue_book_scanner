1. The following is an extract from the 1975-1 AACS Daily Report of Controller dated 3 March 1954 which was supervised by A/C, 11235276, 1975-1 AACS Detachment, Rouasseur Air Depot:

"1918 - AF 6537 reports unidentified A/C or objects making passes at his, RD-77's position was 1500 ft, 3-4 mi SW of station.

"1938 - AF 6529 reports likewise; Unid targets were on West Heading approx 1500 ft, 6-8 mi E of Sta. No incident.

"1945 - AF 6589 rpts again; target was 5 mi SW of station approx 1500 ft, believed to be two or more A/C on passes; target disappeared SW of station; he was unable to ident type, etc. but believes them to be conventional type A/C...all says no visible type, call all can't pin on anything as occur.

"2010 - AF pilot reported as stated, C-47; Rpts he says objects about same locale as 07's but there were no indication; very quick target sighted to be approx 300 ft alt, at his let's side one Maj A/C...still nothing on acreen. "Later - D-47 at 1900-30 on 2/10 at 20 min heard for objects but could see nothing; all A/C mentioned were on ODA appr at the time.

"2155 - AF 2684, C-47: rptd similar sighting...a bright light similar to A/C large in size & pos of A/C oblige an A/C and then either sent off or sent behind cloud...VT carrying clear slotn; also in same vicinity...said this to point them aspp'd up again and ltd and then A/C lost sight of it.

"2223 - Randall Ops says they have target about 10,000 ft on hdng of 180 fm their station and about 25 miles out. Target appears stationary. ODA can see nothing. Contact with RO".

"2243 - Randall new rpts tgt's at 10,000 ft, 12-14 at South.

"2306 - Randall new rpts tgt's at 15,000 ft, 15 mi South, brng 180 degs.

"2310 - ODA rpts 3 tgt's, smaller than a C-47 tgt, NW of station, 3-6 mi, all 2,000 or thereabouts degs.

"2315 - ODA has two more tgt's now same locale on hdng of 255 degs, all moving very slowly.

"2334 - Randall has two tgt's, 2 miles apart, brng 180 degs, 22 nsmt miles, 10,000 ft. Tgt's very alternately in intensity, one bright and one weak, not very large.

"0015 - ...Previous logs being reviewed by BASES regarding 'Flying Saucer' sightings.

"0035 - Randall spotted target 25 miles North of Station.

"0012 - (late Entry/TM) 3 targets sighted duration 15 minutes one. AS 23216.
