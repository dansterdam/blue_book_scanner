In reply to your inquiry we inform you that heretofore we have not observed any "flying saucers" in this country. However, Basilea is negative laboration, we are able to collaborate on drifting with you and post lists on all original observations.

Only last summer, I received an excited telephone call in the middle of the night reporting an unfamiliar object rising on the eastern sky with increasing brightness and a remarkable change in color from red to white at a slowly visible altitude not very high above ground. The observer insisted on the position so it could be traced unmistakably. By an immediate check I identified the mysterious thing as the rising planet Jupiter. Its bright ness at such intensity in spite in its glide not being observable. However, the speed of this motion is overestimated particularly by the layman who believes that the celestial bodies are standing still or moving so slowly that one cannot discern any motion at all.

Another time, a great noise suddenly arose in front of the astronomical observa- tory a "flying saucer". We immediately looked out and saw a IILE of a luminous body flying along the southern horizon. It could immediately be identified through a former AA telescope; it was a glider in tow, the wings of which bore the words "Breguet - Frelette" in luminous letters.

The third time, it was an airliner flying at a relatively high altitude whose essentially weaker brightness was then ques- through the haze directly over the town, produced a spot somewhat weakened in color with a dirty red rim. Thus, we were able to clarify all reports given to us in a very sober manner and, based on these experiences, we are inclined to believe in significantly serious explanations for all of our manifold observations of all reports on flying saucers.
