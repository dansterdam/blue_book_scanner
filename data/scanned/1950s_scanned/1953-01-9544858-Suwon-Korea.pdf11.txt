SUMMARY (What observer assumed he thought, what is usually possible. Give observer's description, estimated size of objects, speed and direction, time of observation, altitude or bearing from observer, etc.):

On 24 January 1953, USAF, in the vicinity of F-13, Suwon, Korea, source observed two (2) unidentified flying objects for a period of three (3) to five (5) seconds.

Source stated objects were round, approximately twelve (12) to fourteen (14) inches in diameter, slightly convex and glowing a whitish silver, flying at low level in a single file. Source was unable to estimate altitude. No propulsion sounds, trails or exhaust, or sounds were noted. Objects disappeared either into or behind clouds.

At the time of sighting, one element or two (2) F-86's and A in the flight pattern to the right of the observer and another aircraft of unidentified type was on his left at low altitude. No unusual meteorological conditions prevailed at time of sighting.
