In accordance with letter, Hq USAF, AFOIN-C/OC-2, subject: Reporting of Information on Unidentified Flying Objects, dated 19 Dec 51, the following information is submitted concerning an observation of an unidentified flying object. Information in sub-paragraphs below is in the same order as par 4d of referenced letter.

a. A bright light was observed coming from the south-west to the north. This was the first reported sighting. A short time later two lights were seen in an oval flight pattern from south to north. All observers stated that objects disappeared to the south-west or south. No sound was heard at anytime. One observer stated that the light varied from a dull red to very bright. No estimate of size was given except that light was larger than usual star. One observer estimated the speed as 900 miles per hour. All other observers just said very fast.

b. Objects were sighted from 2345 26 February to 0100 27 February 1953.

c. Objects were observed visually from the ground.

d. Observations were made from Brooks Air Force Base. Altitude was stated as very high. No estimate in feet was made.

e. Objects were observed by 1st Lt [REDACTED], S/Sgt [REDACTED], S/Sgt [REDACTED], S/Sgt [REDACTED], A/1c [REDACTED], A/2c [REDACTED], A/2c [REDACTED], S/Sgt [REDACTED]. Reliability of their statements (attached) is unknown.

f. Brooks AFB 2300-0100 hours weather sequence [illegible]:
015 135/55/47[?]/7/008
015 172/54/49/5/600

f. There appeared to be no activity or condition, meteorological or otherwise, which might account for the sighting.

h. There is no physical evidence of the sighting.

i. There was no interception or identification as this headquarters was not notified at that time.
