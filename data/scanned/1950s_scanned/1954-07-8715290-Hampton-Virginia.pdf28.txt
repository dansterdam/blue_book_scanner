Miss [illegible], Chief Social Services at the Veteran's Administration in Hampton, Virginia reported to the Base Intelligence Officer that she and her parents sighted an unidentified flying object which appeared from an easterly 45° angle towards Geo Ref Coordinates CWPH 3840, paused about 10 seconds, then changed directions departing on a 50° angle in a north-westerly direction until it gradually disappeared. This incident occurred on Saturday, 31 July 1954, at 2115Z.
