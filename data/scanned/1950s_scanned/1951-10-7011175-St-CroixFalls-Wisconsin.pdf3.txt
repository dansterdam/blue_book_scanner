FOOTNOTE ON C.B. MOORE
PROJECT SAUCER CASE NO. 122

CHARLES B. MOORE, THIS SAME U.P. DISPATCH INFORMED US, WAS "THE MAN WHO FIRST GAVE SCIENTIFIC CREDENCE TO THE 'FLYING SAUCER' WHEN TESTING BALLOON TESTS 15 MILES SOUTH OF HOT SPRINGS, NEW MEXICO (57 MILES NORTHWEST OF THE WHITES SANDS PROVING GROUNDS). HE PICKED UP AN UNKNOWN AERIAL OBJECT ON HIS THEODOLITE.

[Comic-style illustration with speech bubbles containing:]
LOOK!
I GOT IT!
TRACK IT!

THE OBJECT WAS ELLIPTICAL IN SHAPE, ABOUT 100 FEET IN DIAMETER. IT CROSSED THE PATH OF THE BALLOON AT AN ALTITUDE OF OVER 50 MILES. IT WAS TRACKED AT 6 MILES PER SECOND...THAT'S 10,000 MILES PER HOUR.

THERE WERE FIVE TRAINED, HIGHLY-SKILLED, SCIENTISTS PRESENT. THE DATE WAS APRIL 14, 1949. THESE MEN WATCHED THE SAUCER ALTER ITS COURSE AND ZOOM UPWARD....

[Left illustration: Elongated oval object against textured background]

[Right illustration: Landscape scene with curved lines and circular elements]

CLOSE QUESTIONING OF MOORE AND THE OTHER SCIENTISTS BROUGHT OUT THESE FACTS:

[Speech bubbles in comic illustration showing conversation between two figures]

THE FORCE NECESSARY TO PRODUCE SUCH A MANEUVER WOULD BE EQUAL TO 20 G'S!

NO HUMAN BEINGS COULD STAND THAT!

WHITE SANDS, NEW MEXICO MIGHT BE A GOOD LOCATION FOR THE OBSERVATION SITES!

THE U.S. STORY CONCLUDES WITH THIS STATEMENT BY CHARLES B. MOORE...

[Illustration of man in profile]

DIDN'T THE AIR FORCE TAKE KALISZEWSKI'S AND MOORE'S ADVICE? DIDN'T IT ORDER 200 GRID CAMERAS TO BE SENT TO STRATEGIC POINTS, AIR BASES, A-PLANTS AND OTHER SPOTS...WHERE SAUCERS HAD BEEN SIGHTED?
