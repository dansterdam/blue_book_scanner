BEAT/Sgt Polley/da

1. Mr. [illegible] sightings were evaluated as "possible balloon."

2. There is a U.S. Weather Bureau Balloon-launching site in Sacramento which possibly launched a balloon on November 15, 1952 just prior to his sighting.

3. Re Mr. [illegible] on May 20, 1953, sighting in Los Angeles. He states this object descended and came to rest directly over his head at an altitude of five feet. We are sure Mr. White is at least five feet tall, give or take a couple of inches, in which case it would have been sitting on his head. The object remained motionless for about five minutes at this altitude. It was described as the exact size of a "night football." It must not have frightened him because it wasn't reported until the latter part of 1954.
