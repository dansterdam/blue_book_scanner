A RADIO REPORT FROM THE COAST GUARD CUTTER IN THE GULF OF MEXICO
SAID THE OBJECT SPOTTED BY ITS MEN "RESEMBLED A BRILLIANT PLANET"
THAT TRAVELED IN CONCENTRIC CIRCLES AROUND THE VESSEL.

RADAR CONTACT WAS RETAINED OFF AND ON FROM 9:10 A.M. UNTIL 5:37
A.M. THE OBJECT WAS SAID TO HAVE BEEN VISIBLE TO THE NAKED EYE FOR
ABOUT INSETS SECONDS DURING THAT TIME. THE COAST GUARD ALERTED OTHER
SHIPS IN THE AREA TO KEEP A WATCH FOR THE OBJECT.

OUR MEN ABOARD THE SHIP TALKED VIA MARINE PHONE AND RADIO WITH
COAST GUARD HEADQUARTERS IN NEW ORLEANS.

ENS. WAYNE SCHOOLEY, REDLANDS, CALIF., SAID HE SAW THE OBJECT
AT A DEGREE ELEVATION MOVING NORTH. HE SAID IT ENTERED A CLOUD BANK
AND DISAPPEARED.
