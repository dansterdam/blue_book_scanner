2. Dr. DOREM, Chief Astronomer of the Mt. Wilson Observatory was interviewed to speak on the possibility of meteors in the Hollywood and Riverside areas. Dr. Dorem explained that all of their observers are inside the observatories and the field of vision of their equipment is extremely limited. Therefore, the possibility of sighting such objects would be highly improbable. There were no sightings of meteors any time during the night of 3 July 1955 from Mt. Wilson or Palomar Observations, nor could any astronomical conclusions explain the three reported sightings in question.

3. George IKMUTH, Professor of Astronomy, San Bernardino Valley College, was interviewed to determine the possible conclusions of the sightings in this report. He could offer no valid as the information, in his opinion, was contradictory and unreliable in all three sightings with this exception: the moon was rising during the time period of these three sightings and a possible optical phenomena might explain the sighting reported by Mr. DUNN.
