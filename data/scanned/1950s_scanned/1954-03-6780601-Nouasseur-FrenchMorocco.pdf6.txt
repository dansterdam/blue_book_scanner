4. A/Sgt [redacted] 36th ACAW Sq, Nouasseur Air Depot, was interviewed and the following was revealed:

a. At approximately 2230S, 5 March 1954, Lt. [redacted] received a call from Operations (GCI) reporting unidentified targets at approximately 11 miles (11) north of Fez at 10,000 feet moving on a heading of 185 degrees. Lt. Savoy, Site Fl, reported noting moving in that area.

b. At 2330S, 5 March 1954, two (2) targets were reported; one would fade in and the other out.

c. Air Traffic Control, Gamse Aerodrme, stated no aircraft reported here.

d. Captain McBride, USAF Pilot of C-47 #5269 reported that he may be target in same location as C-47 but no incidents.

e. At 1928S and 1940S, 5 March 1954, Aircraft #6259 reported targets at 1500 feet but unable to identify; however it appeared the belief that aircraft were of conventional type.

f. Approximately 2000S, 5 March 1954, Houasseur based aircraft #6944, piloted by Major McCann and Major Fiedler, broke traffic to look for reported objects. Radar reported in area although civil aircraft the same time.

g. Approximately 2300S, 5 March 1954, GCI observed two or three targets smaller than C-47 aircraft. ACAW site could find nothing on their equipment at this time.

h. The Ninth site at Meknes Area (2) objects on equipment at approximately 2310S in another location. These objects appeared 0 and 7 March alike. On 7 March 1954, F-86 aircraft based on Nouasseur were scrambled to the reported location but no positive results.
