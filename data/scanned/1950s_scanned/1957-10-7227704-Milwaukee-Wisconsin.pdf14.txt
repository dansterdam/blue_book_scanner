DAYTON, OHIO]

Several Report Seeing
Red Satellite, Rocket

By KARL ABRAHAM
[Removed Herald Staff Writer]

The Soviet satellite and the third-stage rocket which precedes it by some 15 minutes were reported seen by some half dozen Daytonians as they passed directly down over at 25,000 feet. TUES. 22, '57

Reports of the sightings from 5:15 to 5:50 p.m. came both from adults and youngsters, all of whom claimed to have seen the flying objects with their naked eyes. The length of sightings ranged from three to 60 seconds. The object moved southwest to northeast.

The satellite came closest in Dayton at exactly 8:46.48 p.m., according to Lloyd Noel, director of research for the Bahco Glass Co.

Listening to sensitive radio equipment at his home at 514 East Whipp road, he fixed the distance from Dayton at that time at 418 miles.

Lloyd also reckoned the distance of the satellite by measuring the change in frequency of its radio transmissions as they approach and depart.

On the basis of these calculations he predicted the following times of passage for the satellite today: 2:08, 4:16 and 6:15 a.m., 2:08, 6:48 and 8:58 p.m.

Bock said the best time to sight the satellite, weather permitting, would be at 5:09 p.m. The rocket, somewhat more brilliant, usually passes from 10 to 20 minutes before the satellite or about 8:18 p.m.

Here's Noel's schedule for tomorrow: 1:25, 3:28, 4:39 and 6:10 a.m., and 1:25, 3:28, 4:39 and 6:10 p.m. He said that the satellite will pass close enough to Dayton to be visible. WED. 2/2 '57
