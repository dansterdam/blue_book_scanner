10. BRIEF SUMMARY OF SIGHTING
Had a more yellow light than a star would. Traced its movements roughly in a triangle. It moved from the spot near middle star to above 25d star & from there in a straight line to several degrees left of west star. At this time Sergeant Cook returned & I pointed it out to him. It had returned to near the pt where I first observed it. As we watched it began the same movements. Could not approximate distance. Stay motionless minutes at a time and suddenly start moving again. No sound. Still there when we returned to the base.

11. COMMENTS
Possible balloon or star, however, positional data of object and manner [handwritten: A/d] of disappearance not included. Insufficient data for a firm evaluation.
