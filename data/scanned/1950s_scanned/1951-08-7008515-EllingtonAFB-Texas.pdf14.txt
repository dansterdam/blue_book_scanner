Report of unidentified light, thought to be a Celestial Phenomenon, as seen by Sgt. Douglas Way, Pfc. John H. Gues, and Pfc. Richard Bains at about 0115, 26 Aug 1951.

COMMENTS of Reporting Officer:

1. The reliability of the information of this report can not be judged.

2. The fact the agreement on C.O. was made a witness to this sighting would add more weight to the statements of the other two informants.

3. For additional reports of this type for approximately same period see 1952-2051. Also, see 115-4-51 for similar sighting on a later date.
