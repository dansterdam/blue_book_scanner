SUMMARY: (Limit oneself to summary of report. Give significance in final one-sentence paragraph. List enclosures at lower left. Dsgn last of report on AF Form 112—Part II.)

There were 8 sightings of unidentified flying objects reported to this station through Department 1, 4673d Ground Observer Squadron, Buffalo Filter Center, during the period 11 Feb 53 to 1 Mar 53.

SOURCE of 7 reports was GCC Post 4030 at Olean, Cattaraugus County, New York (MJ205). Sightings were made in all but one case by qualified ground observers.

Source of one report was Corry Fld., GCC Post L1252. Sighting was made by qualified observer.
