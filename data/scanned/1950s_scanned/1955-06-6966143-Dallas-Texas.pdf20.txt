I. SOURCE: Mrs. [redacted]

Address: [redacted] Dallas, Texas

Age: 24

Occupation: Housewife

Education: 4 years college

Qualifications: Bacteriologist

II. RELIABILITY: SOURCE seemed to be a very intelligent young woman. She had done a great deal of research in the field of Bacteriology. SOURCE answered all questions in a pleasing conversational manner and without hesitation. Preparing officer believes the SOURCE to be reliable.

III. SOURCE'S DESCRIPTION OF THE SIGHTING:

1. At about 2150 CST 20 June 1955, SOURCE first observed the object. The object was in a south to north direction and appeared as a bright glow. SOURCE stated the object pursued a very irregular course and then turned right proceeding in a westerly direction. The object moved at a very rapid rate of speed, such faster than any jet aircraft SOURCE had ever seen. The object seemed to flow before fading out of sight. On the second sighting, the object was again in a south westerly direction, but was slightly more westerly than it had been before. On the third and fourth sightings, the object appeared in the east and disappeared in the north. The object appeared much closer to SOURCE on the third sighting than it did on any of the other sightings. SOURCE stated that the objects appeared to be solid and were not from falling objects.

2. SOURCE could not remember the exact times of the sightings.

3. SOURCE stated that she observed only one object.

4. Object was a brilliant white in color and object would turn red before disappearing.

5. SOURCE was looking to the southwest when the object was first sighted.

6. SOURCE had no conception of what the object might have been or what caused it.
