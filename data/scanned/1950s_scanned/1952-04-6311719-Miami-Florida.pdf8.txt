SUMMARY: (A one sentence summary of report. Give significance to final one-sentence paragraph. List enclosures at lower left. Legible type of report on AF Form 12 [if at all])

The report contained herein is an intelligence debriefing of combat crew returnees from Korean Combat Theatre as per North in the Debriefing Requirements for B-26 USAF AFOIN-C/CO-9 dtd 14 April 1952. Salient features of this report are namely:
(1) One encounter with unidentified enemy fighter. (2) Encounter with enemy anti-aircraft fire on missions on primary targets to include one encounter with unguided rockets. (3) Observations of unidentified flying objects at Kaho AFB and Kadena AFB, Okinawa, and (4) Recommendations and comments USAF equipment procedures and training.
