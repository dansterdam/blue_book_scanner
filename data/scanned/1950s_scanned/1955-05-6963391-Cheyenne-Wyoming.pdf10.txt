Cheyenne, Wyoming

29 May 1955

STATEMENT

Last night on the 22nd of May 1955 we were over to back to the base from Greely. We were on route 85. We was out of the about 10 miles from Cheyenne, Wyoming. While two of my friends started to Cheyenne for gas, [illegible] and I stayed in the car. It all started happening somewhere between 2315 and 0030. [illegible] said he saw something but didn't know what it was. I took a look out the window. It disappeared in a minute and then returned, only this time there were two of them. They were there for about two minutes, and then they disappeared completely.

A few minutes later there appeared in the sky moving objects, two of them. There were two lights one on each side of the moving objects. They started moving south, flying not too high. Every couple of minutes it would turn a bluish purple, almost as bright as a light. This is when I caught a glance as to what is was shaped like. I'll try and come as close as possible:

[Drawing of elongated oval/disc shape with horizontal lines]

She was in sight for about ten minutes, then it ascended almost straight up and toward the south.
