[handwritten: 7 NOV 52
ATLANTIC
CARIBBEAN SEA]

HOLOGRAPHIC BULLET 1-

Junior Third Officer F. A. Bennett of the American S. S. Santa Monica, Capt. H. MacDermid, Master, reports that on a voyage from Maracaibo to Barranquilla at 0057 G. M. T., November 7, 1952, in lat. 11°55' N., lon. 73°09' W., he observed a large, brilliant, green-hued meteor falling in an arc from approximately 30° altitude, bearing 160° to 20° altitude, bearing 200°. It was a very dark moonless night, as the sky was almost covered by thick cumuli.
