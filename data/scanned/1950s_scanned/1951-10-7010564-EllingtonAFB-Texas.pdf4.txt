CONCLUSION

Since Source states the object was traveling directly west, it

could not have been a balloon as it would have to be traveling into

the wind if it were above 5,000' or nearly into the wind at below

5,000'.

In addition, if a balloon were released at 1500 in the three hours

from launching until sighting it would have risen too high to be seen.
