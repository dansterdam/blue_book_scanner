11. BRIEF SUMMARY AND ANALYSIS:

Two round shaped glowing whitish silver objects traveled at a h'd rate of speed in a gentle climb disappearing into the clouds.

COMMENTS: Two jet a/c were flying in area at the time of sighting & it is fairly certain that observer saw these a/c when they were at a high alt.

A 2nd information was made of the observer & it was found his estimation of speed & distance was unreliable.

3. At high alt it is difficult to determine the shape of a/c.
