SUMMARY: Enter only summary of report. Give a preliminary paragraph. Lid inquiries if so per suit. Begin text of report on AF Form 112, Part II.

1. Transmitted herewith is preliminary information obtained from two German astrophysical observers as a result of letters written and sent to Dr. H. C. Urey of the German Space Travel Society, who is preparing a report on "unexplained aerial objects".

2. Part II of this report contains a translation of a letter from the Swabian Astronomical Observatory, Stuttgart, which gives explanations of several observations. A translation of a letter from the Observatory at Gomaringen, which also includes a brief description of a sighting near Comabringen, is also included in Part II.

3. Additional information from other institutions will be forwarded by later reports.

4. The information contained herein will be of interest to the Air Technical Intelligence Center, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio.
