                        AFBMD-S. Fridayb/D/2214/File 265

1. The objects sighted over Oklahoma City, Kansas on the night of 13 July 1956
   were reported to have D-2 investigation by a 1-W1. The crew reported one objects were
   sighted over a poultry oil field.

2. The objects sighted over Camarillo, California, toward AID drop, on the
   night of 22 and early morning 23 March 1957, were thoroughly investigated. Three
   objects which flew in the 23 minute intervals from the point they were first seen
   and then circled by them 30. The lights were sharp which dustr normally appeared and wiped
   in the camera day. There was also a beam with four red lights on its underside or
   otherwise locations. This sighting was attributed to the storm, the four red lights
   and light following off of green insulators on a nearby telephone pole.
