Dear Dr. Pickering:

I am enclosing a draft copy of a report together with three photographs which support the nature of this report. The report, as you will note, concerns sighting of an unidentified object on 10 October 1957. Though this report is several months old, it was not brought to my attention until this month. In discussing it with my staff here in Cedar Rapids, he suggested that I send it to you for your information.

I understand from Mr. Halladay that he has forwarded a copy of this report to the interested parties at MAIC in Dayton. In response to your Air Force contact, we visited our Tucson offices and interviewed the people who made the sighting.

This report is forwarded to you in the form of a draft in order that it could be forwarded quickly. If further use of the report is desired, we can supply it in finished form. I have purchased granted the engineers involved that I would return the report to them and so would appreciate its return to me when you have finished with it. I would be very pleased to discuss this matter with you in more detail in the future if you desire.

Very truly yours,
COLLINS RADIO COMPANY
