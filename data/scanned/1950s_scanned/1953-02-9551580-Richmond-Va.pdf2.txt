Richmond, Virginia                                23 February 1953

I. Description of Incident

At 1200 EST an Air Force colonel while traveling at 60 mph in his auto near Richmond observed a flash of metal with a long narrow rectangular contrail trailing from south to north. The object crossed the path of the auto from left to right traveling at less than jet speed. The object was observed approximately 15 minutes.

II. Discussion of Incident

Observer visited a friend of his about 1 hour later. The friend initiated the conversation by stating that he had seen a bright contrail in the sky about 1200 at considerable distance. At 1200 EST on this date the sun was setting. The sun, reflecting from contrail, made it easily visible.

III. Conclusion

Probably contrail of jet aircraft.
