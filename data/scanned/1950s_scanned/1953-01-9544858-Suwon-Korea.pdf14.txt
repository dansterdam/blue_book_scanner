II. Recently Project Blue Book sent five of its best unsolved 1953 sightings to Dr. H. P. Robinson, California Institute of Technology, for his review and comment. The sightings included Lake AOS, Continental Divide, Sea of Japan, and Fort Huron, Michigan.

III. The system of transmitting UFOS/RTFS to Willman Observatory with the possibility of identifying them as astronomical bodies is working out well.
