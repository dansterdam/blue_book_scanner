STATEMENT

At about 11:15 P.M. 22 Mar. '57 Capt Linsley telephoned my home - I had just gone to bed. Answered my phone which is so located in our kitchen that in using it I look out the kitchen window facing East. I told Capt. Linsley "I'm some I'm again: my first flying saucer. Really, we're here, I'll call her" - We hung up and I called Capt Linsley back at Capt. Linsley's suggestion - Capt L watched the original green light and subsequently (sic) 2 rod objects (lights) from 2 East windows until they disappeared (sic) found our house and arrived at 1:30 AM. I talked on the phone much of the 2 hr interval to the O.D. at the Base - describing all the antics the green light performed and later the arrival of the 2 red "midgets". The wording (sic) arrived, talked on the phone - we all watched a locked until 2:30 AM, Mar 23, 1957.
