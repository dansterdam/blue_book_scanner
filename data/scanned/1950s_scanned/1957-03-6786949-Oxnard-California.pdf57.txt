Colonel E. P. Carney, Commanding Officer
Oxnard Air Force Base
Camarillo, California

Dear Colonel Carney:

As a Director of the National Investigations Committee on Aerial Phenomena, I would very much like to furnish you with some additional information regarding a UFO sighting reported to have occurred in the vicinity of Oxnard Air Force Base on March 23rd last.

It has been established that I requested -- following the recent publication by representative John F-38 -- that we be furnished the following information:

1. What is the classification of the reports?

2. By what authority classified?

3. By what authority were they classified?

A self-addressed envelope is enclosed for your return. We will greatly appreciate your cooperation in this matter.

Respectfully,
