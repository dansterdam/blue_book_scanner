1. Reference is made to TWX, date-time 110645Z, this headquarters, symbol LFOPP-3.

2. The following information, submitted in accordance with Air Force Letter 200-5, dated 29 April 1952 is presented in conformity therewith as a summary of the information contained in the transcript of intercepted transmission, attached hereto as Inclosure 1.

     (a) Object sighted consisted of a white light, oval in shape. Size could not be determined, except that it was much larger than any visible star. It ranged in color from white to blue (bluish-white). Only one object appeared. No aerodynamic features were observed, since no silhouette was seen. No estimate of altitude or speed was made. Distances of object was unknown; however, observers felt it was very much faster than any jet aircraft. No exhaust or means of propulsion was visible.

     (b) Time of sighting was 11/0417Z. No positive fix can be made on duration of observation, but observers estimate the total elapsed time at from four to ten seconds.

     (c) Observation was visual only, from the air. Aircraft was a C-47, flying at 9,000 feet approximately at 150 M.P.H.

     (d) Observation was made at approximately 1450 25'N, 64°N. Light was first seen approximately the 9 o'clock position and traveling in level flight from right to left, disappeared when in the 9 o'clock position, off the left wing of the aircraft.
