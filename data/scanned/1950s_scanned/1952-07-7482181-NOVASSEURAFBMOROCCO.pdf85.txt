CAMBRIDGE 38, MASSACHUSETTS


April 18, 1961

Major Robert Friend
Air Technical Intelligence Center
United States Air Force

Dayton, Ohio

Dear Major Friend:

     I wonder if you can send me a copy of the file on

the Pan American Airlines sighting over Chesapeake Bay on

July 14, 1952. This appears to be one of the "classic" cases

and Mrs. Boyd and I are struggling with it. Certain features

suggest meteors, but we need more facts for our analysis.

     Thank you very much for your telephone call about

the Killian case.

                                        Cordially yours,
