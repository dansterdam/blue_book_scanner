SUMMARY

In compliance with AF Letter 200-5, dated 29 April 1952, the following information is submitted:

(1) Objects believed to be round, something like a ball from a "Dama Candle", crew in number, and performed no visible maneuvers. Size and construction of object could not be determined. Object was an intense green in color which faded just before it disappeared. No sound heard was a color any visible means of propulsion. Oposed estimated to be farther than jet aircraft yet slower than a meteor. Objects were first observed in a cloudless condition with 15 degrees above the horizon. It then flew to a two o'clock position in a southwesterly direction about 10 degrees above the horizon and disappeared. Velocity and light was lost by object just before it disappeared.

(2) Objects was sighted 1145 hours, CST, 31 Dec 1952, and was observed for only a few brief seconds.

(3) A visual surface observation without electronic or optical equipment was made.

(4) Observer was on Peace-Gildan Highway approximately one mile South of the Seven Palaces road intersection San Antonio, Texas.

(5) Object was observed by [redacted] Randolph Air Force Base, San Antonio, Texas. Reliability of observer unknown. However, person very well schooled in all conventional type aircrafts. He is presently employed as a draftsman for Dally Air Force Base.

(6) Fair clear weather prevailed at the time of the sighting.

(7) No activity or condition, meteorological or otherwise is known to have existed which may have accounted for the sighting.

(8) No physical evidence of the sighting exists.

(9) No intersection or identification action taken.

(10) Location of air activity in the general area at the time of the sighting is unknown.
