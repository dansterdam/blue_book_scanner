1. I T is forwarding statement concerning an unusual sighting which T witnessed on the night of 16 May 1952.

On the night of MAY 16, 1952, at approximately 2145 hrs, a group of about four or five onlookers, at which time we were on the campus of Furman University located in Greenville, South Carolina. We were getting ready to call it an evening when we sighted two (2) OBJECT. [sic] Illuminated greenish yellow objects traveling in a diamond formation. These objects were eight (8) feet in diameter traveling on an indirect course slightly right to left in bearing of about 300 degrees.

I was unable to estimate the altitude or speed of these obj[ects]. They traveled directly overhead during no sound, and I they would in all flight motion in similar to heavy cargo in flight. It appeared that the slight ground haze over the city, however, did not return to the human campus where it was perfectly clear.

By way of further description, I would say the objects were similar to the hibachi light. I reported in Life magazine. Apparently size would be that of a half dime held at arms length, turned.
