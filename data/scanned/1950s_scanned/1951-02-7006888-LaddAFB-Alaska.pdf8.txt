SYNOPSIS
Investigation initiated 26 February 1951 predicated on information from Lt. MSG J. FULLER, D-2203701, Asst. Division Intelligence Officer, 11th Air Division, that [redacted section] 600lst Air Base Group, had sighted an unusual unidentified object on 26 February 1951.

Investigation disclosed that SAMS, while on guard duty at 1710Z hours, 26 February 1951, allegedly sighted a gray object hovering over the Ladd runway at an altitude of 19,000 feet. The object, 10 to 12 feet thick and 120 feet across and resembling a flattened football, remained in this position about 45 seconds and then moved with great speed to the north after emitting a single white smoke ring. Object was not reported by any other person.

There were no weather observation balloons or aircraft in the air at that time. No project work was being conducted by Cold Water Test, Arctic Test Branch or the Geophysical Institute. No test firing was being done by units at Eielson AFB, Ladd AFB or Big Delta Army Base.
