CITE: SA 1122.

FYGOFRPT. 1 GREEN, BALL SHAPED, UNIDENTIFIED OBJECT WAS OBSERVED IN THE SKY OVER SAN ANTONIO TEXAS BY [redacted] KELLY AFB EMPLOYEE. OBJECT WAS SIGHTED 1315 HOURS 21 DEC 52, AT STEER-TILDEN HIGHWAY APPROXIMATELY 3 MILES SOUTH OF THE 7 OAKS ROAD INTERSECTION AND WAS TRAVELING IN A NORTHERLY DIRECTION. OBJECT WAS AT AN ELEVATION OF ABOUT 15 DEGREES ABOVE THE HORIZON, AND TRAVELED AT A RAPID SPEED IN A SLIGHTLY DOWNWARD COURSE. OBJECTS VELOCITY WAS ESTIMATED TO BE FASTER THAN ANY JET ACFT, YET SLOWER THAN A METEOR. OBJECTS LIGHT INTENSITY VARIED AND AFTER A PERIOD OF BETWEEN 4 TO 4 AND 1/2 SECONDS, THE OBJECT WAS PROCEEDING IN A DOWNWARD TREND, IT TOOK A FURTHER DOWNWARD DIP (SEEMINGLY LOOSING VELOCITY AND LIGHT), AND DISAPPEARED. SIZE OF OBJECT WAS COMPARED TO THAT OF A MEDIUM SIZE BALL. ALTITUDE COULD NOT BE DETERMINED. NO PARTS OF EXHAUST GAS NOTED NOR WERE THERE ANY AERODYNAMIC FEATURES ABOUT THE OBJECT. RELIABILITY OF OBSERVER UNKNOWN. AIR PROVOST MARSHAL.
