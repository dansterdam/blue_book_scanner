Summary recent int sightings Alaskan area.
1. Off in charge wea bureau sta Wales Alaska reported sighting of lights on bearing 226 degrees true fr Wales at 0305OZ Feb. Green light alternating to red observed directly above red light. Faint glow below lights indicated a ship although no superstructure visible. Lights disappeared at 0515Z, returned 1/2 hr later and persisted in same position until 0700Z when obscured by fog. Position USN icebreaker 0305OZ to 0307OZ 70 miles fr Cape Prince of Wales on bearing of 220 degrees true. No barrage balloons in air at time. 24 inch searchlights used intermittently to locate leads in ice. Wea at Wales clear visibility unlimited. Evaluation F-3.

2. Unidentified ship sighted lying approx 8 to 10 miles off cost of Nome in westerly direction between 0324OZ and 0404OZ. Ves appeared gray in color, estimated 250 ft in length, superstructure at one end, no visible stacks and no lights visible after dark. Ship viewed by large nr of mil and civ pers. Not visible on horizon 4 Feb local time. Burton Island nearest known vessel was 105 miles west of Nome at time of observation. Evaluation B-1.

3. Alaskan Airlines MO450 enroute Shenya to Elmendorf direct reported 3 ships at 56 degrees 00 minutes north 174 degrees 30 minutes west at 0708252 that shined light at acft. No known US ships this area. Evaluation F-2.
