SUMMARY: On 12 June, 1952, [redacted] the source of this report, was
standing at the passenger entrance gate south of the Municipal Airport Terminal.
At 1610, he observed an object which was then overdue. He noticed in the east a round white object hovering in the sky. The object remained almost stationary for 10-15 seconds, then moved southwest at a fast rate
of speed. The object stopped, made several maneuvers, and navigated in a field until
it reached its original position, stopped and took off directly east at an estimated
speed of 200 miles per hour. At this time the object appeared to change from a
globular to a saucer shape. Mr. [redacted], the second observer says that the shape
was not discernible, but said the object appeared to glint like metal. He describes
numerous reversals of direction and says the object was moving faster than T-33's
flying in the immediate vicinity of the observers.
