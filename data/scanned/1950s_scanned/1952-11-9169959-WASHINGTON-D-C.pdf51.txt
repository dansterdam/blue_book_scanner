IR-70-52

IR-70-52 stated that two visual nightings were made in the Panama area
at about 1330 25 November 1952. At the time of this report verification
could not be obtained.

Source, member of an Army anti-aircraft unit, was contacted on 3 December
1952. Source did see two objects at approximately 1300 25 November 1952.
However from the description, direction and altitude reporting officer determined that he saw the B-17 and the FBM which were attempting to intercept the
unidentified object.

COMMENT of the Preparing Officer:

It was obvious that source had been well trained in observation. He
knew what he saw, estimated directions, estimated altitudes, estimated size
as well as exact time of sighting.
