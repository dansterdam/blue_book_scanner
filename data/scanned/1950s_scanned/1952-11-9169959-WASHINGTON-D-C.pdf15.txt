CONSISTS of Preparing and Approving Officer (same):

12. This report is considered significant because of the reliability and experience of Major Hartanft and Captain Wheaton, and also because the trailing smoke glow may indicate a method of observation. The weather balloons sighted in this report were released at about the time of the observation, and, as will be seen from the listed window would only described a path similar to the one observed. The light attached to each balloon and swinging at the end of a 3' cord might account for the jerky movement described by A/3 Palmer. None of these facts, however, explain the reported color or size, nor do they explain the apparent hovering of the light, as a weather balloon would be constantly rising at a gradually de-celerated rate.
