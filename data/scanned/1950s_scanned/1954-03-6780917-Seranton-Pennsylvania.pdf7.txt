The following is a copy of a letter, undated and unsigned, mailed by the
2d District OSI (In) Detachment, Scranton, Pa. in an envelope addressed to
Dept of the Air Force, Special Investigation Dept., Select Building, Scranton,
PA., postmarked Scranton, Pa., 15 March 1954:

"Sir:

I do not know whether or not I'm writing to the proper
party or whether this is of any importance) but I figure
there is no harm done.

Friday, March 12 about 20 minutes to 5 in the evening
I was walking home and I looked up into the sky and I was
surprised to see what looked to what I expected a comet
look like. I took a few steps and stopped to watch the
object because it was moving swiftly and rather silently,
but when I stopped I saw that this comet was moving. It was
moving parallel to the street I was walking on but to the side.
Then I saw that it was turning I could see the nose of what
I got that at height front to it. It was about the same
luminosity from front to rear. So then I figured it was a
high flying jet that left their trail there but to start with
wide enough to glow out of heat generated by its prop. The
dwelt seemed to stay about the same height and I did not hear
any sound so in the sky and then it went out. It's
rather difficult to judge the but I watched for about 20
or 30 minutes. If it was a high flying jet it must have
really been going.

My present guess, what ever it is, It may be something
natural, but I am sure to be patented on out around the
cone flying cameras.

Thank you"

[handwritten: Jane [illegible]]                                    75Y-4265-
