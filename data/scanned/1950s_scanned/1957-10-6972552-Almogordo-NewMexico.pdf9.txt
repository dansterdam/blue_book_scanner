ALAMOGORDO, N.M., JULY 30--(UP)--A PHOTOGRAPH OF AN "UNCONVENTIONAL
AERIAL OBJECT"--SAUCER SHAPED AND AN ESTIMATED 1,000 (CORRECTED) FT.
ACROSS--WAS RECEIVED TODAY BY A GROUP OF CIVILIANS AT THIS MISSILE TEST
CENTER.

AERIAL PHENOMENA RESEARCH ORGANIZATION (APRO), WHICH HAS GOVERNMENT
EMPLOYES IN ITS MEMBERSHIP BUT HAS NO OFFICIAL STATUS, SAID A GOVERNMENT
PHOTOGRAPHER TOOK THE PICTURE ON OCT. 16, 1957. THE WEIRD, HUGE OBJECT
HOVERED FOR 15 MINUTES OVER THE NORTH MISSILE TEST RANGE NEAR HOLLOMAN
AIR FORCE BASE, THE APRO CLAIMED.

A DIRECTOR OF ALAMOGORDO, SPOKESMAN FOR THE APRO, SAID THE NAME
OF THE PHOTOGRAPHER COULD NOT BE REVEALED. HE SAID THE HATCH ACT
FORBIDS GOVERNMENT EMPLOYES FROM BECOMING INVOLVED IN SUCH
CONTROVERSIES SUCH AS THE DECADES-LONG DEBATE ABOUT WHETHER FLYING SAUCERS
EXIST.
