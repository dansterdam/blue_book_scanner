AOIT-7-1954 for MOR.
Following report forwarded under provisions PA USAF
Ltr ALO-SC-1, dtd 8 Sep 50. At 2117/02, an airman, while
performing interior guard duty at Ladd AFB, Alaska, reported
observing what appeared to be an unconventional aircraft hovering
over the Airbase. Object appeared oblong in shape, without
appendage, and had a dull, grey metallic color. Observer
estimated object to be at 15,000 ft altitude approximately 80
degrees from the horizon. Object hovered over Ladd AFB for
approximately 45 seconds, then emitted 1 puff of white smoke,
proceeded toward South at great speed and disappeared having
no trail, exhaust or sound. Total time of observation approx
1 and 1/2 min. Observer has 20-20 vision, 2 and 1/2 yrs
service and states object definitely not a conventional aircraft.
Ladd weather at time was thin precipitation ceiling at 500 ft,
visibility 1 mi in light snow and fog. No trails, ceiling
overcast 15,000. No weather balloons or phenomena reported in
area at time of observation. Air traffic in area was 1 Dan
Morrison, C-47 high lander Ladd at 2017/02. Object not sighted
by Radar. No fix given at time of sighting. Preliminary in-
vestigation made at AFB and surrounding area indicates no other
personnel observed object. Investigation continuing. Complete
report to follow. Evaluation C-5.
