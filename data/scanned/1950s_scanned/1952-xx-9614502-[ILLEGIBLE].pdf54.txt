These are my observations of an object that I would call a "fire ball".

DESCRIPTION: The shape was sherical(I am assuming that it had a third dimension). The size was about ⅓ the size of the moon(the noon and the object were in the same field of view). The color was blue white, self luminous,(about the color of a daylight fluorescent tube). There was only one object. It left no visible trail. It made no sound. The speed can be estimated from the following data: the object traversed about 15° of arc in 2 to 3 seconds. The traveled a slight arc from West to East, about parallel to the horizon. The dispae range of the object was as follows: The object was intensly bright and all of a sudden the brightness ceased (no explosion)and what appeared to be sparks (few) seemed to float down from the object. These sparks burned out before reaching the ground.

TIME OF SIGHTING: It was about 10:00 P.M. The length of time observed was about 2 to 5 seconds.

MANNER OF OBSERVATION: The object was viewed with the naked eye from the ground.

LOCATION OF OBSERVER: I was standing at 5520 Montrose, Murray, Utah. The location of the object was as follows: When I first saw the object it was directly South at about the same altitude as the noon. It traveled in a slight arc from West to East (distance traveled about 15 degrees of arc). Its flight ended about 7degrees West of the moon and about 7 degrees below the moon. I can only guess at the distance from me to the object, but because of its size, brightness and the manner in which the sparks fell, I would say that it was relatively close.
