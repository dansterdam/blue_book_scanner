DETAILS:

1. This investigation was initiated by the District Commander, North Africa District OSI, APO 118, USAF, predicated upon information received from Captain THEODORE A. HORST, Commanding Officer, 7769th Air Base Squadron, 316th Air Division (Defense), APO 118, USAF, who, at 2230 hours, March 1954, telephonically advised North Africa District Headquarters that Air Control Site #1 had reported unidentified objects/aircraft flying in the vicinity of Nouasseur Air Depot.

2. Special Agents DANIEL F. WRIGH, DALE W. DAWSON, and WILLIAM T. BRUNETTE assisted in this investigation.

AT NOUASSEUR AIR DEPOT, FRENCH MOROCCO, APO 30, USAF

3. At 2310 hours, 5 March 1954, 1st Lieutenant HARRY J. SAVOY, AQ 1453527, Aircraft Commander, 7769th ACS, on duty at Control Site #1, telephonically advised Special Agent WRIGH that the Nouasseur Control Tower Operator had advised him that Air Force aircraft flying in the local area reported unidentified objects/aircraft in vicinity of Nouasseur Air Depot. SAVOY further advised that the high radar scope reflected a target at 10,000 feet, Ruwiyah (12) miles south of the field. However, he correlates that the target, which appeared to be moving very slowly and which disappeared and reappeared on the scope, could possibly be a cloud. SAVOY added that except with respect to positions due to radar azimuth, SAVOY added that he had reported that information to the Duty Officer at the 316th Air Division (Defense), Captain T. A. HORST. SAVOY stated further that he would continue to monitor area.

4. At 2340 hours, 5 March 1954, 3/Sgt WAYNE F. BRADSHAW, AF 19645322, 776th ACS Squadron, on duty as Aircraft Dispatcher, Base Operations, Nouasseur Air Depot, advised 3/A WRIGH that the following Air Force aircraft had been flying in the local area of Nouasseur Air Depot that evening:

a. Locally based aircraft

AC-97, #6337, Pilot: O. L. BROWN, 3d Lieut
AC-97, #6329, Pilot: R. R. ZADNIK, 3d Lieut
C-47, #2844, Pilot: T. R. MC CANE, 1210sqt

b. Transient aircraft:

C-54, #2684, Pilot: FORD, Home Station: Hely AFB, Texas
C-47, #5209, Pilot: MCSHANE, Home Station: SAC Slimaux Air Base, French Morocco
