1. The following information is being forwarded to your Headquarters
for whatever action you may deem appropriate.

2. On Saturday, 15 November 1952 at 0245 hours, Captain [redacted],
[redacted] a special agent of this district, OSI, and a pilot with
approximately 1000 hours flying time including combat in e. a., along with [redacted],
[redacted], observed an unidentified aerial object. The object
passed over their house at [redacted] traveling due east. The
sound which came became to the window of his house, as close as
he can relate it, was similar to a flight of jets, six (6) or more, at
high altitude. However, on observing the position of the identified
sited object, its altitude was estimated at 4000 feet moving in a northerly
direction. All that could be observed was a white or pale blue light
giving at a speed in excess of any conventional aircraft even observed by
the Captain or his wife. There were no wing lights; and from the angle
first observed, a green, right wing lights as well as a white light
should have been seen. The total time of observation was some seven (7)
or eight (8) seconds before this craft made a left turn, to the south,
that seemed normal and took on abnormal aspect of about a 45-degree angle.
From after the object made its turn, the white lights was still observed
but went out slightly after the object started climbing.

3. The weather at the time of observation was clear at point of
observation with an overcast having in from the west.
