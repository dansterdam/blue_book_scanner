The object was described as circular or disc-shaped. The officer stated that the object was stationary for a brief period and then moved rapidly across the sky. The object was observed on the horizon source of object. In an area 5 miles north of Enid, Oklahoma. The area was rural in nature, and the object appeared to move in a northwesterly direction.
