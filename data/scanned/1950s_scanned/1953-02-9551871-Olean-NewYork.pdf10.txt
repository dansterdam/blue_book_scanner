G. Report number 7 was made on 26 Feb 53.

1. One round object, stationary, and a dim yellow in color, was sighted fading into the distance. It emitted sharp flashes of white light.

2. Time of sighting was 0050Z. Length of time in sight was 25 minutes.

3. Visual from surface.

4. Observer was at MR507. Object was 3 1/2 miles west of GOC Post MR508 and 1/2 miles north of the observer, at a high altitude.

5. SOURCE was [redacted] housewife, of Olean, N.Y. C-3

6. High overcast, no wind.

7. Unknown.

9. None.

10. None.

1. One round, red object, very high,sighted heading south very fast spinning. No information as to aero-dynamic features, propulsion system or other unusual features was given.

2. Time of sighting 1104Z. Length of time in sight was 29 minutes.

3. Observer was at MR205, GOC Post MRGCB, Olean, N.Y. No information as to position of object with respect to observer other than that contained in par 1 above is available.

4. Manner of observation was visual from surface.

5. SOURCE WAS [redacted] Clean, N.Y, phone 22875.

6. Cold and clear.

7. None.
