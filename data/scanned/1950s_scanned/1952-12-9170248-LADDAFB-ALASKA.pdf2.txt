Object unidentifiable due to darkness of night, speed of object, and position of observer. Object not audible. When on ground, nothing of a motor, engine, or other propulsion nature was noticed. Temperature approximately -23° and noise could be (have been) heard at very long distance with no wind at that temperature. Upon landing, (Note: After F-94 landed) the object moved in an erratic flight path for a period of three minutes until it was pointed out again to the pilot and a ground crew members.(???) Then it took up a heading of 160° gaining speed until it looked like a falling star or meteor. Descending all the time, it continued to become a brighter red as it reached the earth. The light appeared to diminish its forward speed and descent to the earth as a helicopter would. At this point the light was noticeably more red than before. Time: 0906164 to 0906263. Weather conditions: Night, VFR CAVU.
