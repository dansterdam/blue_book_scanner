San Antonio, Texas                                          21 December 1952

I. Description of Incident

One round, unidentified object of undetermined size, that emitted an intense green light was observed and reported by a civilian man while driving in his car in San Antonio. Time of sighting was 1815 hrs., CST, for a few seconds only. Object looked like a "Roman Candle" and faded just before disappearing.

II. Discussion of Incident

This can be written off with quite a bit of assurance as simply a common meteor. It has all the characteristics, including a short time in sight and fed out just before disappearing.

III. Conclusion

Probably meteor.
