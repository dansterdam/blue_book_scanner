SUN. TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 5, 1957

AFRadar Hunts Mystery Objects

By the United Press.

The Air Force said today its radar network has been alerted to watch for whatever it has sparked a flurry of reports from the Gulf of Mexico to the Canadian border.

A radar report came today from a Coast Guard craft which relayed word it had kept a brilliant flying object in its radarscope as it circled slowly.

The Coast Guard cutter said yesterday reported that the object was observed by radar and by the ship's high speed.

The object, estimates resembled "a brilliant plane." Three Air Force planes at Reese Air Base said they saw a similar light object flash through the sky near Lubbock, Tex.
