E. Report number 5 was received on 2. Feb 53.

1. One cone-shaped object, size unknown was sighted while moving up and down, headed north. It has turning from green to red to white in color. It had no trail or exhaust and made no sound. Its propulsion system and manner of disappearance was unknown.

2. Time of sighting USAAF. Length of time in sight unknown.

3. Visual from surface.

4. Observer was at Harpell, object was northeast of observer, handling north. Distance, direction and altitude from observer unknown.

5. Observer was SSGT N. Liss, Clean, NY. Phone 477. He was is a qualified ground observer at C/O Post 1,308, phone (IAMB XXXX), C-2

6. None.

7. None.

8. Mr. GEORGE LIAM, Clean Rinse-Merald was taking pictures of the object at the time of sighting. He was advised by the Filter Center to pass negative to 3rd A.C.

9. Condor head was scrambled and directed to the area. No contact was made or any unusual sightings reported.

10. None.

F. Report number 6 was made on 24 Feb 53.

1. One disc-shaped object, silver in coloring sighted moving very fast. It made no sound and moved up and down and back and forth. No information as to aero-dynamic features, propulsion system or other unusual features was given.

2. Observer was at 1350L, Olean, NY. Object was 8 miles west of observer, one-half mile high, heading east.

3. Time of sighting was 0905L. Length of time in sight unknown.

4. Manner of observation was visual from surface.

5. SOURCE was ELLIOT _____, phone clear 756L, Olean, NY. MT. ELLIOT is a qualified ground observer at C/O Post 1,308, Clean, NY, phone 22675, C-2

6. Weather consisted of ground fog.

7. Unknown.
