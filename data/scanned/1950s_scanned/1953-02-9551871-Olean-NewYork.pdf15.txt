There were 8 sightings of unidentified flying objects reported to this station through Detachment 1, USAF Ground Observer Squadron, Buffalo-Niagara University, during the period 11 Feb 53 to 1 Mar 53.

Source of 7 reports was GCC Post M303 at Olean, Cattaraugus County, New York (N40205). Sightings were made in all but one case by qualified ground observers.

Source of one report was Corry Pa., GCC post U253. Sighting was made by qualified observer.
