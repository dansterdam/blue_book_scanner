COMMENT (AUTHORITY AND SOURCE FOR INFORMATION)

1. A celestial formation, believed to be controls, was observed at two locations in Greenland on 10 December at approximately 2030Z, at Cape Dyer on the east coast and at Sonde Stream Air Base, Greenland on 10 December at 1700Z.

2. While the exact cause of the controls formation cannot be ascertained, it is considered possible that they were caused by Soviet aircraft on a reconnaissance mission.
