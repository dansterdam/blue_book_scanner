10. BRIEF SUMMARY OF SIGHTING
One large blue fire ball, 20 feet in diameter or the size of a volleyball at half a mile. Object left a bluish trail. Object left, or there was, an indescribable odor in the air unlike ozone burning. Object moved high and fast. Object was seen in a trajectory arc NE to SW. Object disappeared into woods at visible range after having been seen for 10 to 15 seconds.

11. COMMENTS
Description, duration, flight path and manner of disappearance indicate sighting was caused by a meteor.
