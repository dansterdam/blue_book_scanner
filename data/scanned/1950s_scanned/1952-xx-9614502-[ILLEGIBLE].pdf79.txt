Statement [ Col John A.M. Miller (Contd) ] UNCLASSIFIED

I asked him if he had ever read or studied about this subject. He stated that he had not and was as skeptic as most people of the existence of flying saucers. The interrogation was completed about 4:30 A.M. At that time we did have him sign a single statement that Lt. Albright had typed, to the effect that the notes taken were true. I took him to my hotel room after we had parked his car in a garage. I instructed Lt. Albright to contact the local M.P.I. to have them keep watch on him. We rested at the hotel until 7:00 A.M. and then returned to the Filter Center.

I had him describe the object and give me dimensions as I attempted to sketch it. Again I was impressed with the details that he remembered and wondered if it was a fabrication of his own mind. I do feel that regardless of whether he saw or dreamt up this 'gadget' it has possibilities as a combined autogiro and either jet or rocket propelled platform that might have the characteristics reported by flying saucer observers.

Mr. [redacted] was quite concerned in getting off the monthly payment on his car to a California agent. He also was looking for a job. He departed here at 10:00 AM, but made two phone calls at my request from Monday, which is near by. On the last call he stated that he might be getting a job at Williston, N. Dak. which is approximately 200 miles from here. He did promise to either call or come back here at 8 P.M. This he did not do. I wanted to get him to read and sign the inclosed statements which I have compiled for him from the note and his discussion of the subject.

In the interest of time I am forwarding them with out his signature that appears only on one page of the notes.

[handwritten: John A. M. Miller]
JOHN A. M. MILLER
