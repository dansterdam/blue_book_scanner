TOWN SIGHTINGS 3/15.                    [redacted] CALLING TO ANYPLACE

"Within is something that I should have told you as soon as I
could after it occurred on 24 July 1952. But what I saw scared me
so much that I hesitated to talk to anyone about it. I did talk it
over with my brother who advised me to contact you here at the Filter
Center. Another reason for not mentioning it is because I'm having
trouble with my wife in California and I do not want any publicity that
would get to her and let her know where I am. I kept thinking about
this strange experience so much it bothered my sleep and so today I
decided to call you and get this appointment.

In the afternoon of Thursday, 24 July 1952, I was driving through
Montana on the highway between the towns of Circle and Grassly, Montana.
It was about 8:00 P.M., Mountain Standard time, when I turned off the
road north from Lindsey, Montana to try my luck with my rifle in shoot-
ing a coyote. The road soon disappeared and I drove on out across the
open fields towards some hills. My attention was first drawn to what
appeared to be a water tank. I was in a ravine about a ¼ mile away
and then I proceeded on foot with my rifle in hand. As I approached
I could see it was not a water tank and first got the impression it was
a rocket like the type used to advertise the Oldsmobile Automobile. I
climbed a little hill and my attention was then drawn to a group of
horses who were milling around by a lake. Then I noticed two men who
were swimming or bathing and that was what the horses seemed to be cur-
ious about. Without attracting their attention I managed to get to the
object I first sighted and was surprised to find that it was of an extra
ordinary nature and shape. -I was amazed at the trim shape and workman-
ship; that it represented. It stood on three wheels, was circular in
diameter with the strut bracing and central body giving it the appear-
ance of a big toy top. The stem extending upwards was about 5 feet square and 8 feet tall. I estimate that the overall diameter of the object
to be about 36 feet and it stood about 20 feet tall.

I am a private pilot and am skilled as an automotive mechanic and
body worker. I was more interested in its mechanism than in its over-
all shape. My curiosity overcame my fear and by keeping in eight the
two men who were bathing, I was given about 50 minutes to examine the
structure of the object. It was painted light blue all over and I man-
aged to scrape some of this paint off with my car keys and found the
metal underneath was of a very tough alloy. The seams were either
welded or crimped as no rivets were used. I tried shaking the structure
and it would rock but it was too heavy to lift the wheels by rocking.
