PART TWO

SUPPLEMENTARY INVESTIGATIVE EFFORTS

1. 1st Lt Leonard B. Ott, Officer of the Day on the night of 22, 23 March 1957 at OXNARD AFB, CALIFORNIA, was contacted and a statement from him received. See Incl #2.

2. The 669th Aircraft Control and Warning Squadron located on SANTA ROSA ISLAND, mailing address from FLINDER, CALIFORNIA, was contacted on duty on the night of 22, 23 MAR 57 furnished the following information: 1st Lt Edward E. Martin, two (2) years experience at the station, said that the station was operative during the entire time of sightings. Only one paint was noted that night, at 0215Z, or five (5) hours before the sighting was reported. The paint was noted only once, held two (2) minutes, then dropped. No other paints were noted during the night although Lt Martin stated that this AC & D site does not normally pay much attention to overland activity being primarily interested in over-water, inbound traffic. Personnel also reported no contact with the object.

3. A check with Base Operations, OXNARD AFB, disclosed the following: Two (2) F-89 type aircraft, OXNARD based, were returning from a flight in the LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA area on 23/0300 MAR 57 when informed by the controller of the 669th AQ & W Squadron of the objects in the CAMARILLO area. Aircraft were then vectored to the area of the sighting. A search from one thousand (1000) feet up was conducted. Aircraft reported neither a visual nor a radar sighting. Aircraft returned to base and no further action was taken.

4. A check with Detachment 9, 14th Weather Squadron at OXNARD AFB revealed the following:

Temperature at 23/0900Z MAR 57 at the weather station was 43 degrees. Moonrise was at 23/0915Z MAR 57. There was no cloud cover and visibility was unlimited. There was a shallow surface base temperature inversion around one thousand (1000) feet.

5. Detachment 2, 477lst Ground Observer Corps, Pasadena Filter Center at PASADENA, CALIFORNIA, was contacted and the following information received: The Filter number received a telephone call from an alleged Observer, a line-lineman on duty at the main gate at OXNARD AFB, reporting the object in the CAMARILLO area. When informed of the activity and was simply taking the call at the request of the Sightings Supervisor at Fort MacArthur. The Filter number received no unusual sighting reports from the CAMARILLO area on the night of the sighting.

6. The observatories at MT WILSON and MT PALOMAR, CALIFORNIA, were contacted and a report of no unusual activity received.
