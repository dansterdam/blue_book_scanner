Flight 2-D, 4602d Ai93 | 2D-JFOB-5-55 PART ONE | DATE 3 | OF 21 | PAGES

Pt. 2 Three brilliant whitish-yellow lights proceeded by a red glow with no outline present (through field glasses).

Pt. 3 Three brilliant whitish yellow lights proceeded by a red light. The outline of the lights was blurry and the edges ragged.

3. SOURCE was certain that the actual time of the sighting was one minute and thirty seconds.

4. There were a few stars in the sky and a small moon.

5. SOURCE estimated the object to be about one mile away and solid in shape.

6. SOURCE stated that he believed he saw the same object three separate times rather than three different objects because there was adequate time for the object to circle back because of its tremendous rate of speed.

7. SOURCE stated that the object was about the size of a baseball held in the hand at arm's length.

8. The night of the sighting was very warm, dry, with a slight breeze and a clear sky.

9. SOURCE stated that he had no conception of what the object might have been but that it was not a meteorite.
