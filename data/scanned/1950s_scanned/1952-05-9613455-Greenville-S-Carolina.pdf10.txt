TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN:

Inclosed is information of an unusual sighting which is true and correct to the best of my knowledge.

On the night of May 16, 1952, at approximately 2230 h., a group of amateur astronomers of which I was one were preparing to return home after an evening of observing from the Furman Campus located in Greenville, South Carolina.

One of the group called our attention to what I would say were four objects. The shape of good, without much or detail which showed a luminous brown or amber color. Incident and flying in a diamond formation. Distance between each object was about five (5) times the width of each object.

They were flying about ten (10) to fifteen (15) degrees from directly overhead on a fixed course heading about 135 degrees. The elevation was about ten (10) degrees or from 1000 feet (1000) feet. The flight time within five seconds.

In all eight or a still dim a minute light (a) degree up on one side and at the same time down on the other.
