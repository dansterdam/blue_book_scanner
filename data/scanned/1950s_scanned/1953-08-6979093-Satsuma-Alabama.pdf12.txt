On the night of 1-2 August at approximately 12:30 AM local time, I
was driving from home toward Creola, Alabama, on Highway 43, heading south. I
was approaching the area of Gunnison Creek between Creola and Satsuma,
Alabama, when I saw an object in the sky East of the hiway. To me the
object had a curved landing edge which was the only striking feature seemed
to be distinct in shape. The rest of the object appeared to taper to
the rear to a point with the entire object glowing in amber color. The
object was all that and slightly on a heading from North to the south at
an instantaneous rate of speed. There was no sound associated with the object
and it did not resemble a star or a meteor. The object was fairly large
in size and was in sight to my knowledge at approximately 20-30 degr in
altitude. I saw the object for what seemed to be only a few seconds then
disappeared to the South paralleling the hiway. There is also a beacon
light located in the area, but I can definitely state that it was not
connected with the object. The weather was clear with no moon but with
many bright stars.
