ALAMOGORDO, N.M., AUG. 9.—(UPI)—A CIVILIAN RESEARCH GROUP
SAID TODAY IT HAS PHYSICAL EVIDENCE INDICATING "EXTRA-
TERRESTRIAL BASIS FOR AT LEAST A PORTION" OF REPORTS OF UNIDENTIFIED
FLYING OBJECTS.

[redacted], PUBLIC RELATIONS DIRECTOR FOR THE AERIAL
PHENOMENA RESEARCH ORGANIZATION HERE, MADE THE STATEMENT AS NEW
MEXICO REPORTED YELLOWISH STARLIKE LIGHTS IN THE SKY FRIDAY NIGHT.
AND THE "PHYSICAL EVIDENCE" WAS SECURED "THROUGH THE
EFFORTS OF OUR WORLD WIDE ORGANIZATION." HE DID NOT GIVE FURTHER
DETAILS, EXCEPT TO SAY THE ORGANIZATION WAS CHECKING THE EVIDENCE
MEANWHILE, A ROCKET SCIENTIST AT WHITE SANDS MISSILE RANGE, N.M.,
REPORTED SEEING A GROUP OF UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECTS NEAR THE
ALAMOGORDO MISSILE TESTING CENTER AT ABOUT THE SAME TIME WITNESSES
SAW A CLUSTER OF LIGHTS.

THE ENGINEER, WHOM THE APRO DID NOT IDENTIFY, SAID THE OBJECTS
DESCENDED AND CLIMBED FROM THE SOUTHWEST TOWARD THE TESTING CENTER
AT AN ALTITUDE OF ABOUT 10,000 FEET.

THE APRO SAID A CHECK WITH AIRPORTS IN THE AREA INDICATED NO
AIRCRAFT WERE IN FLIGHT PLANES IN THE AIR AT THE TIME—ABOUT
10:30 P.M.—AND NO WEATHER BALLOONS OR ROCKET LAUNCHINGS PLANNED.

EARLIER, THE APRO SAID THAT PERSONS HAD REPORTED SEEING MYSTERIOUS
OBJECTS AT ABOUT THE SAME TIME.

THE APRO IN ALBUQUERQUE, SAID APRO'S FILTER CENTER "I WOULDN'T HAVE BELIEVED IT IF I HADN'T SEEN IT
MYSELF."

A DAUGHTER [redacted], 20, SAID, "IT LOOKED LIKE DOZENS OF STARS ALL
IN A CLUSTER, WEAVING IN AND OUT OF THE CLOUDS. IT MOVED TOWARD THE
MOUNTAINS TO THE EAST, THEN SEEMED TO BREAK IN TWO OR SEPARATE. ONE
PART WENT SOUTHEAST, THE OTHER TO THE NORTHEAST."

AN ELECTRONICS ENGINEER SAID THE OBJECTS SEEMED
TO BE TRAVELING AT SUPER-HUMAN SPEEDS, AND MAKING MANEUVERS NO
AIRCRAFT COULD WITHSTAND.

LORENZEN, APRO PUBLIC RELATIONS DIRECTOR, SAID "DISTORTIONS" IN
STATEMENTS FROM AN ARTICLE PRINTED IN THE APRO BULLETIN BY NAMED
PSYCHOLOGIST DR. CARL JUNG OF SWITZERLAND MAY HAVE BEEN DUE TO
"HAPHAZARD QUOTING."

JUNG SAID SATURDAY IN ZURICH THAT SOME AMERICAN NEWSPAPER HAD
QUOTED HIM AS SAYING FLYING SAUCERS WERE REAL.

IN THE ARTICLE, WHICH THE APRO REPRINTED FROM A BRITISH JOURNAL,
JUNG WROTE THAT IN SOME CASES OF SIGHTINGS "I CAN ONLY SAY FOR CERTAIN
THESE THINGS ARE NO MERE RUMORS.—SOMETHING HAS BEEN SEEN."
