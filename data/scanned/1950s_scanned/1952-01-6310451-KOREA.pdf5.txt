Info: C5 USAF Wash DC, For Director of Intel

Subj is report on info on unidentified flying objects: (1) observed 1 globe shaped object with a slight tear drop affect noted on lower side. Estimated size to be 3 feet across when at its nearest point to F-29. The size was not definitely established as the distance from the observer was not known. The color of the object was tinted the sun, a light orange, and it occasionally changed to a bluish tint. The outer edge of the object appeared to be fuzzy and it seemed to have an internal churning movement like flames or flory gases when it was at its nearest point to F-29. The object was first observed parallel on course at 8 o'clot lower, where it seemed to be about the side of a saucer, gradually becoming larger as it approached the F-29. It came in at the same level as the F-29 and remained in the same relative position to the F-29 for approximately 1 min and then receded on the same path, falling away in the distance. (2) Sighted on Jan 29 13 00z for a period of approximately 1 min. (3) The left gunner and tail gunner observed the object without the use of any optical or electronic equipment. The F-29 was at 22250 feet on cousr almost 125 knots. (4) The 2 observers were airborne in the F-29 in the cross position of left gunner and tail gunner. Object was observed at 10 30'0 and level with the F-29. (5) The observers are veterans of world war 2 comat and have flown several comat missions over Korea and are thoroughly familiar with a/c, clouds and rockets. The observers state very emphatically that the object observed bore no resemblance whatsoever to anything they had previously seen. This sighting is almost the same as sighting reports in war time. This report came from a different squadron and interrogations were conducted at different times.
