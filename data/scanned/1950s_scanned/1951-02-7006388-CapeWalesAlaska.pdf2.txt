DTG:  Feb 51

AAOID C881 officer in charge Powell of Weather Bureau Airways Station Wales Alaska reports sighting of lights on bearing of 242 degrees from Wales at 0305OOZ Feb. Green light alternating to red observed directly above red light. Faint glow below lights indicated a ship although no superstructure visible. Lights disappeared at 0515Z, returned one half hour later and persisted in same position until 0700Z when obscured by fog. Request position of Burton Island at time of observation and whether flying barrage balloon.

A016 (TOT)
