1. Attached is a copy of a "CIRVIS" report submitted by the crew of a MATS R/V Flight, Trip #352/238. Also attached is a statement by LCdr Roach who was piloting the aircraft at the time of the sighting. This report is forwarded to your headquarters as a result of a telephone conversation between Colonel Frasman, your headquarters, and Major Erwin of the 1502d Air Transport Wing, Guam.

2. This report was given to the Guam Air/Ground Station initially and reported to Guam Operations upon landing at Mas Agana. The report is forwarded to your headquarters for your information and further distribution as you deem necessary.
