Flight 2-D, 4602d ATSS

SUPPLEMENTARY INVESTIGATIVE EFFORTS:

1. A check with the United States Weather Bureau, Love Field, Dallas Texas for balloons released in the area at the time of sighting. Negative results.

2. Contacted Civil Aeronautics Administration, Love Field, Dallas Texas for aircraft over the area at the time of sighting and received negative results.

3. Duncanville Radar Station, Duncanville Texas reported no blips on the scope from 0400Z to 0300Z 21 Jun 55.

4. Base Operations, Naval Air Station, Hensley Field, Dallas Texas was contacted for aircraft over area of sighting and obtained negative results.

5. Base Weather Station at the Dallas Naval Air Station, Hensley Field, Dallas Texas stated no balloons had been released 20 Jun 55. Weather information on 21 Jun 55 at 0300Z was clear and winds were holding out of the west up to forty-thousand (40,000) feet. Wind speeds up forty-thousand feet averaged twenty-five (25) miles per hour. Wind direction and speed for the following altitudes were as follows:

Altitude                    Wind Speed              Wind Direction
41,000 feet                15 knots                350 degrees
47,000 feet                40 knots                350 degrees

6. A check was made with Mr. [REDACTED], Director of the Texas Astronomical Association, [REDACTED], Dallas Texas and received negative results.

7. Carswell Air Force Base Weather Service stated that a rawinsonde type weather balloon was release at 0930Z 21 Jun 55.

8. Dr. [REDACTED], S.M.U. University Professor of astronomy, was contacted and Preparing Officer received negative results.

9. Carswell Rawinsonde Service revealed the following information: A rawinsonde type balloon was released at 0930Z 21 June 1955. Wind direction and speed for the following altitudes were as follows:

Altitude                    Wind Speed              Wind Direction
20,000 feet                25 knots                350 degrees
25,000 feet                25 knots                300 degrees
30,000 feet                30 knots                200 degrees

10. Flight Operations, Convair Aircraft Corporation, tested two aircraft 20 June 1955, but both of the flights terminated at 0000 and 0200 CST.

11. Chance Vought aircraft corporation test center tests experimental aircraft only during the daylight hours.
