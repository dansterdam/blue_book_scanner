II. RELIABILITY: SOURCE was sincere, friendly, and cooperative. All questions asked were answered in a straight forward manner. SOURCE was very interested in the sighting and anxious to find out the results of the investigation. Reporting officer believes the source to be reliable.

III. SOURCE'S DESCRIPTION OF THE SIGHTING: On 20 June 1955, SOURCE's attention was directed to an unknown object travelling at a tremendous rate of speed in a south westerly direction. The object followed an erratic course in the southwest direction and changed course from 195 degrees to about two hundred and sixty degrees. The object held this course until it faded from view. The first sighting lasted a total of twenty-five to thirty seconds. The time of the first sighting was 2150 CST.

2. On the second sighting the object was observed through binoculars. The object appeared to be either in color and very fuzzy around the edges. SOURCE stated that there was no sound. The total time of the second sighting lasted twenty seconds. The object followed a course of approximately one hundred feet, and travelled at a tremendous rate of speed. The second sighting occurred at 2200 CST.

3. At 2215 P.M. CST, 20 June 1955, the object was sighted a third time. The object approached SOURCE maintaining a heading of about ten degrees and then changed to a heading of about one hundred and sixty degrees. During this third sighting, the object appeared as three bright incandescent lights. The lights were arranged in a straight line and the outline of an object was seen. The total time of sighting was approximately twenty-five seconds.

4. At 2220 CST, 20 June 1955, the fourth sighting occurred. The object appeared on a course of two hundred eighty degrees, and then again changed to a heading of ten degrees. On this sighting there were three green lights accompanied by one white light. One of the green lights seemed to fade out, and then one back on. Total time of the last sighting was approximately twenty-five to thirty seconds.
