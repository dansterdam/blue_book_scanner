UNIDENTIFIED TARGET OF AN/FPS-17(XW-2) RADAR MISSION 164

I. INTRODUCTION

The AN/FPS-17(XW-2) radar at the Laredo Test Site, Laredo, Texas

was in operation on 11 October 1958. The expected target was 1958

△ 2, the instrumented portion of the third Russian astellite, Sputnik

III. A target was observed, but the characteristics of the intercept

would make one believe that the object observed was not the one expected.

Other possible explanations are discussed below.

II. CONCLUSIONS

The object observed was most probably:

(a) a meteor showing both a head echo type reflection
and a specular type reflection from the trail, or

(b) two meteors occurring in the same portion of space
at very nearly the same time.
