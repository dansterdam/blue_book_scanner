B. The following is an extract from Controller's log, Combat Operations Center, 25th Air Division (Def):

0320: Radio report of explosion over Seattle was relayed by CCO, this fn.

0520: An object believed to be a meteor was sighted coming from the south and going North. The object exploded over Seattle. The object was sighted by a Northwest Airlines pilot, Seattle Police, and many others. Its general description was a red body with trailing streaks but no estimate was given of its speed. The airlines pilot felt the altitude was about 7000 feet. There were no reports of damage to any radar trucks. This station was off the air at the time on minor [illegible]. Reports of the concussion came from as far away as North Tacoma. Explosion time 0035 PST.

1125: The pilot of the Northwest airliner was [REDACTED] - Login SMPT. He reports sighting the object 2 minutes before passing Robert Inn at 0061 or 0032 PST. He reports the object was left of his A/C (30-3) and appeared to be a rocket-type, giving off sparks or streamers coming from it. The object exploded slightly below his A/C level (7000 ft t) with a lingering flash which lighted the sky but not the ground. He did not hear or feel explosion. The A/C was loaded 8 Sea-Tac. The Sea-Tac controller [REDACTED] also saw the object and believed it exploded in the Renton area. Washington State Patrol, Seattle Patrol, Sea-point Naval Tower were among people sighting the object. First report received was from a person on the North end of Mercer Island who reported the explosion northeast of the island. He saw the flash and several seconds later felt the blast. Apparently no one kept any record of names of people sighting the object because of the volume of calls. [REDACTED] (Game Operator) reported seeing the object. Mr. [REDACTED] Summit, also reported sighting the object. MAP advised.

2035: The University of Washington professor mentioned in the reports of explosion over Seattle is [REDACTED], Phone [REDACTED] in Seattle. He now says he believes the explosion to be a meteor and is compiling all data for a report. For which he did not say.

3. From evidence available it is the opinion of the undersigned, the unidentified object was a meteor.
