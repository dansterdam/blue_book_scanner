1. Reference is made to AFCIC letter No. AFCIC-13 current 1952
and letter Department of Air Force, Headquarters, USAF, Washington 25,
D.C., subject: reporting of Unconventional Aircraft,

2. The following information is furnished as directed in above
cited letters for most rapid means of communications to appropriate
headquarters. Regular report on Air Department Form 10 C-23 is forthcoming.

SHAPE: Definitely circular in shape and very flat.

SIZE: Estimate approximately 100 feet in diameter. Determined
by use of pencil, diameter of objects approximately one and one-
half inches on pencil at arms length. Believes distance of objects
at closest proximity to Pan American aircraft at 6,000 feet. Also,
estimated size of unconventional aircraft in relationship to city
and surrounding area.

COLOR: White, top surface appeared to be a hot coal, with steady
glow of light. a little more acute than a clean tall light. The
very brilliant glow than the lights of Norfolk and Newport News,
Virginia. Used extinguished directly below it at after that.
Then reappeared approximately 6 miles out and was again extinguished
a few seconds later. [redacted section]
