I. SOURCE: Mrs. [redacted]

Address: [redacted], Dallas, Texas

Age: 23

Occupation: Housewife

Education: 2½ years college

Qualifications: Civil Defense Worker

II. RELIABILITY: SOURCE answered all questions in an informal, conversational manner. She seemed to be very concise and emphatic in answering all questions. SOURCE was quite friendly, congenial and willing to cover every available detail of the sighting. Details of the sighting seemed to be quite vivid in the SOURCE's mind. Reporting officer considers the SOURCE reliable.

III. SOURCE'S DESCRIPTION OF THE SIGHTED:

1. At approximately 2230 CST 20 June 1955, SOURCE was attracted by neighbors talking about an object flying in the sky. SOURCE came outside and observed an object directly over neighbor's home. The object proceeded from the south west traveling in an arc to the north. SOURCE stated the object seemed to be disk in shape and very bright and shiny. The object appeared to be moving at a rate of speed faster than any jet aircraft the source had ever seen. While watching the object, the source stared at it changed from a shiny silver color to a red. SOURCE stated that the object did not travel at the same speed all the time it was in view, but rather slowed down and accelerated frequently. SOURCE sighted the object for a second and third time traveling from the west and disappearing in the north. During the second and third sightings, the altitude of the object appeared to be much higher than it was in the first sighting. SOURCE stated that during the entire period of sighting, no smoke or sound were observed.

2. SOURCE was certain that the total actual time of sighting the object was one minute.

3. Object seemed to be much brighter than the background of the sky and brighter than any star in the sky.

4. Object seemed to round and solid.

5. SOURCE did not observe the object through field glasses at any time.

6. SOURCE stated that she would estimate the real size of the object to be about the size of a 2-35 aircraft.
