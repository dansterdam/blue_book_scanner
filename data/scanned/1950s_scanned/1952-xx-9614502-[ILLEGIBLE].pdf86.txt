Teletype FIFORBT submitted by March Flight Service Center 252L49SCT July

Object was sighted by civilian observer employed by the Sheriff's Office, Norwalk, California. The object was found, very bright, and motionless until 120525Z when it tilted or turned to one side and the color changed to dull orange. As the object tilted apparent propulsion was noted. A puff of smoke 5 or 6 times as large as the object spread and it shot ahead at a high rate of speed. A momentary glint was continued for about 40 seconds thereupon the object disappeared at a very high altitude.
