Statement of [redacted]. High at 9 Squadron, Ellington
Air Force Base, Houston, Texas

On 06 duty when [redacted] came up talking on to open outside
and saw this strange thing. I immediately went out and saw this thing passing over-
head. It was circular in shape and had lights on it (white lights, about the size of
[redacted] light). I had no definite shape, but appeared to be a perfect light structure
across the sky. The color was not blue white as a star, but more yellow light.
It was no small jet but a fairly large object, at least some 200, 300 feet in diameter
[redacted] altitude of over 25,000 feet. I also noticed that it was making a distinct
[redacted] swag. It was the engine would always veer in its general direction - north.
When it disappeared it was going in a northen. I followed until it was completely out of sight, we just before fading
from view I saw no blink on and off.

Remark: I heard no sound from object.
