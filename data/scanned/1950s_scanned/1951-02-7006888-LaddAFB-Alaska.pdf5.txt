FOL report forwarded under provisions of USAF ltr AFOTO-CC-1, dated 5 Aug 1950. At 0930Z, 10 March while performing aircraft check duty at Ladd ARS, Alaska, reported observing what appeared to be unidentified object over Ladd ARS, Alaska. Object appeared oblong in shape, without appurtenances, and had a dull, pearly metallic color. Observer could not see to be at 35,000 feet altitude To see from his location. Object hovered over Ladd AFS for approx 45 seconds, then streaked one gust of white smoke, rose rapidly and disappeared from sight, exhaust or turbo propulsion indicated by smoke, exhaust or turbo. Total time of observation approx 2 min and one half minutes. Observer is 2530 visibility, white and one half miles in light, two year old call, considered observer to 30,000 ft. Three balloons of commercial pattern. Lead was at same was main identification ceiling at 500 feet, visibility one and one half miles in light two year old call, considered observer to 30,000 ft. Three balloons of commercial pattern reported in area at 0530Z of observation. Air traffic in area was one so American C-47 which landed Ladd at 0530Z and One C-47 enroute Ladd to Elmendorf by prior. No other craft or bearing of sighting. Preliminary investigation Ladd AFS and surrounding area indicated no other personnel observed objects. Investigation continuing. Ceremony will follow. Preliminary evaluation U.S.
