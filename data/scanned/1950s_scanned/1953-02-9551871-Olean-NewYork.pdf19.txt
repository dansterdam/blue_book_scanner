There were 8 sightings of unidentified flying objects reported to this
station through Detachment 1, 4673d Ground Observer Squadron, Buffalo Filter
Center, during the period 11 Feb 52 to 1 Mar 53.

SOURCE of 7 reports was GCC Post M308 at Olean, Cattaraugus County,
New York (AMC203). Sightings were made in all but one case by qualified
ground observers.

Source of one report was Corry Pa., GCC post LM25F. Sighting was made
by qualified observer.
