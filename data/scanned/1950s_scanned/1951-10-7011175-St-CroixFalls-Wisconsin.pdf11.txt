THE KALISZEWSKI-MOORE STATEMENTS

A U.P DISPATCH, DATELINE, MINNEAPOLIS, MINN, APRIL 12, 1952.

KALISZEWSKI'S OWN WORDS WERE: "I CAN'T SAY WHETHER THEY WERE SPACE SHIPS, FLYING SAUCERS OR WHAT, BECAUSE I DON'T KNOW. I'VE NEVER SEEN THEM BEFORE. THEY WERE STRANGE, TERRIFYINGLY FAST. THEY HAD A PECULIAR GLOW."

CARRIED THE STATEMENTS OF TWO TOP BALLOON EXPERTS. THE STORY WENT LIKE THIS: C. KALIZEWSKI, SUPERVISOR OF BALLOON MANUFACTURE FOR GENERAL MILLS, CLAIMED TO HAVE SEEN SEVERAL HIGH-SPEED UNIDENTIFIED OBJECTS WHILE MAKING TWO SEPARATE AIRPLANE TRIPS OVER MINNEAPOLIS AND ST. PAUL ON OCT 10 AND 11, 1951.

[Illustration shows a pilot in a plane tracing a balloon flight]

KALISZEWSKI WAS IN A PLANE TRACING A BALLOON FLIGHT WHEN HE SAW THE OBJECTS. HE WENT ON TO STATE:

"I'VE SEEN UNUSUAL THINGS, AND FROM PAST EXPERIENCE I KNOW THAT THIS THING WAS NOT A BALLOON, JET, CONVENTIONAL AIRCRAFT OR CELESTIAL STAR..."

[Illustration labeled "ACTUAL QUOTE - EDS."]

[Illustration shows ground crew at a tracking station]

GROUND CREW WHEN AT THE GENERAL MILLS TRACKING STATION, DOUG SMITH AND RICHARD DURBIN, BOTH CONFIRMED SIGHTING THE OBJECTS (WHILE KALISZEWSKI WAS IN THE AIR) IN A THEODOLITE.*

[Illustration labeled "A TELESCOPE-LIKE MEASURING DEVICE - EDS."]

INCIDENTLY, GENERAL MILLS, WORKING ON ARMED FORCES CONTRACTS, WAS AT THAT TIME ATTEMPTING TO CONSTRUCT A BALLOON CAPABLE OF RISING 60,000 FEET IN THE AIR WITH INSTRUMENTS SENSITIVE TO COSMIC RAY ACTIVITY. MOORE CONCLUDED WITH:

"I MUST CONFESS THERE ISN'T ENOUGH DATA TO SAY MORE THAN THAT THE OBJECTS ARE OF UNDETERMINED ORIGIN."

[Illustration shows two men in discussion]

[Upper right illustration shows a UFO with glowing appearance]

CHARLES N. MOORE, AN AERONAUTICAL ENGINEER IN CHARGE OF BALLOON OPERATIONS FOR GENERAL MILLS, URGED...

"THE OFFICER OUGHT TO CORRESPOND DEPENDING ON CHANCE OBSERVERS AND THEIR TESTIMONY SOMETHING OF THIS NATURE? THESE STRANGE OBJECTS ARE IDENTIFIED. THEY'VE BEEN SEEN AT LEAST 20 TIMES BY PERSONNEL WORKING WITH THE FIRM'S BALLOON OPERATION."

[Illustration shows a man in a window or frame]

BUT I SUSPECT THAT SOME PRETTY INTERESTING ANGLES WOULD TURN UP IF THE GOVERNMENT WOULD ONLY SET UP AN OBSERVATION SIGHT.

[Lower right illustration shows two men discussing]

J.C. KALISZEWSKI AND D. MOORE WERE EXPERTS IN THE FIELD OF BALLOON RESEARCH.

THE AIR FORCE'S BALLOON EXPLANATION WAS NOW A COMPLETE BUST!
