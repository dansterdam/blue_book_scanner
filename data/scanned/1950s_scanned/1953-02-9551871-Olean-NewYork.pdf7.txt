There were 8 sightings of unidentified flying objects reported to this station from Ground Observer Squadron, Mifflin Filter Center, during the period 11 Feb 53 to 1 Mar 53.

SOURCE of 7 reports was CCC Post M1303 at Olean, Cattaraugus County, New York (WDJ205). Sightings were made in all but one case by qualified ground observers.

Source of one report was Corry Pa., GOO post L1255. Sighting was made by qualified observer.
