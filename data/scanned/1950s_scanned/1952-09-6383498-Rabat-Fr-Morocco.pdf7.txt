Casablanca, 24 September 1952
Dr. L/Mo

Dear Sir:

In your letter addressed to the private astronomical observatory of my brother, you raised the question as to whether, within the scope of his observations, he had observed any phenomena like so-called "flying saucers". I take the liberty to answer your question as follows:

My brother died a few years ago and his astronomical observatory ceased to exist. I provide a number of aerial sightings that in a certain respect correspond to observations. However, these observations can, in no account, be considered as fully reliable. I have never observed phenomena similar to the above-mentioned ones, however, as mentioned before, my observations are not fully reliable.

It is correct that almost all reports on "flying saucers" come from the USA. However, some days ago, I found an included newspaper article according to which a "flying saucer" has been observed by many people on the coast of Morocco near Casablanca. I do not know whether you read this article and, therefore, I inclose it for your information.

Very truly yours,
/s/ [redacted]

Enclosure: "On Sunday evening, thousands of people observed a "flying saucer" emitting a metallic green light which was rushing at 11 nights-speed across the skies over Casablanca. It was visible for about an second. Eight minutes later, it was observed over Boulhaut which is 50 kilometers away from Casablanca."
