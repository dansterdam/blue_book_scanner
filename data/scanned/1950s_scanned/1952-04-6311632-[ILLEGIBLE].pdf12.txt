[handwritten: Sault Ste. Marie, Mich.
June 23 1952]

Dear Sir:

The following is an account of unidentified high speed objects observed on the night of April 29 1952, between the hours 2305 to 0225 from a location one mile north of the city limits of Sault Michigan. I observed at least twenty (20) flights of these objects over the above mentioned area. Most of the flights consisted of two three, or four objects in a group, each to the best of any judgment the objects were in sight from about 06 to 10 seconds, and I estimate they covered a mile in 3 seconds or less.

The official ATIC weather at Sault Ste. airport at 2330 over visibility 12 miles, unobstructed ceiling, temperature 69° (I neglected to get the dew pt.) The objects were clearly visible and it was not until about 2300 that a high thin scattered cloud formation started moving from west to east clearing some areas.

On the particular night my craft and it were at a drive-in theatre parked on a heading of approximately 070°. The first indication of these objects there a red glow moving across the windshield from west to north, at an elevation of approximately 40°. At high rate of speed and the brilliance. At the color of this light compared to the yellowing light of an aircraft attracted my attention
