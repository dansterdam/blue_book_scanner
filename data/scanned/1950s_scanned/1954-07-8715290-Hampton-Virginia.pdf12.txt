12. Base Intelligence Officer - Conversation

I have talked to the lady making this report and feel that she is a sincere and trustworthy individual. It is my opinion that what she saw was a possible weather device released at Norfolk Naval Air Station at 1503. This device was carried aloft b a white balloon, 10 ft. in diameter and the winds were from a direction and of a velocity that well might have placed it over the Hampton, Va. area at the time of the reported sighting. Also there were numerous jet and Conventional type aircraft in the Hampton, Va. area at that time. I feel convinced that the above mentioned weather balloon was the object sighted in this instance and feel that no further investigation is necessary.

[handwritten signature]
George W. Satterfield IV
MAJOR T. SATTERFIELD JR.
Major, USAF
Intelligence Officer
