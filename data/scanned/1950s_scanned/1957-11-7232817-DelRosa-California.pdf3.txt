Object Over Wisconsin

Spooner, Wis., Nov. 5 —
Widely separated residents of
this area—including an Episcopal
minister and a newspaper editor—reported sighting
a strange object in the sky at
about sunset yesterday. The
Rev. R. C. Warder, rector of
St. Albans church, said he
was driving 20 miles northeast of Spooner when he saw
a "cigar shaped object" in
the western sky.
William Stewart, editor of
the Spooner Advocate, received a call from a woman
who reported she also saw
something in the westerly sky.
Stewart went outside his office
with a pair of field glasses
and confirmed that he could
see a distant object, but could
not identify it.

Washington, Nov. 3 —A—
The possible future development of motor vehicles with

military armed forces
