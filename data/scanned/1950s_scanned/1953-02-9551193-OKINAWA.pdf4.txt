SUBJECT: Unidentified Flying Object — Detailed Report

1. Unidentified flying object picked up on radar at approximately 27 minutes north [of station], 125 degrees east, then at 45 minutes east [bearing/position]. Two (2) additional plots obtained [total 3 plots per card].

2. F-94 scrambled; climbed to intercept altitude.

3. Visual contact: F-94 crew obtained visual contact with object at 2114Z [local]. Object described as bright orange, changing to red and green intermingled colors.

4. Object pulled away from F-94 at high speed. F-94 dropped to 5,000 feet altitude attempting to regain contact. Object lost.

5. After stern chase of approximately 15 minutes, object disappeared over horizon at [0722001 / approx 0722Z].

6. F-94 crew noted CCAF [Chinese Communist Air Force?] aircraft in area.

7. F-94 broke off chase due to fuel shortage.
