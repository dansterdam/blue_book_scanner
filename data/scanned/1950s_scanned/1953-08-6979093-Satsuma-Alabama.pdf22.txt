CRIFO NEWSLETTER

[handwritten: VISIT FLIGHT REPORTED BREAKS SUMMER LULL]

On the night of August 1st, a fireball, metal signboard appeared over New Haven, Conn. The disc-shaped object traveled horizontally at great speed. The apparition did not appear over the same locality. The object left traces of copper, which ruled out the meteorite explanation [illegible section]
