SUBJECT: Unidentified Flying Object Reportedly Sighted over Omaha, Nebr.,
28 July 1952

TO: Director of Special Investigations
Headquarters USAF
Washington 25, D. C.
ATTN: Chief, Counter Intelligence Div.

1. SYNOPSIS: Three (3) individuals reported the sighting of an
unidentified flying object at approximately 2130-2140 hours, 28 July
1952, at Omaha, Nebraska.

2. DETAILS: Mrs. [redacted], Omaha, Nebr.
(employee of Offutt AFB, Nebr.) reported to this office that on 28 July
1952, at approximately 2130 hours, she sighted a round, disc-like object,
about one foot in diameter, with a yellow-orange glow, traveling in a circular pattern, at varied speeds, in the sky. Mrs. [redacted] advised that
she was able to distinguish the object from a spotlight traversing the sky
in a similar pattern. Inasmuch as the spotlight emitted a white-silver
light, while the unknown object emitted a yellow-orange light. Mrs.
[redacted] added that the object remained in sight for about ten minutes,
at which time it traveled in a southwesterly direction and disappeared.
Mrs. [redacted] said that her husband had also witnessed the object.

Mr. [redacted] furnished substantially the same information as his
wife, except that he added that he believed a dark object had also been
in the area where the glow was observed. Mr. [redacted] noted that the
dark object may have been used to divert attention from the light.

Mr. [redacted], Omaha, Nebr.,
also reported to this office that he had sighted a round, disc-shaped
object, with a yellow glow, travel in a circular pattern in the sky and
disappear in a southwesterly direction at approximately 2140 hours, 28
July 1952. However, Mr. [redacted] later reported that at a later observation he had seen a spotlight in the sky with rays from the ground. Mr.
[redacted] opined that the unidentified object may have been the spotlight,
but that the rays had not been visible at his first sighting.
