An unidentified flying object was sighted at approximately 2055 Item time, 11 August 1954, by Mr. [illegible], of Moeller Shipping Company, Chitosan, and Kosai Nakamoto, his assistant.

The object was sighted from the deck of a salvaged ship anchored off Yoro-Jima at 2°06'10"N/126°41'15"E. The object first appeared north, northeast of Mt. Toromachi, approximately 20 degrees above the horizon, displaying a bright blue light about 200 to 300 feet above the high voltage electric transmission lines. The object remained stationary at that point for approximately 5 minutes in the same blue and agitated there, then turned into an east-northeast direction. Over the vessel it became a bright light and several thousand feet altitude. It then disappeared in a northwesterly direction over the sea with a slight glow above the clouds.
